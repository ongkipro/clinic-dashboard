# UI brief — Clinic Dashboard

<!-- dotuiux brief. Lands at design/brief.md on promotion. Direction: full Apple style, light only (D-14, D-15). -->

## Niche
Multi-tenant SaaS for Indonesian beauty and aesthetic clinics with 1–5 branches: staff operations, clinical record, cashier, stock, commission, WhatsApp CRM, books.

## Audience
Clinic staff on phones and clinic-room iPads first, desktop second (front office, BC, doctor, nurse, therapist, pharmacist, cashier, manager, owner, finance, CS). They must trust that numbers (money, doses, stock, sessions) are exact and that clinical data is private. Patients see the tenant's public site (generated pages) and booking. Our vendor team uses a desktop vendor console. Domain is a beauty/aesthetic clinic, not an online shop: no cart or checkout.

## Primary job
Move each patient visit from arrival to closure without retyping, with money, stock and commission derived from what actually happened.

## Primary action
Per role: Check-in (FO), Kirim ke Dokter (BC), Tanda Tangani (doctor), Selesai Tindakan (room), Serahkan (pharmacy), Terima Pembayaran (cashier), Lihat yang perlu ditangani (owner). Public site: Buat Janji. Public booking: Konfirmasi Jadwal. Vendor console: Tandai Siap Live.

## References wanted
Apple Human Interface Guidelines (rules captured in `DESIGN.md` and `UX-CONCEPT-APPLE-STYLE.md`) and Apple's own apps as patterns: Health (summary and records), Messages (inbox), Reminders (lists and swipe actions), Calendar (schedule), Wallet (amounts). Vendor admin patterns observed in research (Assist/Aesta, ClinicHub) are not copied.

## Non-goals
No dark mode in Release 1; no glass or blur on content; no desktop ERP squeezed onto a phone; no KPI tiles without decisions; no colour-only status; no AI skin scores; no session replay or ad pixels on health pages; no Apple fonts, SF Symbols or Apple trademarks bundled.
