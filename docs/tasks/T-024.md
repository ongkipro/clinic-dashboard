# T-024 — Onboarding grant (PR-58, TEN-13)

Status: Planned · Risk: R4 (vendor access into a tenant, invitations, tenancy) · Depends on: T-023 · Executor: pi (cc/claude-opus-5-5) · Review: claude + codex (R4)

## Requirement

- **PR-58:** an onboarding grant lets onboarding staff configure branches, catalog, staff invitations, imports and branding until go-live or 30 days (renewable once by the owner in Akses Vendor), never clinical rows, and visible to the owner.
- **TEN-13:**
  - The grant is tenant-scoped, limited to configuration objects, never clinical policy kinds.
  - Invitations created under the grant are drafts until the owner confirms each invitee and role set.
  - Owner, Admin Pusat, Finance, Facility head, Records officer and Internal auditor are never assignable under a vendor grant.
  - An account linked to `vendor_staff` can never accept such an invitation.
  - Patient, contact and balance imports run as owner-started jobs; vendor staff see counts and row-error codes only.
  - Every change is written to the clinic audit log.
- **Done when (TASKS.md):**
  - Scenario PR-58 boundary passes: an onboarding specialist with an active grant who opens a patient's medical record is denied, and the attempt is audited.
  - Scenario PR-58 self-invite passes: inviting their own account as Owner is refused and audited.
  - The grant expires at go-live or on day 30.
  - Scenario PR-60 "expired grant" (day 31, not live): the catalog is denied.
- **Visual contract:** not applicable. The console screen comes with T-027 (UX-41); the owner's Akses Vendor view comes with T-028 (UX-48).

## What exists and what does not

- **Exists:** tenant lifecycle (T-022), first Owner (T-023), staff invitations and the `staff_invite_accept` flow (T-031, DEC-031), the vendor console session and `vendor_guard` (T-020/T-021), `memberships_vendor_guard` (IAM-10).
- **Not yet built:** catalog (T-033), imports (PR-85), branding/templates beyond T-014, clinical records (M3+). So this task cannot exercise catalog writes under the grant. Instead it builds the grant and one authority function that every later configuration path must call (as T-022 did with `assertTenantCapability`):
  - `onboarding_grant_allows(tenant, object_kind)`, for object kinds in `branches | catalog | staff_invitations | imports | branding | templates`.
  - Anything else, including every clinical kind, is refused and audited.

## Design

1. **`onboarding_grants`** (platform table, RLS on, no grants):
   - tenant, granted_at, expires_at (= granted_at + 30 days), renewed_at / renewed_by_staff (the clinic Owner, once), ended_at / end_reason (`go_live`, `owner_ended`, `expired`).
   - One grant per tenant.
   - Created automatically when the tenant moves provisioning → onboarding (`tenant_lifecycle_transition`, after-insert trigger on `tenant_lifecycle_events`). Ended when the tenant moves onboarding → live, or leaves onboarding by any edge.
   - **Active** = not ended, `now() < expires_at`, and the tenant is in `onboarding`. A tenant still not live on day 31 has no access: scenario PR-60 expired grant.
2. **`vendor_console_onboarding_access(token, ip, tenant, object_kind)`** (clinic_vendor):
   - Session + `vendor_guard` (roles `onboarding` or `platform_owner`).
   - Then the grant must be active and the kind in the configuration list.
   - The result is logged in the vendor log and, for the tenant, in its audit chain (SEC-16, `vendor_log`). A clinical kind (`patient_record`, `clinical_note`, `photo`, `consent`, `prescription`, ...) or an unknown kind is refused with `not_in_grant`; that is the PR-58 boundary scenario.
   - Later configuration writes call the same check inside their transaction (`onboarding_grant_check`, internal).
3. **Draft invitations** (`vendor_console_onboarding_invite(token, ip, tenant, email, name, roles, branches)`):
   - Requires the step-up and an active grant that covers `staff_invitations`.
   - Roles must be Level 3 only (manager, fo_cashier, bc, doctor, nurse, therapist, pharmacist, central_cs, marketing, purchasing). Owner, admin_pusat, finance, facility_head, records_officer and auditor are refused (`role_not_assignable`), refused and logged.
   - Refused for an email that belongs to a vendor staff account (any state): this is the self-invite scenario. Inviting their own account as Owner fails on both rules, and both are audited.
   - Stored in `staff_invitations` (T-031 table) as state **`draft`**, with `invited_by` NULL and `drafted_by_vendor` = the vendor staff id. A draft has no token yet.
   - The `staff_invitations_no_owner` trigger from HYG-002 still applies.
4. **Owner confirms** (`staff_invite_confirm_draft(invitation, token_hash)`, clinic_app, owner only, under the `staff_access:` lock):
   - The owner sees invitee and role set (UX-48 later; module function now).
   - Confirming turns the draft into a normal pending invitation from the owner (`invited_by` = owner, 7 days) and returns the one-time link to the owner, who sends it (DEC-031 D1).
   - Declining cancels it. Both are audited.
   - `staff_invite_accept` already refuses vendor-staff accounts through `memberships_vendor_guard`; a test asserts it for an invitation that started as a draft.
5. **Renewal once** (`onboarding_grant_renew()`, clinic_app, owner only): adds 30 days from now, only while active and not renewed before; audited.
6. **Owner view data:** `onboarding_grant_status()` (clinic_app, owner) returns state, expiry, renewed flag and drafts awaiting confirmation. The page is T-028.
7. **Imports:** the owner-started import job comes with PR-85. This task only reserves the rule: `imports` in the grant means the vendor may *prepare* (upload, dry-run) and sees counts and row-error codes only. Recorded for PR-85; nothing to build here.

## Acceptance (runnable)

`tests/vendor/onboarding.test.ts`:
- **Lifecycle:** the grant is created on provisioning → onboarding and ended on go-live. A tenant not live by day 31 is denied (catalog), and the denial is audited.
- **PR-58 boundary:** with an active grant, a request for `patient_record` (and each clinical kind) is denied, logged in the vendor log and audited in the tenant chain. The configuration kinds are allowed. Vendor roles other than onboarding and platform owner are refused, as are a stale step-up for writes and an IP outside the allowlist.
- **PR-58 self-invite:** inviting one's own vendor account as Owner is refused and audited. Every Level 2 role is refused, and a vendor-staff email is refused even for a Level 3 role.
- **Drafts:** a draft has no token and cannot be accepted. Only the owner confirms or declines; Admin Pusat and other roles are refused. A confirmed draft becomes a normal invitation from the owner and is accepted by the invitee. A vendor account cannot accept it.
- **Renewal:** renewed once by the owner only, refused the second time.
- **Isolation:** another tenant's grant or draft cannot be read, confirmed or used. `clinic_app` and `clinic_vendor` read no table.
