## Good

- The domain guard (P-13) is explicit in the PRD §3 and the UX-51 contract: no cart, checkout or shipping; clinic vocabulary only.
- The three levels are coherent between 17:22 and 05 §2.1:
  - The vendor never joins tenant rows except through security-definer functions and owner-visible support sessions.
  - Vendor staff are never clinic memberships (DATA-55).
  - Patients belong to one brand and are shared across its branches (DATA-56).
- The happy-path hand-off has a signal and a blocker per state. Exceptions all route through one Persetujuan inbox with requester ≠ approver.
- The research gaps that most matter for a 1–5 branch beauty clinic are now Must, each with a scenario: reminders, aftercare as a care message, walk-in and queue number, discount limits, STR/SIP alerts, letters, supplier returns, global search.
- Public site safety:
  - Generated from master data, no page builder.
  - Doctors listed only with consent (PR-66).
  - Clinical text published only after clinical-lead review, no superlatives (PR-65).
  - Price hidden unless published.
  - Suspended or unpublished tenants show no tenant data (PR-67).
  - Testimonials and before/after deferred to Phase 4.
- The state and permission matrix covers publishing, vendor access, server-fee changes and discounts.
- Release 1.1 items (PR-77–PR-82) are kept as Should, with scenarios.

2. Does each branch have its own Penanggung Jawab Klinik and its own records officer, or is there one for the whole brand? (R3C-9)

7. Does Numa run promos with price, quota and period that must appear on a website at go-live? Does it already have a website or domain the generated site would replace? (R3C-4, public-site scope)
