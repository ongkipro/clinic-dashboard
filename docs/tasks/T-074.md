## Sources

- **PR-15 (02:156).** An additional treatment starts only after the patient and an authorized clinician have agreed, and the agreement is recorded.
  - FO/cashier may release it before payment. The release records the staff member accountable for collecting. There is no amount limit and no manager approval.
  - FO is alerted when the addition is approved, and again if it finishes unpaid.
  - A visit cannot close with an unpaid bill or pending dispensing.
  - After the WhatsApp-bill fallback, FO may convert the remainder to a patient receivable, which settles the visit bill.

## Design (proposed)

### 1. The addition's payment record (table `addition_releases`; module `src/modules/cashier/additions/`)

- **Columns:** `addition_releases (tenant, branch, visit, plan_item (the approved addition), request (clinical_request), released_by, released_as, collector_staff_id, released_at, command_key)`. One per plan item. Insert-only.
- **Writer `addition_release(key, plan_item, {collector_staff_id})`** for FO/cashier and manager at the visit's branch. Requirements:
  - the plan item comes from an approved addition request of an open visit;
  - the patient agreement for it is recorded (`consent_state` active for the plan);
  - the collector is a staff member with an FO/cashier or manager role at the branch.

  It audits `addition_released` and emits `AdditionReleased {release_id, visit_id, plan_item_id}`.
- **Release scope.** A release lets the addition's execution start before payment. Nothing else is released: initial items stay subject to BILL-1 and the visit's payment step.

### 2. Start before payment (re-created `treatment_start`)

`payment_unpaid` no longer blocks the start when every item being started is a released addition. Concretely: the item has a release row, and the visit's other unpaid amount does not belong to non-addition items (open point 1). Otherwise the T-064 rule stands.

**Open point 1:** the simplest correct reading of B-01/BILL-3. The payment check skips only the released addition's own start. A visit with an unpaid *initial* bill still cannot start anything, including a released addition, because initial items are paid before they start (B-01). Proposed: the addition starts if it is released, whatever its own invoice state, and the initial bill must be paid (`kasir_visit_payment` on the non-addition invoice).

### 3. The addition's bill (a second invoice on the visit)

- **Today.** `invoice_issue` (T-072) refuses a second issued invoice per visit.
- **Change.** An *addition invoice* is allowed: a visit invoice whose lines are all approved additions of this visit (`plan_items.request_id IS NOT NULL`), each line naming its plan item.
- **Implementation.** Add `invoice_lines.plan_item_id` (nullable, FK). `invoice_issue` (re-created) accepts `plan_item_id` on lines. The one-issued-invoice rule then applies to non-addition invoices only.
- **Visit payment step.** `kasir_visit_payment` is unchanged: any issued, unpaid invoice of the visit keeps the step `in_progress`. That is what blocks closure (PR-15 "cannot close unpaid") and lists the visit as outstanding (PR-7).
- **Scope.** This touches T-072's `invoice_issue`, `invoice_lines` and the Kasir module: `src/modules/cashier/schema.ts` is allowed, `src/modules/cashier/kasir/kasir.ts` is a scope expansion for the line type.

### 4. Alerts (PR-15, PR-54)

- **First alert.** Exists already (T-056): FO `clinical_request_approved` on approval.
- **Second alert.** New notification type `addition_unpaid_finished` (FO/cashier and manager of the branch).
  - **When:** raised when the execution of an addition plan item finishes with a counted outcome while that plan item has no fully paid invoice line.
  - **How:** an AFTER trigger on `treatment_executions` (state → finished), so `treatment_finish` is not re-created (open point 2).
  - **Detail:** the subject is the plan item (or the release). One alert per execution. Unpaid means no issued invoice line for the plan item with `kasir_line_paid = line_total`.

### 5. The close guard and the outstanding list

- **`visit_close`.** Already refuses `unpaid_bill` (any issued invoice owing) and `pending_dispensing` (CLIN-025). T-074 adds `unbilled_addition`: a released or approved addition of this visit that was executed (counted) but has no issued invoice line. Without this, an addition performed but never billed would let the visit close (CLIN-020's "+5 add-on, closure blocked"). `visit_close` is re-created, and `CloseBlocker` and its label are extended (scope expansion: `src/modules/visits/visits.ts`).
- **The outstanding list (PR-7).** Already lists visits whose payment step is `in_progress` or `blocked` (T-047 `unpaid`). An unbilled executed addition makes the payment step `in_progress` too. `kasir_visit_payment` (re-created) treats an unbilled counted addition as owing, so the board lists it and closure is blocked.

### 6. Receivable fallback

Not in this task: T-078 owns receivables (DATA-32) and their PR-15 conversion, and the WhatsApp bill is outbound. T-074 leaves the bill unpaid, so the visit stays blocked. A test documents that closure stays refused until paid; conversion comes with T-078.

2. The second alert comes from a trigger on executions (no re-creation of `treatment_finish`).
3. The addition's bill is a second invoice with `plan_item_id` lines. Alternative: append lines to the issued invoice (refused: an issued invoice is frozen, T-071).

## Plan review r1 (Argon `cb5f62d0`, REVISE): every finding checked against the code and adopted

Open points 2, 3 and 5 accepted as proposed; 1 and 4 accepted with the corrections below.

- **P1-1. The payment rule at start (`treatment_payment_unpaid(v, item)`).** Used by both `treatment_start` and `treatment_item_blockers`, both re-created.
  - **(a) The initial bill is unpaid.** This blocks every item, released additions included. It holds when any of these is true:
    - `v.payment = 'blocked'`;
    - an issued non-addition invoice of the visit owes;
    - the selection is done and the visit has no issued non-addition invoice;
    - `v.payment = 'in_progress'` with no issued invoice and no counted addition (the T-064 test fixture that sets the column directly).

    This is BILL-1's tightening, which DEC-083 (5) left to this task: a visit whose selection is confirmed but not billed cannot start.
  - **(b) An addition item is unpaid and unreleased.** This blocks that item only. An approved addition of this visit starts only when it is released, or when its own addition line is fully paid in real money.
  - **(c) Initial items unaffected.** An unpaid addition never blocks the visit's paid initial items.
- **P1-2. An unbilled counted addition.**
  - **Trigger.** An `AFTER UPDATE OF state` trigger on `treatment_executions` (`ENABLE ALWAYS`) fires when a counted addition finishes. It raises `addition_unpaid_finished` when the addition is not fully paid, and calls `kasir_visit_payment`.
  - **Payment step.** `kasir_visit_payment` (re-created) checks `in_progress` first: an issued invoice that owes, or an unbilled counted addition (`addition_unbilled_counted`). Only then does it fall back to `not_started` or `done`.
  - **Close order.** `visit_close` (re-created) checks `unbilled_addition` before `unpaid_bill`.
  - **TypeScript.** `CloseBlocker` gains `unbilled_addition` ("Tindakan tambahan belum ditagih").
- **P1-3. Addition invoice lines.**
  - **Column.** `invoice_lines.plan_item_id` with a composite foreign key, unique per invoice. `invoice_lines_guard` (re-created) freezes it and allows it only on a visit invoice that is not a counter sale.
  - **`invoice_issue` (re-created) rules:**
    - an invoice's lines are all addition lines or all ordinary lines (`mixed_addition_lines`);
    - `visit_already_invoiced` counts only ordinary invoices;
    - each addition line must be an approved addition of this visit, not removed, with the same catalog item and quantity as the plan item;
    - an addition is billed on one issued invoice only (`addition_already_invoiced`).
  - **Readers.** `invoice_line_snapshot` writes the plan item, and `kasir_invoice_get` shows it.
- **P1-4. `addition_releases` and the writer.**
  - **Columns.** It also records the authorizing clinician (`authorized_by`) and the patient agreement (`consent_id`, the active consent at release), so cashier readers see the BILL-22 record without reading clinical tables.
  - **Writer `addition_release`:**
    - takes the actor lock; the actor is FO/cashier or a manager at their active branch, and the tenant has the cashier capability;
    - locks in order: visit (open, the actor's branch), plan (active), plan item and request (an approved addition of this visit);
    - requires the consent to be active, and the collector to hold FO/cashier or manager at the branch with an active membership now;
    - an already released item answers `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: addition_already_released`, checked before the insert;
    - events name the tenant, branch, staff and role.
  - **Table gates:** RLS (`kasir_readable`), operator policy, scope map, support class `personal`, no-truncate, and an insert-only guard.
  - **Reader.** A commercial-only reader `addition_visit_list(visit)` returns the visit's additions with release, invoice line, paid and counted. It carries no reason, dose or condition, and masks the patient for the auditor.
- **P2-5. Scope expansions, declared up front:**
  - `src/modules/visits/visits.ts`;
  - `src/modules/cashier/kasir/kasir.ts` (the line's `planItemId`);
  - `tests/db/additions-drift.test.ts` and its `.expected.json`, declaring every re-created function;
  - `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`;
  - any test outside the allowed paths that the BILL-1 tightening changes (to be listed at close).
- **P2-6. Added tests:**
  - non-counted stops: no alert, no blocker; a patient stop counts;
  - declined, pending, plan-change and substitution items refused by both writers;
  - the invoice-line refusals; a void of an unpaid addition invoice brings `unbilled_addition` back;
  - a simulated or partial payment leaves the addition unpaid (alert, outstanding, `unpaid_bill`);
  - no clinical text in alerts, events or the reader; the auditor masked.
