## Design (proposed)

### 1. Tables (migration 0087; module `src/modules/cashier/`)

- **`payments`** (tenant-ledger, personal; append-only): tenant, branch, invoice, patient (nullable for an anonymous counter sale), method (`cash`, `edc`, `transfer`, `qris`, `va`, `payment_link`, `balance`), amount (1..1e12), `cash_tendered` and `change_given` (cash only, tendered ≥ amount), `details` jsonb (EDC: card type, bank, trace, approval code; transfer: bank, reference; bounded keys), provider, provider_account, environment, `is_simulated` (false in this task), `command_key`, `receipt_id`, received_by/as/at.
- **`payment_allocations`** (tenant-ledger; append-only): payment, `invoice_line_id` (the only target in this task; the other four target columns come with their tasks, CHECK exactly one), amount (> 0), `vat_portion` (the VAT newly recognised by this allocation), copies of method / provider / environment / `is_simulated`.
- **`receipts`** (tenant-ledger, personal; immutable): branch, number `KW-<BRANCHCODE>-YYYYMM-NNNN` (branch-local month, unique per tenant), invoice, the payments of one "Terima", the rendered snapshot (`body` text from the tenant's active receipt template via `template_fill`, or a built-in layout), totals, issued_by/at. Number serialised by the staff_access lock (as `po_next_number`).
- **`invoices` additions:** `number` set at issue (`INV-<BRANCHCODE>-YYYYMM-NNNN`; the T-071 guard already freezes it once set), `void_approval_id`, `voided_at`; state `void` (T-071 enum). New table `invoice_void_requests` is not needed: the approval row is the request (subject `invoice`).
- **Dispensings for counter sales** (scope expansion, see §5): `dispensings.invoice_line_id` (nullable), `visit_id` and `patient_id` nullable with CHECK (`visit_id` or `invoice_line_id`).

### 2. Allocation (BILL-2, PR-19)

- A payment of amount P on an invoice with outstanding lines o_i (line_total − allocated so far, > 0): a_i = floor(P × o_i / Σo), then the residual rupiah one each to the largest remainders (ties by line creation order). Σa_i = P, 0 ≤ a_i ≤ o_i always.
- **Weights are the outstanding after discount, VAT-inclusive.** BILL-2 says "excluding tax". On an invoice whose lines share one tax state (all PKP-taxable, or all non-VAT) the ex-tax and VAT-inclusive proportions are identical. On a mixed invoice (taxable + exempt lines of a PKP tenant) ex-tax weights can push a line above its outstanding (e.g. 111 taxable + 100 exempt, full payment of 211 split 50/50 gives the exempt line 105.5). **Open point 1:** use VAT-inclusive outstanding weights (always within each line's outstanding; equal to the ex-tax rule on single-rate invoices), recorded as PENDING for the accountant (the posting map sign-off, DEC-080). The commission basis (ex-tax, BILL-15) is derived per allocation later from `amount − vat_portion`, so M5 is unaffected.
- `P` may not exceed the invoice outstanding (`ERR_INVALID_INPUT: overpayment`); cash change is the tendered surplus, not an overpayment.
- **VAT at payment (tax point, A-12).** For a PKP line, target_i = round-half-up(vat_i × allocated_i_after / line_total_i); `vat_portion` = max(0, target_i − vat_recognised_amount_i); the line's `vat_recognised_amount` grows to target_i and reaches vat_i exactly when the line is fully paid. A line already recognised at delivery (later tasks) gets no second VAT (the guard keeps `max`).

### 3. Writers (SECURITY DEFINER; actor lock first, then role, capability, branch; idempotency by key; events ids only)

Actor: `invoice_actor` (T-071; owner, manager, fo_cashier, finance; cashier capability; branch roles at their active branch). Payments: manager and fo_cashier at their active branch, owner and finance at a named branch (07 ◐).

- **`invoice_open`** (re-created): adds `counter: true` (no visit; patient optional). A visit invoice requires an open visit at the branch.
- **`kasir_issue`** (wraps `invoice_issue`; T-071's signature kept): issues with lines, sets the invoice number, and for a visit invoice sets the visit `payment` step (§4). A counter invoice accepts products only (services need a visit; `ERR_INVALID_INPUT: counter_products_only`); each product must have an active stock item (as `selection_confirm`) and becomes one queued counter dispensing (§5).
- **`payment_receive(key, invoice, {tenders: [{method, amount, cash_tendered?, details?}] 1–6})`**: one "Terima". Methods allowed now: `cash`, `edc`, `transfer`, each enabled in SET-14 at the payment instant (`setting_value_at`, snapshot). `qris`, `va`, `payment_link` → `ERR_NOT_AVAILABLE: payment_port` (T-073); `balance` → `ERR_NOT_AVAILABLE: balance` (T-075). At most one cash tender. Locks the invoice, then its lines in id order; refuses a non-issued invoice, an anonymous counter sale needing nothing special, and Σamount > outstanding. Writes payments, allocations per §2, VAT recognition, one receipt; updates the visit step and the counter dispensings' blocker (§4, §5). Events `PaymentReceived {payment_id}` per payment, `PaymentAllocated {allocation_id}` per allocation. Audit `payment_received`.
- **`invoice_void_request(key, invoice, reason)`**: FO/cashier, manager, finance; reason 3–500. Allowed only while nothing is allocated to the invoice and no counter dispensing is handed (**open point 2:** a paid invoice is corrected by refund, T-078 — "Sudah ada pembayaran — gunakan refund"). Requests approval type `invoice_void` (requesters fo_cashier, manager, finance; approvers owner, manager; requester ≠ approver by `approval_decide`). One pending request per invoice.
- **`invoice_void_sync`** (AFTER UPDATE OF state ON approvals, as `stock_count_sync`): approved → invoice `void`, `voided_at`, queued/prepared counter dispensings cancelled with reason "Invoice dibatalkan", visit step recomputed, event `InvoiceVoided {invoice_id}`; rejected/cancelled → nothing. Re-checks the void preconditions under lock (a payment arriving after the request blocks the approval: `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: invoice_paid`).
- **Receipts:** `receipt_get(receipt)` returns the frozen body and fields; the PDF is rendered in TypeScript with `renderPdf`. "Kirim WhatsApp link" is shown blocked with "WhatsApp belum terhubung. Owner dapat menghubungkan di Pengaturan." until the messaging tasks (M6).

### 4. Visit payment step (T-047's open item)

`kasir_visit_payment(tenant, visit)`: `in_progress` while any issued, not void invoice of the visit has an outstanding amount; `done` when every such invoice is fully paid and at least one exists; unchanged (`not_started`) when none exists. Called by `kasir_issue`, `payment_receive` and the void sync. `treatment_start` and dispensing already block on `in_progress` (DEC-074, 0082). **Open point 3:** BILL-1 ("initial selected treatment is paid before it starts") would also block `not_started` with a selection; that tightening re-creates `treatment_start` and belongs with the close guard in T-074 — this task does not change it.

### 5. Counter sale (Jual Cepat) and the pharmacist hand-over (scope expansion)

- There is no regulated-item classification in the catalog or stock items, and stock moves only through the pharmacist's dispense (DEC-075). Proposed: **every counter-sale product goes through the Serahkan list** (stricter than "regulated items", needs no new classification or setting); a product without an active stock item is refused, as in `selection_confirm`.
- `dispensings` gains `invoice_line_id`; `visit_id`, `patient_id` become nullable (CHECK: a visit dispensing has visit and patient; a counter dispensing has the invoice line). `dispensing_blocker` reads, for a counter dispensing, the invoice's outstanding instead of the visit step (`payment_unpaid`). `dispensing_locked`, `pharmacy_patient_json`, `pharmacy_handover_list`, `pharmacy_prep_list`, `dispensings_guard`, `pharmacy_visit_steps` callers handle a null visit ("Penjualan langsung"; the patient when named).
- Scope expansions (recorded with `expand-scope`): `src/modules/pharmacy/schema.ts` (the columns), `src/app/(app)/apotek/**` only where it prints `patient.mrn` (null for an anonymous sale), `tests/pharmacy/pharmacy.test.ts` only if a re-created function changes a tested output. **Open point 4:** the alternative is to defer the counter-sale hand-over to a later task; that leaves PR-19's counter sale incomplete, so it is not proposed.

### 6. Readers

- `kasir_queue()` (manager, fo_cashier at the active branch; owner, finance, auditor read a named branch): open visits whose selection is done and that have no issued invoice ("Siap ditagih"), issued invoices with an outstanding amount ("Tagihan Terbuka"), pending void requests. No clinical content (item names are commercial; no diagnosis, notes or plan text).
- `kasir_invoice_get(invoice)`: header, lines (frozen price, tax badge), allocations per line, payments, receipts, outstanding, the tenders offered now (SET-14 minus port and balance, each with its blocked reason), void state.
- `kasir_visit_lines(visit)`: the suggested lines from the confirmed selection (catalog item and integer quantity; a non-integer quantity is shown for the cashier to edit).
- `kasir_counter_items(query)`: sellable products with an active stock item and their branch price now.
- `receipt_get(receipt)`.

### 7. UI (`src/app/(app)/kasir/**`)

- `/kasir`: segments Siap Ditagih · Tagihan Terbuka; row → `/kasir/[invoiceId]` (or "Buat Tagihan" for a visit: lines prefilled from the selection, quantities editable, items removable, no discount field — manual discounts and their limits are T-080, so none are offered here).
- `/kasir/[invoiceId]`: lines with frozen price and tax badge ("PPN" / "Bebas PPN" / "Bukan objek"), totals (subtotal, PPN included, total, paid, remaining in Title 1); tender sheet with chips (Tunai, EDC, Transfer active; QRIS and Link blocked "Belum tersedia — menunggu port pembayaran"; Saldo blocked "Saldo pasien belum aktif"), keypad amount, cash tendered and change, EDC and transfer details; prominent "Terima Rp…". Success: receipt number with Cetak (PDF) and Kirim WhatsApp link (blocked). Secondary: "Ajukan Void" with reason (sheet), shown only when nothing is paid.
- `/kasir/jual-cepat`: optional patient search, product search with branch price, quantity, then the same tender sheet; after payment the receipt and "Diserahkan apoteker" note.
- `/kasir/terbuka`: open invoices (Belum dibayar, Sebagian).
- Stale-context protection on every action (branch/role change), refill after remount, `canSee` gating as in earlier pages.
