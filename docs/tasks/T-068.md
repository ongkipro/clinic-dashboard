## Design

Migration 0085 `safety`:

1. `device_schedules`: one per branch device and kind (`calibration`, `maintenance`). It holds `interval_days` (1–1825) and `next_due_on`. Set by the manager. An entry of that kind moves `next_due_on` to entry date + interval.
2. `logbook_entries` (insert-only, never deleted; a correction is a new entry with `corrects`). Kinds:
   - `sterilization`: cycle number, method (`autoclave`, `chemical`, `uv`, `other`), the items sterilized, result `pass` or `fail`, optional device or room.
   - `calibration` and `maintenance`: device (required), result `ok`, `adjusted` or `out_of_service`, vendor or technician name, optional certificate number.
   - `b3_waste`: waste date, category (`infectious`, `sharps`, `pharmaceutical`, `chemical`, `other`), quantity in grams as an integer, authorized handler name, handover document number. This is a register link only, not a logistics module.
   
   Every entry has `performed_on` (not in the future), the officer (`staff_id`) and the role.
3. `clinical_incidents`:
   - classification `ktd`, `knc`, `ktc`, `kpc` or `sentinel`;
   - occurred_at, an optional patient (KPC has none), an optional visit;
   - summary, immediate response, reporter, responsible officer (facility head default);
   - state `reported` → `investigating` → `closed`;
   - investigation notes and root cause;
   - reporting determination: required, recipient and deadline, plus submission evidence (submitted_at and reference);
   - corrective action and closure.
   
   Closing needs a finished investigation, a corrective action, and either "no report required" or a submission. Otherwise it is refused with "Investigasi belum selesai" or "Pelaporan belum dikirim". Events are insert-only in `clinical_incident_events`.
4. Roles:
   - Logbook writers: nurse, therapist, doctor and manager at their branch (sterilization and waste). Calibration and maintenance entries are also written by these roles. Schedules are set by the manager.
   - Logbook readers: those roles plus the facility head, owner, admin_pusat and auditor.
   - Incidents: any clinical role (doctor, nurse, therapist, pharmacist, bc) and the manager can report. The facility head handles them (investigate, determine, submit, close). Reporters read their own reports. The facility head, owner and auditor read all of them.
   - Content is clinical: no clinical text in events, logs or notifications (ids only).
5. `logbook_due_scan(p_today)`, called on an unbound worker connection like `credential_alert_scan`: for every active device schedule with `next_due_on < today` and no notification for that due date yet, it raises `logbook_calibration_due` (or `logbook_maintenance_due`) for role `manager` at the branch, once per schedule and due date (dedupe table `logbook_due_alerts`). The reader `logbook_overview` also returns the overdue devices, so the page shows "Alat lewat jatuh tempo kalibrasi" without waiting for the job.
6. UI:
   - `/logbook` has segments Sterilisasi · Kalibrasi & Perawatan · Limbah B3. Overdue devices are shown first. There is a Catat sheet per kind, and the manager sets schedules on the device rows.
   - `/keluhan/insiden` holds the list and detail with Catat Insiden and Tutup (UX-28 ▸ Insiden).
   - The Insiden segment of `/keluhan` links to it. This needs expansions on `src/app/(app)/keluhan/page.tsx` and a new `src/app/(app)/keluhan/insiden/**`, both required by UX-28, which names Insiden as a Keluhan segment.
