# T-024 — Onboarding grant (PR-58, TEN-13)

Status: Done — codex APPROVE (round 7), claude APPROVE (round 2, through 9Router) · Risk: R4 (vendor access into a tenant, invitations, tenancy) · Depends on: T-023 · Executor: pi (cc/claude-opus-5-5) · Review: claude + codex (R4)

## Requirement

- **PR-58:** an onboarding grant lets onboarding staff configure branches, catalog, staff invitations, imports and branding until go-live or 30 days (renewable once by the owner in Akses Vendor), never clinical rows, and visible to the owner.
- **TEN-13:**
  - The grant is tenant-scoped, limited to configuration objects, never clinical policy kinds.
  - Invitations created under the grant are drafts until the owner confirms each invitee and role set.
  - Owner, Admin Pusat, Finance, Facility head, Records officer and Internal auditor are never assignable under a vendor grant.
  - An account linked to `vendor_staff` can never accept such an invitation.
  - Patient, contact and balance imports run as owner-started jobs; vendor staff see counts and row-error codes only.
  - Every change is written to the clinic audit log.
- **Done when (TASKS.md):**
  - Scenario PR-58 boundary passes: an onboarding specialist with an active grant who opens a patient's medical record is denied, and the attempt is audited.
  - Scenario PR-58 self-invite passes: inviting their own account as Owner is refused and audited.
  - The grant expires at go-live or on day 30.
  - Scenario PR-60 "expired grant" (day 31, not live): the catalog is denied.
- **Visual contract:** not applicable. The console screen comes with T-027 (UX-41); the owner's Akses Vendor view comes with T-028 (UX-48).

## What exists and what does not

- **Exists:** tenant lifecycle (T-022), first Owner (T-023), staff invitations and the `staff_invite_accept` flow (T-031, DEC-031), the vendor console session and `vendor_guard` (T-020/T-021), `memberships_vendor_guard` (IAM-10).
- **Not yet built:** catalog (T-033), imports (PR-85), branding/templates beyond T-014, clinical records (M3+). So this task cannot exercise catalog writes under the grant. Instead it builds the grant and one authority function that every later configuration path must call (as T-022 did with `assertTenantCapability`):
  - `onboarding_grant_allows(tenant, object_kind)`, for object kinds in `branches | catalog | staff_invitations | imports | branding | templates`.
  - Anything else, including every clinical kind, is refused and audited.

## Design

1. **`onboarding_grants`** (platform table, RLS on, no grants):
   - tenant, granted_at, expires_at (= granted_at + 30 days), renewed_at / renewed_by_staff (the clinic Owner, once), ended_at / end_reason (`go_live`, `left_onboarding`). Expiry is judged by time; it is not an end reason.
   - One grant per tenant.
   - Created automatically when the tenant moves provisioning → onboarding (`tenant_lifecycle_transition`, after-insert trigger on `tenant_lifecycle_events`). Ended when the tenant moves onboarding → live, or leaves onboarding by any edge.
   - **Active** = not ended, `now() < expires_at`, and the tenant is in `onboarding`. A tenant still not live on day 31 has no access: scenario PR-60 expired grant.
2. **`vendor_console_onboarding_access(token, ip, tenant, object_kind)`** (clinic_vendor):
   - Session + `vendor_guard` (roles `onboarding` or `platform_owner`).
   - Then the grant must be active and the kind in the configuration list.
   - The result is logged in the vendor log and, for the tenant, in its audit chain (SEC-16, `vendor_log`). A clinical kind (`patient_record`, `clinical_note`, `photo`, `consent`, `prescription`, ...) or an unknown kind is refused with `not_in_grant`; that is the PR-58 boundary scenario.
   - Later configuration writes call the same check inside their transaction (`onboarding_grant_check`, internal).
3. **Draft invitations** (`vendor_console_onboarding_invite(token, ip, tenant, email, name, roles, branches)`):
   - Requires the step-up and an active grant that covers `staff_invitations`.
   - Roles must be Level 3 only (manager, fo_cashier, bc, doctor, nurse, therapist, pharmacist, central_cs, marketing, purchasing). Owner, admin_pusat, finance, facility_head, records_officer and auditor are refused (`role_not_assignable`), refused and logged.
   - Refused for an email that belongs to a vendor staff account (any state): this is the self-invite scenario. Inviting their own account as Owner fails on both rules, and both are audited.
   - Branches must belong to that tenant.
   - Stored in a separate tenant-scoped table **`onboarding_invitation_drafts`** (state draft → confirmed | declined; drafted_by vendor staff; no token). `staff_invitations` is not changed, so every T-031/HYG-002 rule on real invitations stays as built.
4. **Owner confirms** (`onboarding_draft_confirm(draft, token_hash)` and `onboarding_draft_decline(draft)`, clinic_app, owner only, under the `staff_access:` lock):
   - Confirming creates a **normal invitation from the owner** through `staff_invite`, so the same validation, lock, Owner-invitation trigger, inviter-authority check at accept, and audit apply. The draft is marked confirmed with the invitation id. The one-time link goes back to the owner, who sends it (DEC-031 D1).
   - A vendor account still cannot accept: `memberships_vendor_guard` refuses it at accept time (tested).
   - Declining is audited.
5. **Renewal once** (`onboarding_grant_renew()`, clinic_app, owner only): adds 30 days from now, only while active and not renewed before; audited.
6. **Owner view data:** `onboarding_grant_status()` (clinic_app, owner) returns state, expiry, renewed flag and drafts awaiting confirmation. The page is T-028.
7. **Imports:** the owner-started import job comes with PR-85. This task only reserves the rule: `imports` in the grant means the vendor may *prepare* (upload, dry-run) and sees counts and row-error codes only. Recorded for PR-85; nothing to build here.

## Acceptance (runnable)

`tests/vendor/onboarding.test.ts`:
- **Lifecycle:** the grant is created on provisioning → onboarding and ended on go-live. A tenant not live by day 31 is denied (catalog), and the denial is audited.
- **PR-58 boundary:** with an active grant, a request for `patient_record` (and each clinical kind) is denied, logged in the vendor log and audited in the tenant chain. The configuration kinds are allowed. Vendor roles other than onboarding and platform owner are refused, as are a stale step-up for writes and an IP outside the allowlist.
- **PR-58 self-invite:** inviting one's own vendor account as Owner is refused and audited. Every Level 2 role is refused, and a vendor-staff email is refused even for a Level 3 role.
- **Drafts:** a draft has no token and cannot be accepted. Only the owner confirms or declines; Admin Pusat and other roles are refused. A confirmed draft becomes a normal invitation from the owner and is accepted by the invitee. A vendor account cannot accept it.
- **Renewal:** renewed once by the owner only, refused the second time.
- **Isolation:** another tenant's grant or draft cannot be read, confirmed or used. `clinic_app` and `clinic_vendor` read no table.

Also: mutation-test every control; full suite, typecheck, lint, docs:check; review by claude and codex until both APPROVE.

## As built

- **Migrations:** `0043` adds the `onboarding_grants` and `onboarding_invitation_drafts` tables (both platform tables); `0044` adds the rules.
- **Grant lifecycle:** an after-insert trigger on `tenant_lifecycle_events` creates the grant (30 days) on entering onboarding and ends it on leaving (`go_live` / `left_onboarding`). `onboarding_grant_active` = not ended, not expired, tenant in onboarding.
- **`onboarding_grant_check(token, ip, tenant, kind, write)`:** the single authority.
  - Session, then `vendor_guard` (onboarding or platform owner; 10-minute step-up for writes, 12 h for reads), then tenant exists, kind in the grant, grant active.
  - Every outcome goes to `vendor_log`, so also to the tenant's audit chain. A refused kind is recorded as `not_in_grant_<kind>`.
  - `vendor_console_onboarding_access` exposes it to the console; later configuration writes call it.
- **Drafts:** `vendor_console_onboarding_invite` refuses, each refusal logged:
  - Level 2 roles (`role_not_assignable`);
  - vendor-account emails, in any state (`vendor_account`);
  - foreign or missing branches, and invalid input;
  - existing members;
  - an open draft for the same email.

  It runs under the tenant's `staff_access:` lock.
- **Owner functions (clinic_app, bound to `app_tenant_id`, `staff_access` lock, Owner only):**
  - `onboarding_grant_status`.
  - `onboarding_draft_confirm`: re-checks the vendor-account rule, then calls `staff_invite`, so every T-031/HYG-002 invitation rule applies; the Owner gets the one-time token.
  - `onboarding_draft_decline` and `onboarding_grant_renew` (once, while active).
- **History guards:** grants only end or renew once; drafts are decided once; no delete or truncate.

## Evidence

- `tests/vendor/onboarding.test.ts`: 10 tests, covering both PR-58 scenarios, PR-60 expired grant (day 31 denied, day 29 open), go-live end, renewal, drafts, isolation, privileges and history.
- 27 mutants, all killed in the final run (`/data/tmp/t024-mut.py`). The renewal's active check after `FOR UPDATE` and the repeated condition in its UPDATE are deliberate defence in depth: removing either alone is equivalent, removing both is caught ("renew expired (both)"). The draft history test was strengthened after the first run: constraint-valid rewrites now expect 42501.
- Full suite 707 passing.
- Wave CI found an unrelated flaky test in `tests/org/context.test.ts` (T-030): the default branch depends on the UUIDv7 order of two branches created in the same millisecond. Fixed separately in `a755508`.
- Review round 1 (codex):
  - **HIGH, fixed:** `onboarding_grant_active` used `now()` (transaction start), so a long transaction or one that waited on a lock could use an expired grant. It now uses `clock_timestamp()`, and drafting re-checks the grant after taking the `staff_access` lock.
    - Tests: a transaction started before expiry is refused after it; a draft that waited on the clinic's staff lock past expiry is refused and logged; renewal uses `clock_timestamp()`.
    - Mutants: "grant by now()" and "draft no recheck" are killed.
  - **MEDIUM, kept by design, now asserted:** a caller without a valid session is not logged. This matches T-021/T-023: no one to attribute, and an unauthenticated caller who knows a tenant id must not be able to write into that tenant's audit chain. A test asserts that neither the vendor log nor the tenant chain grows.
- Review round 2 (codex), **HIGH, fixed:** a renewal that passed the active check, then waited on a vendor transaction holding the grant row for share, could extend a grant that expired meanwhile.
  - The renewal now locks the grant row `FOR UPDATE` before the check, and the UPDATE repeats the conditions (not renewed, not ended, `expires_at > clock_timestamp()`).
  - Test: a vendor transaction holds the grant past expiry while the Owner renews; the renewal is refused, with no change and no audit row. Mutant "renew no relock" is killed.
- Review round 3 (codex), two HIGH findings, both fixed:
  - **Lock order.** Drafting took the grant row (share) and then the `staff_access` lock; renewal took them in reverse, which could deadlock. Drafting now takes `staff_access` first and then the grant (inside the check), the same order as renewal. Test: 5 rounds of two drafts racing a renewal produce no deadlock (`40P01`) and all drafts are stored. Mutant "draft lock order" is killed.
  - **Predicate before the lock.** In `onboarding_grant_active`, the expiry predicate ran before the `FOR SHARE` wait and was not re-evaluated for elapsed time. It is now plpgsql: lock the row first, then judge with `clock_timestamp()`. Test: an access check waits on a row held past expiry; the holder rolls back; the check is refused. Mutant "predicate before lock" is killed.
- Review round 4 (codex), fixed:
  - **HIGH, deadlock within one transaction.** A vendor transaction could check access (grant row share) and then draft (wait on `staff_access`) while the Owner's renewal held `staff_access` and waited on the row. `onboarding_grant_check` now takes `staff_access` before the grant row on every path, reads included, so a transaction holding the grant always holds `staff_access` first.
    - Test: access, then a renewal that waits, then a draft in the same vendor transaction; both succeed.
    - Mutant "check without staff lock" is killed.
  - **Time-of-check before the insert.** The check's `vendor_log` can wait on the tenant's audit lock after the grant was judged. Drafting judges the grant again right before the insert.
    - Test: the audit lock is held past expiry; the draft is refused and nothing is written.
    - Mutant "draft no final recheck" is killed.
- Review round 5 (codex), **HIGH, fixed:** lock inversion with the lifecycle. A lifecycle edge locked the tenant row and then (through the grant trigger) needed `staff_access`, while a vendor check held `staff_access` and later needed the tenant row (the foreign key of its log row), so the two could deadlock.
  - One order per tenant now holds everywhere: `staff_access`, then the tenant row, then the grant row, then the audit chain.
  - The trigger takes `staff_access`. Every lifecycle entry point takes it before the tenant row: `vendor_console_tenant_advance`, `tenant_lifecycle_apply_request` and `tenant_lifecycle_lock`, all redefined in 0044.
  - Test: a vendor check holds `staff_access` while go-live runs through the console; the vendor then logs and reads, and both finish. The mutant without the lock deadlocks (`40P01`) and is killed.
- After round 5, two earlier locks became redundant, and the full mutation run proved it (their mutants were equivalent): the extra `staff_access` lock in drafting, and the renewal's `FOR UPDATE` (no vendor transaction can hold the grant row without already holding `staff_access`, which the renewal holds). Both were removed. The renewal UPDATE keeps its repeated conditions. The round-2 and round-4 regression tests still pass.
- Review round 6 (codex), **HIGH, fixed:** a refused `vendor_console_tenant_advance` (or a refused guard in `onboarding_grant_check`) logged, which takes the audit lock, before taking `staff_access`. Mixed calls in two transactions could then deadlock. Both functions now take `staff_access` first, before anything that can log, and only for a tenant that exists.
  - Test: two transactions, a refused advance plus access checks on each, finish without a deadlock.
  - Mutants: "check without staff lock" and "advance staff lock late" are killed.
  - Other console writers in 0040/0042 (owner invitation, Tim Vendor) log to a tenant without `staff_access`. They never lock the grant or tenant row while holding the audit lock and then wait for `staff_access`, so they cannot close a cycle with the grant paths.
- Review round 7 (codex): **APPROVE**. Final run: 27/27 mutants killed (migration file verified intact after the run); full suite 717 passing.
- Review round 1 (claude, through 9Router `cc/claude-opus-5-5`), all fixed:
  - **MEDIUM:** a refused kind of 29–31 characters made `not_in_grant_<kind>` longer than the `audit_events.reason_code` check (41). The audit insert failed, the whole call rolled back, and the probe left no trace. Kinds are now recorded by name only up to 27 characters; longer ones are recorded as plain `not_in_grant`. Test: kinds of 28 and 29 characters, both in the vendor log and the tenant chain. Mutant "long kind overflow" is killed.
  - **LOW:** a caller without a session took the clinic's `staff_access` lock (contention). `onboarding_grant_check` now resolves the session first. `vendor_console_tenant_advance` takes the lock only for a valid session (`vendor_session_valid`). Test: while `staff_access` is held, sessionless calls return at once. Mutant "lock before session" is killed.
  - **LOW (test gap):** an email that becomes a vendor account after the Owner confirmed cannot accept the invitation (`staff_invite_accept` raises 42501, no membership). Now tested.
  - **INFO:** the design text was aligned with what was built (end reasons; draft states).
- Review round 2 (claude): **APPROVE**. Its notes were taken: the vendor-account acceptance test now also checks the `memberships_vendor_guard` message. The double session read in the advance path is a documented residual: the window is tiny and the worst case is one `40P01` abort, with no wrong grant and no lost audit.
