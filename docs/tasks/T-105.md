## Boundaries recorded before any code (Panel 2, 2026-11-19)
- **G1 (b): no production settlement intake.** `GatewaySettlementReceived` has no fact and no writer on disk. Scenario 20 runs the REAL
  posting path on a synthetic settlement fact created by the test harness; no finance form, no corrections policy, no provider call.
  **Production gateway-settlement intake: NOT BUILT** (gateway clearing stays open in real books until a later task builds it).
- **G2 / G3:** statements are internal and generic, by account class, with stable account keys and Indonesian labels; they are **not**
  labelled SAK-compliant (SAK EMKM / EP presentation waits for the accountant, A-03). No numeric account codes.
- **G4: draft map ≠ approved books.** PR-26 posts automatically "per an approved posting map". The rules shipped here are the DRAFT map
  (`11` + the fixture), versioned. Every automatic entry records the map version it was posted under; an entry could become **official** only
  through an explicit activation decision (below) for a version covered by approved `posting_map_signoff` evidence (PR-97). Until then the
  books are **Pratinjau** (preview): the page,
  statements and trial balance say so on every view, and journal export is refused ("Posting map belum ditandatangani"). Rules marked
  PENDING in the fixture (A-10 forfeited DP, the 12a split, Q-05 DP / forfeit VAT, rounding) stay draft until a signed version contains
  their decision. Blocking export is not what makes a rule official; the signed version is.
- **E1 / E2 (Panel 2 clarification): fail-closed until decided.** Production activation of the books and any promotion of historical
  draft entries stay closed until authority and retroactivity are explicitly decided; a compliance evidence row alone promotes nothing.
  T-105 ships no activation writer: every automatic entry is draft (Pratinjau), and journal export needs the signed map version AND an
  activation that cannot exist yet, so it is refused. PENDING rules stay fixture / draft only. The open gates are listed separately
  (G-ACT, G-RETRO, G-PENDING, G-EXPORT, G-SETTLE; T-105 notes `OPEN-GATES.md`) and are not approvals.

## What is built (migration `0123_ledger`; DEC-118)
- **Database invariants:** `ledger_accounts` (31 system accounts seeded per tenant now and at creation; five classes; stable keys, Indonesian
  labels), `ledger_periods`, `journal_entries` (unique posting key; map version on automatic entries; `original_period`), `journal_lines` (one
  side, branch dimension), `ledger_activations` (no writer). Append-only (entries, lines, periods, accounts, activations; TRUNCATE refused);
  every entry balanced with ≥ 2 lines at COMMIT (deferred constraint trigger); lines only with their entry (`ledger_entry_open`); nothing posts
  into a locked period. RLS read for owner / finance / auditor; the operator in a support session (money totals class); the app writes nothing
  directly.
- **Posting rules** (one per canonical matrix event; definers, not granted): PaymentAllocated (line → paid-not-delivered or package line →
  packages; VAT = the allocation's `vat_portion`), TreatmentFinished (counted single treatment; VAT = its `line_delivery`; an addition
  performed before its line exists, or whose line's manual discount is pending, waits — nothing posts — and is woken durably by the
  line's `InvoiceIssued` / the discount's `ApprovalDecided`; it posts the final line at its delivery date), SessionRedeemed (VAT `session_delivery`;
  cross-branch clearing in the same entry), ConsultationFeeCharged, ProductHandedOver (+ COGS at moving average), BalanceToppedUp,
  BalanceMoved (expiry / cancel / substitution / interrupted / DP / refund; delivered refund → revenue; VAT reversed per its `vat_events` or
  refund), ReceivableCreated / Collected / WrittenOff, booking DP received / applied / forfeited (PENDING A-10, draft) / refunded, RefundPaid
  (cash exception), ProductReturned (COGS), StockConsumed / StockWasted, StockTransferred (inter-branch), GoodsReceived, SupplierInvoiceRecorded
  (± price variance), ExpenseRecorded / Reversed, SupplierPaymentAllocated / Reversed, SupplierReturned, SupplierCreditApplied, Commission
  accrued / adjusted / paid, InvoiceVoided / SupplierInvoiceReversed / SupplierCreditReversed (mirrors of their originals), and the
  GatewaySettlementReceived rule (no production intake; synthetic test fact only). Exact scope: the 33 posting event types of the
  `ledger.posting` consumer, plus two wake-ups that post nothing of their own (`InvoiceIssued`, `ApprovalDecided` of a manual discount). Not posted by design: `StockReturned` (unconsumed issued stock, `11` :133), the notification-only `ConsultationFeeRecharged`,
  `BookingDepositMovedToBalance`, `JournalReversed`, and the non-posting events of `11` :136. `OpeningImportApplied` is T-128.
- **Consumption order** (Panel 2 REVISE r2): `events_claim` skips live leases and locked rows, so a domain reversal can be consumed before its
  original. Only reversal rules read journal entries; each now posts its original from the immutable fact first (same posting key, so the
  original's own event later posts nothing; `source_event_id` stays NULL for an original posted on a reversal's behalf), then mirrors it.
  Applies to InvoiceVoided (consultation charge, allocations), SupplierInvoiceReversed, SupplierCreditReversed (an application already
  reversed with its note still posts — it happened), SupplierPaymentReversed, ExpenseReversed and the cancelled inter-branch transfer.
  Every mirror is dated at the domain reversal (`voided_at`, `reversed_at`, `closed_at`), never at the worker's clock. An original that
  legitimately posts nothing (a zero, simulated or unpaid-undelivered fact; an expense-kind supplier invoice) leaves nothing to mirror and the
  reversal acks with nothing posted — not a loss: there is no original entry to reverse. Dispatch `ledger_post_event` (granted; refuses another tenant's event); consumer
  `ledger.posting` (`src/modules/accounting/ledger/posting.ts`).
- **Finance:** `ledger_manual_post` (balanced, reasoned, idempotent, open period only; audited), `ledger_period_lock` (past months only, once).
- **Readers:** `ledger_report` (trial balance, P&L, balance sheet with the period result, cash-flow by counter class; branch or consolidated;
  generic, not SAK-labelled), `ledger_journal`. Every result carries the books' status (preview).
- **Export:** `ledger_journal_export` returns a refusal (audited) unless the posting map version is signed AND the books are activated.
- **Page** `/keuangan/buku-besar`: status (Pratinjau — bukan buku resmi), filters, KPIs, statements, cash-flow, trial balance, journal list,
  Jurnal Manual and Kunci Periode (finance), export state; `/keuangan/buku-besar/ekspor` (409 with the refusal reason).
