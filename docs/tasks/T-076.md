## Gap: the package's contents

A catalog item of kind `package` has a price, `min_dp_pct`, `installments_allowed` and `validity_days`, but nothing says which services it contains or how many sessions. No spec names where that is defined (DATA-15 lists no composition).

**Proposed:** a `package_contents` table in the packages module: the package's catalog version, the service item and a count.
- **Who writes it:** the owner or admin pusat, through `package_contents_set(version, rows)`, only while the package version is not yet used (`catalog_version_reference`, CLIN-011). Each later version needs its own contents.
- **UI:** shown and edited in Paket & Saldo › Isi Paket, with no Katalog change.
- **Alternative:** a `package_items` jsonb on `catalog_versions`, which re-creates the catalog publish path (T-033's).

Open point 1 asks whether this is a contract gap that needs the owner (the escalation condition: "needs a contract change in docs/spec/").

## Design (proposed)

### 1. Sale through the Kasir: a package invoice

- **The invoice.** A package is sold on a Kasir invoice for the patient with **no visit and not a counter sale**. `invoice_open` already allows a patient with no visit. Its lines are package items only, one per package. Package and ordinary lines never mix.
- **At issue** (`invoice_issue` re-created): each package line creates one `packages` row (`sold_branch_id`, price = the line total, `validity_end` = issue date + validity, min DP per BILL-9, the line's id) and its `package_sessions`:
  - `allocated_value` per BILL-7: equal split, or proportional to the services' normal prices, floor then largest remainder (the T-072 helper);
  - a tax snapshot from the line (category, PKP at issue, the session's VAT share).
  - The line's discount applies to the package price before the split.
- **Paying.** Payments on that invoice are ordinary T-072/T-075 Terima receipts: the DP, then each Bayar Cicilan, in cash, EDC, transfer or balance (scenario 13), with VAT at payment.
  - A package's `paid_value` = the real money allocated to its line (`kasir_line_paid`). No new allocation target: DATA-28's "package" target is realised as the package's own invoice line.
  - The port works the same way; its simulated money never counts.
- **Minimum DP.** The first Terima must reach the minimum DP, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_INPUT: min_dp`. Before that, no session unlocks anyway, because the minimum DP is at least the first session's value.
- **Installments.** No schedule, no interest. The open package invoice stays in the Kasir's open list as Bayar Cicilan.
- **Unlocking (BILL-8)** is computed, never stored. A session is unlocked when its allocated value fits within `paid_value − Σ(redeemed + reserved allocated values)`.
  - Single-treatment packages unlock in session order.
  - Mixed packages: the patient chooses within the paid value (CLIN-010); the doctor's stage is a booking matter.

### 2. Reserve and redeem (B-15, BILL-24)

- **`package_reserve(package, session or service, visit)`:** FO/cashier or a manager at the visit's branch, any branch of the tenant (BILL-16).
  - Moves available → reserved for an open visit of the package's patient (canonical).
  - Refused with no eligible session (CLIN-028: nothing touched) or when `redeemed + reserved + this > paid` ("Perlu bayar RpX lagi").
  - Booking-time reservation (Jadwal) is left to the booking writer. This task reserves at check-in, on the visit.
- **Release.** `package_release(session)`, which T-064's allowed cancellation uses (CLIN-009), returns the session to available.
- **Redemption.** A trigger on `treatment_executions` (as in T-074): when an execution of the visit finishes for a plan item whose service matches a session reserved on that visit:
  - counted (completed or patient stop) → redeemed, at the executing branch, with the execution referenced;
  - medical or clinic stop → back to available (scenario 7d, PR-14).
  - **Event:** `SessionRedeemed`, carrying ids only.
- **Closing the visit** with a reserved session unused releases it.
- **Treatment start** (re-created `treatment_payment_unpaid`, T-074): a plan item covered by a session reserved on this visit is paid; nothing on the Kasir is needed. The T-074 BILL-1 rule "a confirmed selection with no issued ordinary invoice" does not apply to it.

### 3. Expiry and cancellation (BILL-10, PR-20)

- **Writer.** `package_close(package, reason)`, with two triggers:
  - **Expiry:** an internal call from a daily expiry job over packages past `validity_end` (worker, `src/modules/packages/expiry.ts`, idempotent per package).
  - **Cancellation before expiry:** Batalkan Paket, owner/manager with a reason.
- **Effects:**
  - reserved sessions are released;
  - the paid-unused value goes to the patient balance (`from_expiry` or `from_cancel`, gross, through T-075's `balance_post`);
  - the unpaid remainder is cancelled: not a debt, no receivable;
  - the package closes and its open sessions are `closed`;
  - VAT recognised on the paid-unused part is reversed as a fact (`vat_reversed` on the package), journals in M8 (scenario 19).
- **The package invoice's outstanding.** It must drop the cancelled remainder, so it leaves the open list and refuses further payment. `invoice_lines` gains `cancelled_amount` (set once, when the package closes; guard), and the outstanding and room helpers subtract it.
  - **Re-created:** `kasir_outstanding`, `invoice_lines_guard`, `kasir_record_payment`'s room, and `payment_line_room`.
  - **Open point 3:** is this acceptable, or should a closed package's invoice keep its outstanding but be marked "dibatalkan" and refuse payment?

### 4. Substitution (BILL-11, scenario 12)

- **`package_substitute(session, new service, reason)`** by FO/cashier, approved by a doctor or facility head of the branch through the approvals framework (type `package_substitution`).
- **Cheaper substitute:** the session's value becomes the new service's price at the branch, and the difference goes to balance (`from_substitution`).
- **Dearer substitute:** the session keeps its value and the difference is billed as an ordinary visit invoice line (scenario 12a), paid as usual.
- **Record:** `package_substitutions` keeps the history; the session row is versioned, never rewritten.

### 5. Tax (scenarios 16 and 19)

- **Each session carries:** the tax category, the basis, `vat_amount` (its share of the line's VAT), `vat_recognised_amount` and `vat_recognised_at`.
- **At payment:** the line recognises VAT as in T-072, and the sessions receive their share by allocated value.
- **At redemption after the PKP date, for a session whose VAT was not recognised (scenario 16):** the delivery VAT is recorded as a fact only while the `vat_on_invoices` gate is open. PENDING Q-05; no journal here.
- **At expiry (scenario 19):** the reversal is recorded and the balance entry is gross. VAT counted once is asserted through the line and session facts.

### 6. Paket & Saldo (UX-13): `src/app/(app)/kasir/paket-saldo/**`

- **Patient search, then the patient's page:**
  - packages: paid, Terpakai, Dipesan, available, expiry, sold branch;
  - the balance with its entries (T-075's reader);
  - booking deposits: a placeholder until T-077.
- **Actions:**
  - Pesan Sesi, for an open visit today, with the blocked copy "Perlu bayar RpX lagi";
  - Bayar Cicilan, a link to the Kasir invoice;
  - Batalkan Paket, owner/manager with a reason;
  - Isi Paket, the contents editor (open point 1).
- **Top Up Saldo:** UX-13 lists it, but a top-up is a payment with no invoice line (DATA-28 `balance_top_up` target). **Open point 2:** do it as a Kasir invoice line of a platform "Isi Saldo" item (credit `top_up` at payment), or defer.
- **States:** loading, empty, filtered-empty, error, success, blocked. Browser evidence at 390/820/1180/1440 and 200 % text.

## Plan review r2 (Argon `33416c49`, REVISE): findings checked against the code and adopted

This section supersedes r1 where they differ.

- **F1 (allocation).**
  - **The split:** `package_allocate(amount, weights)`, with weights > 0, at least one, no cap; floor in numeric, then +1 to the largest remainders, ties by index. It replaces `kasir_allocate`, whose overpayment cap makes it unusable here (0087:174).
  - **Order:** `package_contents.seq` and `package_sessions.seq` (1..N).
  - **Refusals at issue:** `invoice_issue` refuses a package with no contents (`package_empty`) or a component without a positive branch price.
- **F2 (interrupted to balance).**
  - **New columns on `invoice_lines`:** `moved_to_balance` (≤ paid, guarded), `vat_reversed_amount` and `vat_reversed_at`. Interrupted moves and T-078's refunds share the ceiling `paid − moved_to_balance`.
  - **A stopped addition:** its unpaid remainder is cancelled (`cancelled_amount`) and its paid part is moved.
  - **An initial item:** its share `= line_total × plan_item.qty ÷ line.quantity`, from a fully paid ordinary line with room left.
  - **VAT:** reversed in proportion on the line.
  - **The visit's blocker:** cleared only when no other unresolved clinic stop remains; then `kasir_visit_payment` runs.
- **F3 (substitution invoices).**
  - **Invoice kinds:** ordinary, addition (`plan_item_id`), substitution (`substitution_id`, with or without a visit), and package. The one-ordinary-invoice rule and the start rule count ordinary invoices only.
  - **`invoice_line_snapshot`:** the 6-argument version is dropped for a 7-argument one. The price override is derived only from the approved `package_substitutions` row locked by `invoice_issue` (quantity 1, no discount, its new item); `substitution_id` is frozen in the guard.
  - **A zero difference:** changes the session's service only (no balance entry, no line).
  - **Lookup:** the start check finds the substitution line by `(tenant, substitution_id)` across invoices.
  - **`package_close`** also cancels unpaid substitution lines and moves paid ones for unredeemed sessions (with the VAT reversal).
- **F4 (VAT).**
  - **`package_sync_vat(tenant, package, at)`:** distributes `max(0, line recognised − package reversed)` over the sessions ordered redeemed, reserved, then available by seq, each capped at its own VAT. It is called on payment, reserve, release, redemption, cheaper substitution and before close.
  - **At redemption after PKP with the gate open (scenario 16):** a session with `vat_amount = 0` is re-split by `tax_vat_split(allocated, category, true)`, and the unrecognised part is recognised at `finished_at`.
- **F5 (triggers).**
  - **Finish order:** the package finish trigger is named `treatment_executions_00_package_finish`, so it fires before `treatment_executions_addition_finished`.
  - **The binding:** `reserved_execution_id` stays on a redeemed session and is cleared only when the session returns to available. `addition_unbilled_counted` and `addition_finished` skip an execution bound to a session.
  - **One shared coverage helper** (used by the start rule and the AFTER INSERT binding): the plan item's qty must be 1, with an unbound reserved session of its service on the visit whose dearer difference, if any, is paid in real money.
  - **Uncovered items** need the paid ordinary bill when the selection is done **or** any session is reserved or redeemed on the visit.
  - **Clinic-stop blocker:** a clinic stop of a package session clears the blocker that `treatment_finish` sets. The package finish trigger cannot, because it runs first, so it is done at `treatment_finish`'s end; re-creating `treatment_finish` (0081) is added to the drift list.
- **F6 (top-up through the Kasir records).**
  - **Columns:** `payments.invoice_id` and `receipts.invoice_id` become nullable; `payment_allocations.top_up_id` is added (`num_nonnulls(invoice_line_id, top_up_id) = 1`).
  - **The writer:** `balance_top_up` issues a KW receipt, a payment with no invoice (the patient named) and its allocation, then credits `top_up`.
  - **Re-created:** `payments_guard`, `payment_allocations_guard`, `receipt_get` (auditor masking from the payment's patient), `kasir_receipt_body` (a top-up body).
- **F7 (scan).** `package_expiry_scan` takes each tenant's `staff_access` lock before locking its rows, re-checks under the lock, and audits through `package_audit_service` (`package_expiry`).
- **Build order inside the one run.**
  - **Checkpoint 1:** migration 0091, the domain, the drift test, and the domain, RLS and regression suites green.
  - **Checkpoint 2:** the UX-13 UI (one route, inline sections), the browser spec, isolation and stale-context, mutations, close.

## Plan review r3 (Argon `53d8864b`, REVISE): three findings checked and adopted

1. **A package execution keeps its identity after a non-counted finish.**
   - **Recording:** `package_sessions.returned_execution_ids uuid[]` gets the execution appended when the finish trigger returns the session to available.
   - **The helper:** `package_execution_covered(tenant, execution)` is true when the execution is reserved on a session or in its returned list.
   - **`treatment_finish`** (re-created) does not set the clinic-stop blocker for a covered execution, instead of setting and then clearing it.
   - **`interrupted_to_balance`** refuses a covered execution and leaves covered ones out of its "unresolved clinic stop" check.
2. **`package_sync_vat`.**
   - **Non-PKP at issue:** a no-op when the package invoice was not PKP at issue. The session's VAT is written only at redemption after the PKP date (scenario 16), and nothing later overwrites it.
   - **PKP at issue:** when the line's recognised VAT minus the package's reversed VAT exceeds Σ(non-closed sessions' VAT), as after a cheaper substitution, the excess is added to `packages.vat_reversed_amount`.
   - **On close:** `vat_reversed_amount = line recognised − Σ(redeemed sessions' recognised)`.
3. **Payments with no invoice.**
   - **Matching:** `payments_guard` matches the receipt with `IS NOT DISTINCT FROM` and the same branch.
   - **A payment with no invoice** must be cash, EDC or transfer, name a patient, be real, have no charge, and match a `balance_top_ups` row for its receipt.
   - **Error code:** `ERR_MIN_DP_NOT_MET` is added to the known codes of `src/modules/payments/payments.ts` (a new declared scope expansion) and of `kasir.ts`.
