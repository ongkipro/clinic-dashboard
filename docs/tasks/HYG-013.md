## Why

DEC-030 (1) (owner 2026-11-03) lets a clinic Owner revoke an approved dual vendor/clinic identity in Akses Vendor, "built with T-028". T-028 could not build it: no database function existed, `drizzle/**` was protected there, and revoking needed a decision on the already-active membership. Owner decision 2026-11-07 (DEC-044, option A): revoking ends that account's clinic membership in this tenant at once; the vendor account is not touched; done before T-032.

Verified gap (source, HEAD `29b6a55`): `vendor_dual_identity_clinic_decide` refuses every call once `clinic_approved_at` is set (0048:584); `vendor_history_guard` (0032:719–754) allows no transition out of an approved, clinic-approved request; `vendor_dual_approved` (0032:163) is read only when a membership is written (0048:950) or a vendor account is created/activated.

## Design (migration 0052)

- **Columns** on `vendor_requests`: `clinic_revoked_at`, `clinic_revoked_by` (clinic staff id), `clinic_revoked_account` (account); all three null or all set, only on a clinic-approved dual identity (CHECK). State stays `approved` (history: both approvals and the revocation remain visible).
- **`vendor_dual_approved`**: also requires `clinic_revoked_at IS NULL`.
- **`vendor_history_guard`**: one more allowed transition: an approved, clinic-approved, not yet revoked dual identity gets its revocation stamp once; nothing else changes.
- **`vendor_dual_identity_clinic_revoke(p_request)`** (SECURITY DEFINER, `clinic_app`): `tenant_actor_lock` (staff_access, actor on the wall clock); Owner only; request of this tenant (`FOR UPDATE`); refusals returned and audited (`not_owner`, `not_found`, `self`: the subject never revokes their own, `not_effective`: not approved by both or already revoked). On success, under `vendor_account_lock(subject)` (the order memberships_vendor_guard uses): stamp the revocation; end the subject's active membership in this tenant (`effective_to = clock_timestamp()`), its duties, its pending invitations re-checked, its sessions revoked (`staff_revoke … 'membership_ended'`), each audited; audit `vendor_dual_identity_revoked`. Returns `revoked`.
- **`vendor_dual_identities`**: adds `revoked_at`; `effective` false once revoked.
- **App**: `vendorDualIdentityClinicRevoke` (staff module), `revokeDualIdentityAction` (stale context first; result cookie `identitas-dicabut:<id>`, shown only when the row shows the revocation), a "Cabut" button with a confirmation naming the consequence on an effective identity (Owner only), a "Dicabut" badge and the revocation date.

- **Review round 1 (GPT-6.1 Sol) REQUEST_CHANGES, both fixed:**
  - P1: a second request for the same account carrying the clinic approval and still awaiting the platform was not revoked, so the platform's later approval made the identity effective again without a new clinic approval.
