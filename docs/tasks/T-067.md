## Open points (for the reviewer)

1. **Purchase orders** (`purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_lines`; branch-scoped, personal): supplier, branch, number (per tenant, generated `PO-YYYYMM-NNNN`), expected date, lines (stock item, quantity, unit price in integer rupiah), state draft → sent → partially_received → received | cancelled (cancel only before any receipt). Writers `po_save` (draft edit), `po_send`, `po_cancel`; purchasing, admin pusat, owner (any branch named), manager (own branch).
2. **Goods receipts** (`goods_receipts`, header: PO optional, supplier, branch, depot = default gudang, delivery note, received at/by; lines through T-066's `supplier_receive_into` with source `goods_receipt`/header id → `supplier_receipts` rows (batch, expiry, quantity, unit cost)). Partial and full receipts against a PO line (received ≤ ordered, a receipt beyond the ordered quantity refused); a receipt without PO allowed (cash purchase, BILL-34). Writer `goods_receipt_record` (pharmacist, purchasing — the receipt roles of T-060/T-066). GRNI per receipt line = quantity × unit cost (integer rupiah, rounded per line).
3. **Supplier invoices** (`supplier_invoices`, `supplier_invoice_lines`; tenant-ledger, money_total): type `purchase` (lines matched to goods-receipt lines: each receipt line invoiced at most once in total quantity; the invoiced amount per line may differ from GRNI — purchase price variance is a posting concern, stored per line as `grni_amount` and `amount`) or `expense` (reserved for T-084: refused here); supplier, branch (of the receipts; one branch per invoice), number unique per supplier ignoring case (blocked copy "Nomor faktur sudah ada untuk pemasok ini"), invoice date, due date (default invoice date + supplier terms), total = sum of lines, state open → paid (outstanding 0) | reversed. Writer `supplier_invoice_record` (purchasing, finance).
4. **Payments and allocations** (`supplier_payments`: supplier, amount, method cash / bank_transfer, date, reference, command key; `supplier_payment_allocations`: payment → invoice, amount, command key). `supplier_payment_record(p_key, p)` records a payment and allocates it in the same transaction to the named invoices (or oldest due first); **no over-allocation**: the invoice row is locked (`FOR UPDATE`) and outstanding computed under the lock; an allocation takes `least(requested, outstanding)` (the second concurrent payment of scenario (b) allocates 1,000,000 and the rest stays unallocated on the payment as a supplier prepayment) — or is refused when outstanding is 0. Unallocated payment amounts can be allocated later (`supplier_payment_allocate`). Finance only (payments are money out; purchasing records invoices). Outstanding = total − allocations − credit applications.
5. **Credit notes and applications** (`supplier_credit_notes`: supplier, number unique per supplier, amount, source `return` (closes T-066's credit expectation as `received` with `credit_note_id`) or `invoice` (a price correction against an invoice), available = amount − applications; `supplier_credit_applications`: credit note → invoice, amount, both locked, min(available, outstanding)). Writers `supplier_credit_note_record` (purchasing, finance), `supplier_credit_apply` (finance). Write-off of an expectation (`supplier_credit_expectation_write_off`, finance, reason).
6. **Reversals** (BILL-34 "never deletion"): `supplier_payment_reverse` (finance, reason: the payment and its allocations marked reversed, invoices' outstanding restored) and `supplier_invoice_reverse` (purchasing/finance, reason; only with no live allocation or application). Credit-note reversal: deferred (no scenario; recorded open).
7. **Balance and roll-forward**: reader `supplier_balance(p_supplier)` = invoices − allocations − applied credits (open invoices with outstanding, unallocated payments, available credits); the monthly roll-forward report is M8 (posting) — recorded open.
8. **Events** (ids only): `PurchaseOrderSent`, `GoodsReceived {receipt_id, purchase_order_id?}`, `SupplierInvoiceRecorded`, `SupplierPaymentAllocated`, `SupplierCreditApplied`, `SupplierInvoiceReversed`, `SupplierPaymentReversed`.
9. **UI** (`src/app/(app)/pembelian/**`): `/pembelian` → role home; `/pembelian/pemasok` (list, detail, add/edit — T-066 writers), `/pembelian/po` (list by state, create/edit draft, send, cancel; detail with receipts; Terima Barang sheet per line with batch, expiry, quantity, unit cost — blocked copy for a missing expiry), `/pembelian/penerimaan` (receipts list, receipt without PO), `/pembelian/retur` (T-066 return form and list), `/pembelian/utang` (invoices: open, due, overdue; Catat Faktur from receipt lines; Bayar; credit notes; supplier balance). `/pembelian/transfer` (T-061's writers; adopted r1 P1-1). Finance sees Utang only; pharmacist PO and Penerimaan.
