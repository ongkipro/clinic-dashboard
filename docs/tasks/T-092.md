## 3. Flows
- Create: from a conversation (CS/BC in the inbox; source whatsapp, conversation_id, the conversation's branch/patient), or manually (walk-in,
  phone; source required). One open lead per conversation.
- Stage changes forward only (new → contacted → booked → arrived → converted; lost from any open stage with a reason; reopen lost → new?
  open point). booked needs an appointment of the lead's patient; arrived needs a visit (checked in) of that appointment / patient;
  **converted needs a payment** (Scenario PR-29): an allocation (paid > 0, not voided/refunded in full) on an invoice of the lead's patient
  at or after the lead's creation (or of the arrived visit) — the definer checks it; otherwise `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: payment_required`.
- Automatic transitions (open point): booking an appointment for a lead's patient / checking in / the first payment move the lead forward
  (definer hooks on appointments/visits/payments would touch 0061/0063/0087 tables — outside the allowed paths? drizzle/** is allowed, but
  re-creating those writers is risk; prefer pull-based: the lead page derives "booked/arrived/paid" facts and staff confirm, or a lightweight
  AFTER trigger on payment allocations). Decide in review.
- first_response_at: the linked conversation's first_response_at, else the contacted transition time.
