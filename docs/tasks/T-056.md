## Open points (for the reviewer)

1. **What approval changes.** Proposal: an approved request applies its change to the patient's active care plan through a definer (not through `plan_item_add`, which needs a draft exam): addition → a new plan item (stage, catalog version, qty, dose) at the visit's plan; plan change → qty/dose of an item (as remove + add, keeping history: `plan_items` are insert-and-remove only); substitution → remove item X, add item Y. Each applied item references the request. The consent then reports "outdated" (T-054) — that is the "patient agreement step opens": `/persetujuan-tindakan/[visitId]` shows Perlu persetujuan ulang. No money effect (M4).
2. **Plan items after signing.** Checked: `plans_guard` (0070:293) allows inserts and removal-only updates on any active plan regardless of the examination state; only the writers (`plan_item_add` / `_remove` via `exam_draft`) require the author's draft. So the request definer can insert and remove items on a signed examination's plan without touching the guard. Proposal: add nullable `plan_items.request_id` (the request that added it) and `removed_by_request_id` (the request that removed it) and keep `plans_guard` unchanged except that the removal path must also allow setting `removed_by_request_id` together with `removed_at` (one-line change, drift-tested). `plan_items.examination_id` of an added item = the plan's creating examination? or the visit's examination? Proposal: the visit's examination (FK requires the same branch).
3. **Who proposes.** 07 has no row for "propose"; PR-45 says "from the room or BC". Proposal: nurse, therapist, BC, and doctor of the visit's branch propose (DEC-068, revisable pending Q-07); FO and others never.
4. **Who decides.** 07:47: doctor ●, facility head ●, nurse ◐ delegated. Proposal: the examining doctor of the visit's examination, any doctor of the branch when the exam's author is not on shift? Simpler default: **any doctor of the branch or the facility head**; nurse delegation is not modelled yet (no delegation record exists) — refused with a recorded reason; the requester never decides their own request.
5. **Which visit/plan.** A request names an open visit at the branch that has a signed (or draft) examination with an active plan; additions without a plan open… Proposal: require an active plan of the visit's patient cluster (the plan the room works from); a request on a visit without a plan is refused (`no_plan`).
6. **Price impact.** 17:153 lists "price impact" on the card. Proposal: show the branch price (`catalog_branch_price`, 0056:85) × qty for additions/substitutions (difference for substitution), as display only; no money written (M4).
7. **FO alert.** PR-54 notifications: a new type `clinical_request_approved` for `fo_cashier` (subject `clinical_request`), raised at approval of an addition (also of substitution/plan change with a price impact? proposal: every approval, the FO's queue needs to know). A `clinical_request_pending` type to the deciders when a request is made.
8. **Withdraw / expire.** Proposal: the requester may cancel a pending request; pending requests of a closed visit are cancelled automatically at read time? Simpler: refused to decide when the visit is closed (`visit_closed`); cancel by requester.

When the plan's consent is then not active, an open visit's `consent` step moves from `done` to `in_progress` — the patient agreement step opens (`/persetujuan-tindakan/[visitId]` shows Perlu persetujuan ulang). 2. **Plan items after signing:** nullable `plan_items.request_id`, `removed_by_request_id` (CHECK: the latter only with `removed_at`); the added item takes the plan's branch and the visit's examination; `plans_guard` re-created: inserts open without removal columns; the one update sets `removed_at` and optionally `removed_by_request_id`; drift test `tests/db/plans-drift.test.ts` (+ expected).
3. **Proposers** (DEC-068, revisable pending Q-07): BC, doctor, nurse, therapist at the visit's branch; a BC never sets a dose (addition/substitution: none; plan change: the target's dose kept).
4. **Deciders:** doctor or facility head at the request's branch; nurse refused (`nurse_delegation_not_configured`) until Q-07; self-decision refused in the writer and by a CHECK.
5. **Visit and plan:** an open visit at the active branch with an examination (draft or signed: `no_examination`), an active plan of the patient's merge cluster (`plan_closed`, `no_plan`).
6. **Price impact:** live, integer rupiah, `proposed qty × branch price − current qty × branch price` for all three kinds; display only.
7. **Notifications:** `clinical_request_pending` (doctor, facility head) at create; `clinical_request_approved` (FO/cashier) at every approval; ids only, never clinical text, items, doses or amounts.
8. **Cancel:** the requester cancels a pending request; decide refuses a closed visit (`visit_closed`) or plan (`plan_closed`); no auto-cancel.
