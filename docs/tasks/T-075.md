## Design (proposed)

### 1. Tables (`src/modules/balance/schema.ts`; migration 0090)

- **`balance_accounts`:** tenant, patient (the canonical patient at creation), `balance` (bigint ≥ 0, the running total) and a version. One per tenant and patient. A tenant-shared account, readable across branches.
- **`balance_entries`:** insert-only. Columns:
  - tenant, account, the branch where it happened, kind (the DATA-30 list) and signed `amount`;
  - `balance_after` (≥ 0), `vat_settled` (default false; no writer sets it true until Q-05);
  - source references, each nullable with exactly the one its kind needs: `payment_id` for `use`; `payment_allocation_id`; `charge_id`; and free `source_kind`/`source_id` for later tasks;
  - `reason` (required for manual kinds), `command_key`, the creator and role, `created_at`.

  A CHECK ties the sign to the kind: credits are > 0; `use` and `cash_refund` are < 0.
- **Never negative.** The account row is locked `FOR UPDATE`. `balance_after = balance + amount` must be ≥ 0, enforced by a CHECK on both tables and by a guard trigger requiring that `balance_after` equals the previous `balance_after` plus `amount`. Nothing is ever deleted.
- **Never expires.** No expiry column and no job.

### 2. Internal writer `balance_post(tenant, patient, branch, kind, amount, refs jsonb, key, staff, role)`

- **Not granted.** Called by definers only: this task's tender, and later T-076/T-077/T-078 and the import.
- **Steps:** resolve the canonical patient, create the account if missing, lock it, then insert the entry and update the account.
- **Errors:** `ERR_BALANCE_INSUFFICIENT` (new code, 55000) when the result would be negative. A replay of the key returns the same entry.
- **Event:** `BalanceMoved`, carrying ids, kind and amount only.

### 3. Balance as a tender (re-created `payment_receive` and `kasir_record_payment`; scope expansion of the Kasir readers)

- **Availability.** `balance` becomes available when SET-14 includes `saldo`, because `kasir_method_enabled` already maps balance to `saldo`, and the invoice has a patient.
- **Payment.** A `balance` tender writes the payment (method `balance`) and its allocations the same way as cash: the same proportional allocation and VAT at payment. The `use` entry is −amount, linked to the payment.
- **Rules.**
  - The patient of the invoice: a counter sale with no patient cannot use the balance.
  - The account must cover the amount, otherwise `ERR_BALANCE_INSUFFICIENT`.
  - No cash tendered or change on it.
  - Never through the port.
- **Readers.** `kasir_invoice_get` shows the balance as available with the patient's current balance (owner/finance/FO/manager; masked for the auditor, who sees no amount per patient).
- **Lock order:** staff_access → visit → invoice → balance account.

### 4. Readers

`balance_get(patient)`: the canonical patient's account and its last 50 entries (kind, amount, after, branch, date, source reference). Readable by owner, finance, auditor, manager and FO/cashier (any branch, since the balance is tenant-wide). No clinical content.

### 5. Open points (for the reviewer)

1. **Patient merge (T-040's `patient_merge` and `patient_unmerge`).** Proposed: the account belongs to the canonical patient. A merge that finds two accounts keeps both, and the reader and the tender use the survivor's account only. Moving value on merge needs a decision, so it is listed for the owner and not built. Alternative: refuse a merge while the merged patient has a positive balance.
2. **`payment_charges.balance_pending` (T-073).** Crediting it needs a review of the flag (late or mismatched money). Proposed: leave it to T-078 (refunds and the review screen), which owns where unexpected money goes. T-075 only provides the `from_refund` kind.
3. **Top-up (DATA-28 `balance_top_up` allocation target).** It belongs to the package/balance screen in T-076, whose allowed paths include `kasir/paket-saldo`. Not built here.
4. **The kind list.** All DATA-30 kinds are allowed by the CHECK, but only `use` has a writer here; the others arrive with their tasks through `balance_post`.

## Tests (`tests/balance/balance.test.ts`)

- **Scenario PR-21.** A balance of 400,000 with its last entry back-dated two years pays a product in full. No expiry exists.
- **Never negative.**
  - A use above the balance is refused and nothing is written.
  - Two concurrent uses that together exceed the balance: one succeeds.
  - The guards refuse a direct negative insert, a wrong `balance_after`, an update or a delete.
  - The CHECK refuses a wrong sign per kind.
- **The tender.**
  - Split tender: balance plus cash.
  - VAT recognised on a PKP line paid by balance.
  - The visit step becomes done.
  - Refused on a counter sale with no patient, when SET-14 excludes saldo, and on insufficient balance.
  - Replay by key.
  - Simulated never applies.
- **`balance_post`** (exercised through a test-only direct call by the owner role? Not granted; tested through the admin connection as a definer call).
  - Each credit kind.
  - Idempotent by key.
  - The canonical patient after a merge.
- **Readers.** Roles and branches; the auditor masked; another tenant not found.
- **Isolation, RLS allow-list, authority clock and drift** (re-created `payment_receive`, `kasir_record_payment`, `kasir_invoice_get`; scope expansions as before).

## Plan review r1 (Argon `25f6145d`, REVISE): every finding checked against the code and adopted

- **Open point 1 (merge).** A merge is refused while the duplicate has a positive balance: `patient_merges_balance_guard` raises `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: patient_has_balance` (DEC-061: redirect, never move money). No account is created for a merged patient. Every balance path resolves `patient_canonical`.
- **Open point 2 (`balance_pending`).** Not credited, recorded in DEC-086.

  - BILL-29 forbids simulated value from ever reaching balances.
  - So nothing in `balance_pending` may become balance. Code that credits real money would be unreachable and untestable now; it comes with the real adapter. DEC-084 (5)'s "until T-075" is corrected in DEC-086.
- **Open point 3.** Top-up is left to T-076. The `top_up` kind is allowed now.
- **Open point 4.**
  - All ten DATA-30 kinds are allowed.
  - Source references: `num_nonnulls(payment_id, charge_id, source_id) = 1`, `(source_kind IS NULL) = (source_id IS NULL)`, and `(kind = 'use') = (payment_id IS NOT NULL)`. `payment_allocation_id` is dropped: an entry names its payment.
  - `reason` is nullable, 3–500 characters when given.
  - `vat_settled` must be false (a CHECK), until Q-05.
- **F1 (P1, events).** `events_payload_ok` refuses numbers, so the payload carries ids and the kind only, with explicit tenant, branch, staff and role. The event type follows the kind:

  | Kind | Event |
  |---|---|
  | `use` | `BalanceUsed` |
  | `top_up` | `BalanceToppedUp` |
  | the six reclass kinds | `BalanceMoved` |
  | `cash_refund`, `opening_balance` | none (their owners emit `RefundPaid` / `OpeningImportApplied`) |

- **F2 (P1, idempotency).**
  - At most one `balance` tender per Terima.
  - A unique index on `(tenant_id, payment_id)` for `use` entries.
  - The `balance_post` key replay must match every argument; otherwise `ERR_IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. The `use` entry's key is `use:<payment_id>`.
- **F3 (P2, the running balance).** The 0077 stock pattern: `balance_entries.seq` (identity); `balance_entries_guard` (BEFORE: lock the account, `balance_after = account.balance + amount ≥ 0`, refuse update and delete); `balance_entries_apply` (AFTER INSERT: set the account's balance and version); `balance_accounts_guard` (insert at 0, version 0, not merged; update only to the newest entry's `balance_after`, version + 1; no delete).
- **F4 (P2, compatibility).**
  - `kasir_invoice_get`: balance is available only with a patient, saldo enabled in SET-14 and a balance > 0; otherwise blocked `balance` (`method_disabled` when SET-14 excludes it). It also returns `patient_balance`, which is null for the auditor or with no patient. The existing T-072/T-073 assertions keep passing.
  - `payment_receive`: `method_disabled`, or `ERR_NOT_AVAILABLE: balance` without a patient.
  - `kasir_receipt_body` re-created with "Saldo".
- **F5 (P2, masking and gates).**
  - `balance_get` masks the patient and the reason for the auditor; amounts stay visible to the auditor (IAM-12).
  - `balance_entries` is granted by column, without `reason`.
  - Scope map, support class `personal`, operator policies and no-truncate triggers on both tables.
- **F6 (P2, guards).**
  - A `use` entry's payment: method `balance`, not simulated, the same branch, `amount = −entry`, and the same canonical patient.
  - `payments_guard` (re-created): a `balance` payment has a patient and is never simulated.
  - `charge_id` is allowed by the CHECK for the later adapter, and the guard refuses a simulated charge.
- **F7 (P3, tests added).**
  - the conflicting key replay;
  - merge refused with a balance; after a zero-balance merge, the survivor's account is used;
  - credit at branch 1 spent at branch 2, readable by FO of both;
