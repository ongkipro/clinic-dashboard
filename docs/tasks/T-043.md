## Design (proposed)

**Data** (migration after T-038's):
- `staff_shifts`: tenant, branch, staff (composite FKs), date (branch-local), `starts`, `ends` (time; `24:00` as in T-036), `kind` (`work`, `break`), `created_by`, `created_at`. A break must lie inside a work shift of the same staff and date. No overlap of two work shifts of one staff on one date across all branches (one person is in one place): an exclusion constraint on (tenant, staff, tstzrange computed in the branch timezone) for `work` rows.
- `staff_leave`: tenant, staff, `kind` (`leave`, `sick`, `absent`), `from_date`, `to_date` (inclusive, branch-independent: leave applies to the person), reason, created_by/at, and `cancelled_at/by` (a leave is cancelled, never deleted).
- A week is saved as a set: `roster_set_week(branch, week_start, rows)` replaces that branch's shifts for that ISO week (rows dated in the past are kept as they were; editing the past is refused).
- RLS: read by the tenant's members with a role in the branch-scoped readers (Owner, Admin Pusat, Manager, FO/cashier, Central CS, BC, doctor, nurse, therapist — the booking roles and the staff themselves); `scope_map` branch-scoped for shifts; leave tenant-shared (the person). Support class `config`? No: leave reasons can be health-related → `personal`; shifts `config`. Operator policies (TEN-19).

**Writes** (SECURITY DEFINER under `tenant_actor_lock`, audited, events with ids only):
- `roster_set_week(branch, week_start, rows)`: Owner, Admin Pusat, the Manager for a branch in their set. Each staff must hold an active membership with a role assigned to that branch (`staff_branches`) for the shift dates. Shifts outside the branch hours or on a closure date are allowed but flagged? Proposal: refused (`outside_branch_hours`), using T-036 `branch_is_open` for the interval.
- `leave_add(staff, kind, from, to, reason)` and `leave_cancel(id, reason)`: the same roles; the Manager only for staff assigned to a branch in their set.
- Conflict with booked appointments (UX-33): appointments do not exist yet (T-044). The writers take a hook point: T-044 adds the conflict check to `roster_set_week` and `leave_add` when it creates `appointments`. T-043 documents the gap; no fake check.

**Reader (module-level availability, Done-when):**
- `staff_available(staff, branch, from, until)`: true when the interval lies inside one work shift at that branch on that date, outside every break, outside every active leave, and `branch_is_open` holds. T-044 uses it to offer slots.

**UI:** `/jadwal-staf`: branch picker (the active branch, or the first allowed), week navigation, week grid at regular widths and a day list at compact widths, per staff row; edit cells (start/end, break), Simpan Jadwal for the week; a Cuti section (add, cancel). Read-only for roles without the writer.

## Open points for the plan review

1. **Leave scope.** Per person (all branches) vs per branch. Proposal: per person; a doctor on leave is away from every branch.
2. **Shifts outside branch hours.** Refuse (proposed) or allow with a warning (a doctor may come in early to prepare)?
3. **Reader roles** for shifts and leave, and whether leave `kind`/`reason` should be hidden from non-managers (privacy: "sick").
4. **Past edits.** Proposed: shifts and leave whose date has passed (branch time) are kept, never changed.
5. **The appointment-conflict rule** of UX-33 is deferred to T-044, which owns `appointments` (no fake check here).

## Plan review round 1 (Argon; GPT-6.1 Sol 429): REVISE PLAN → adopted

Open points:
1. **Leave is per branch** (`staff_leave.branch_id NOT NULL`, branch-scoped): TASKS Done-when, PR-48, DATA-16 and 19:67 all say "per branch", and a person-wide leave would let one branch's manager block or cancel another branch's days. `staff_available` is evaluated at the target branch, so scenario PR-48 still holds.
2. **Shifts outside branch hours are allowed at save time**; `staff_available` requires `branch_is_open` for the asked interval. Refusing at save time breaks split hours with a break and goes stale when hours or closures change later. The grid shows the branch hours and closures.
3. **Readers:** `staff_shifts` for the 07 "Booking & schedule" roles (owner, admin_pusat, manager, fo_cashier, central_cs, bc, doctor, nurse, therapist; branch-limited by `app_branch_allowed`) plus the staff themselves; support class `config`. `staff_leave` only for owner, admin_pusat, manager (branch-limited) and the staff themselves; support class `personal`. Booking roles get availability and leave date ranges through definer readers without `kind` or reason. `/jadwal-staf` is for Owner, Admin Pusat and Manager (07 "Staff schedules").
4. **The past is kept:** `roster_set_week` only replaces dates ≥ today in branch time and refuses rows dated in the past (`shift_past`); `leave_add` and `leave_cancel` need `from_date` ≥ today; guards enforce both.
5. **Appointment conflicts:** the SQL check is T-044's (it owns `appointments`), but T-043 defines the contract now: `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: appointment_conflict` mapped to the UX-33 blocked copy on `/jadwal-staf`, so T-044 adds only the check.

Findings adopted:
- **P1-1:** no exclusion constraint over a branch-timezone lookup (a fake-IMMUTABLE function corrupts the index when a timezone changes and breaks restore). Overlap of one person's work shifts across branches, break containment and break non-overlap are checked in `roster_set_week` and a `staff_shifts_guard` trigger, serialized by `tenant_actor_lock` (the `staff_access` advisory lock), comparing instants in each branch's timezone over date ±1.
- **P1-2:** `staff_leave` carries `cancel_reason` (3–500 chars, set together with `cancelled_at/by`); free text never goes to `audit_append`.
- **P1-3:** `staff_available(staff, branch, from, until)` (STABLE, definer) returns false, never null, for nulls, an empty interval, no bound staff or a closed branch; requires an active membership on the wall clock and a `staff_branches` row at the branch; evaluates shifts and breaks as instants in the branch timezone (`24:00` stored as `23:59:59.999999` maps to the next midnight; the interval end uses `p_until - 1 µs` as `branch_is_open` does); and no active leave at the branch covering the date.
- **P1-4:** `domain_events` payloads carry ids and lower-case codes only (`events_payload_ok`): `RosterChanged` with `branch_id`, `staff_id`, `leave_id`, `change`. Audit `settings_change` / `branch_roster` or `staff_leave` with reason codes `roster_week_set`, `leave_added`, `leave_cancelled`. Every writer authorizes with `branch_authorize(…, branch, true)` on the row's own branch first (leave_cancel looks up the leave's branch, then authorizes, then locks the row), the 0058 lock order. Guards are `ENABLE ALWAYS` with `BEFORE TRUNCATE`: `staff_shifts_guard` (no update; insert/delete only from today in branch time) and `staff_leave_guard` (no delete; insert from today; update only sets the cancellation once while `from_date` ≥ today).
- **P2-1:** scope expansions to record: `src/components/shell/nav.ts` (`/jadwal-staf` "O AP M"), `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`, `tests/context/stale-context.test.ts`, `tests/isolation/cases/jadwal-staf.cases.ts`, `tests/db/rosters-drift.test.ts`, `tests/ui/jadwal-staf.spec.ts`, plus any role-matrix or operator file the TEN-19 / readers gates require.
- **P2-2:** a batch reader for T-044: `roster_window(branch, from_date, to_date)` (definer, booking roles) returns the branch's shifts and active leave date ranges without `kind` or reason; TS `rosterWindow` and `isStaffAvailable` live in `src/modules/scheduling/rosters/`.

## Plan review round 2 (Argon): APPROVE PLAN

Implementation notes adopted:
- (a) The structural checks of `staff_shifts` (one person's work shifts never overlap across branches, compared as instants in each branch's timezone over dates ±1, `24:00` as the next midnight; a break inside a work shift of the same staff, branch and date; breaks never overlap; no orphan break after a work shift is removed) run in an `AFTER INSERT OR DELETE` statement-level check, after all rows of the statement apply. The `BEFORE` row guard refuses updates and any row dated before today in branch time and takes the tenant's `staff_access` advisory lock first (a no-op re-entry from the writers; keeps the lock order for direct SQL).
- (b) `staff_available` returns false (never null) for nulls, an empty interval or a closed branch (`branch_is_open`), and requires an active membership on the wall clock and a `staff_branches` row at the branch.
- (c) `roster_set_week` refuses a week already past and rows outside the week or before today, deletes only from `greatest(week_start, today)`, and writes one audit row and one `RosterChanged` event per call (payload keys omitted, never null).
- (d) `roster_window` validates the caller with `approval_actor()`, a "Booking & schedule" role and `app_branch_allowed(branch)` (else not found).
- (e) Nothing cut.
