## Sources

- **PR-22 / BILL-13 / B-05.** The booking DP is optional per treatment, default off (catalog `booking_dp_enabled`/`booking_dp_amount`; SET-10 `dp_booking` on/off and `batas_ubah` hours, default 24). What happens to it:
  - applied on arrival;
  - a patient change ≥ cutoff → balance;
  - a no-show, or a patient change < cutoff → forfeited;
  - a clinic cancellation → balance, or a cash refund on the patient's request, without the B-04 exception approval (it is clinic-caused).
- **Scenario PR-22:** a DP of Rp200,000; the patient reschedules 10 h before → forfeited and recorded.
- **Scenario PR-83:** a no-show with a DP → forfeited (plus a CS task, not here).
- **Money scenario 6:**
  - 6a: a change ≥ 24 h → `BalanceMoved` (`dp_transfer`);
  - 6b: < 24 h and 6c: no-show → `BookingDepositForfeited`;
  - 6d: a clinic cancel → balance;
  - 6e: arrival → `BookingDepositApplied` to the treatment line (the remainder paid as usual);
  - the PKP gross variant: a DP of 222,000 = base 200,000 + VAT 22,000; moved to balance gross with the VAT reversed; the balance later pays a taxable line, so VAT is credited once.
- **Money scenario 17:** a clinic cancel with a cash refund (`BookingDepositRefunded`), gross; under PKP the VAT is reversed.
- **Events (18 TD-7):** `BookingDepositReceived`, `BookingDepositApplied`, `BookingDepositForfeited`, `BookingDepositMovedToBalance` (posting via `BalanceMoved`), `BookingDepositRefunded`.
- **DATA-28:** the `booking_deposit` allocation target. **DATA-33:** a refund's source can be a booking deposit (T-078's refunds table; here only the clinic-cancel cash refund).
- **Existing code:**
  - 0062 `appointment_cancel`, `appointment_no_show`;
  - 0067 `appointment_reschedule` (re-created there);
  - `visit_check_in` (0067);
  - T-076: `payments.invoice_id` nullable for top-ups, `payment_allocations.top_up_id`, `balance_top_ups` (the same pattern fits a deposit);
  - T-075: `balance_post` (`dp_transfer` kind).

## Design (proposed)

### 1. Tables (`src/modules/cashier/schema.ts`; migration 0093)

- **`booking_deposits`:**
  - tenant, branch, appointment (unique: one DP per appointment chain), patient (canonical), the catalog version booked;
  - `amount` (gross), `vat_base`/`vat_amount` (the booked item's category, PKP at receipt, through `tax_vat_split`), `vat_recognised_amount`;
  - receipt and payment, state `held | applied | moved_to_balance | forfeited | refunded`, `decided_at/by/as`, the reason code, `applied_line_id`, `command_key`.
  - Insert-only except for one decision transition.
- **`payment_allocations.booking_deposit_id`:** a new target (DATA-28); the check becomes `num_nonnulls(invoice_line_id, top_up_id, booking_deposit_id) = 1`.
- **Rescheduling:** the DP follows the appointment chain. The new appointment of a reschedule ≥ cutoff keeps a held DP: a reschedule ≥ cutoff moves it to balance per BILL-13 (patient change ≥ 24 h → balance), so no follow-through is needed. Open point 1 asks whether "patient change ≥ 24 h" includes a reschedule (kept) or only a cancellation (moved).

### 2. Writers (module `src/modules/cashier/deposits/`)

- **`deposit_receive(key, appointment, p)`.** FO/cashier, a manager, the owner or finance takes the DP for a booked/confirmed appointment whose version has the DP enabled, with SET-10 `dp_booking` on.
  - Methods: cash, EDC, transfer. The port and the balance are open point 2.
  - A KW receipt plus a payment with no invoice (T-076's top-up pattern), allocated to the deposit.
  - VAT is recognised at receipt when PKP and the booked item is taxable (the tax-point guard; PENDING Q-05).
  - Event `BookingDepositReceived`.
- **Effects inside the re-created appointment writers:**
  - `appointment_cancel(p, reason)` gains a cause: patient or clinic (open point 3: a parameter or a separate writer).
    - Patient: ≥ cutoff before start → balance (`dp_transfer`, gross, VAT reversed on the deposit); < cutoff → forfeited.
    - Clinic: balance by default, or a cash refund on request through `deposit_refund_clinic` (no B-04 approval).
  - `appointment_reschedule`: ≥ cutoff → the DP follows the new appointment (open point 1), < cutoff → forfeited.
  - `appointment_no_show` → forfeited.
  - The cutoff is SET-10 `batas_ubah` hours at the branch, measured against the original `starts_at`, at the action's instant.
- **Applied on arrival.** At the Kasir, the visit's invoice for the booked service receives the DP as an allocation from the deposit:
  - `deposit_apply(invoice)`, or automatically at `invoice_issue` when the visit came from an appointment with a held DP;
  - the deposit's money is allocated to the matching line, VAT preserved (no second VAT);
  - `BookingDepositApplied`.
  - Open point 4: an automatic or an explicit apply.
- **Clinic-cause cash refund (scenario 17).** `deposit_refund_clinic(deposit, method)` by FO, a manager or the owner:
  - only for a clinic-caused cancellation;
  - a negative-tender record (a refund row; T-078 builds `refunds` — open point 5: a minimal deposit refund row here, or a DATA-33 `refunds` subset).

### 3. Money facts and VAT (scenarios 6 and 17, PKP variant)

- **Received:** the gross amount; VAT recognised on the deposit when PKP and taxable.
- **Moved to balance:** `balance_post(dp_transfer, gross)`, the deposit's VAT reversed (`vat_reversed_amount/at`). A later use of the balance on a taxable line recognises VAT at payment (T-075), so it is counted once.
- **Forfeited:** the state plus the event only (other income, PENDING A-10; the VAT on a forfeit is gated by Q-05, so the recognised VAT stays as is).
- **Applied:** the deposit's allocation on the line carries its VAT portion, so the line's recognised VAT includes it, and the remainder's payment recognises only the rest.
- **Refunded:** gross, VAT reversed.

## Open points

1. Does a reschedule ≥ cutoff keep the DP on the new appointment, or move it to balance? BILL-13 says "patient change ≥ 24 h → balance". Proposed: keep it on the new appointment. Moving it is the stated rule for a change, though, so the reviewer should decide.
2. Deposit methods: cash, EDC and transfer only, or also the port (QRIS) and the balance?
3. The cancel cause: a parameter on `appointment_cancel` (a signature change, with callers in `src/modules/scheduling`, outside the allowed paths), or a separate `appointment_cancel_by_clinic`?
4. Apply on arrival: automatic at invoice issue, or explicit?
5. The refund record for scenario 17 versus T-078's `refunds` table.
6. Scope: re-creating the appointment writers (0062/0067) is drizzle; the scheduling module's TS callers are outside the allowed paths.

## Plan review r1 (Argon `d2add5d2`, REVISE): every finding checked against the code and adopted

This section supersedes the draft where they differ.

**Open points.**
1. **A patient reschedule ≥ cutoff moves the DP to balance** (`dp_transfer`), the same as a patient cancel ≥ cutoff. BILL-13, PR-22, scenario 6a and DATA-31 all say "patient change ≥ 24 h → balance". The new appointment carries no DP. This is the contract, not an owner choice.
2. **Methods:** cash, EDC and transfer only (SET-14, real, no charge, no invoice). The port is refused (`payment_port`); the balance is not a deposit tender.
3. and 6. **`appointment_cancel(uuid, text)` keeps its signature and stays the patient-caused cancel.** Clinic causes get their own writers:
   - `appointment_cancel_clinic(appointment, reason)`: scenario 6d, the DP to balance;
   - `deposit_refund_clinic(key, deposit, p)`: scenario 17, which cancels the appointment and refunds the DP in cash.

   Nothing changes in `src/modules/scheduling`. The re-created SQL writers are `appointment_cancel`, `appointment_reschedule` and `appointment_no_show`.
4. **Apply on arrival:** automatic inside the re-created `invoice_issue`, for an ordinary visit invoice whose appointment has a held DP and a line for the booked item, plus an idempotent explicit `deposit_apply`.
5. **The scenario 17 refund is recorded on `booking_deposits`:** state `refunded`, method, details, VAT reversed. The `refunds` table (DATA-33) is T-078's.

**Findings.**
- **P1-1:** the clinic decision is atomic with the cancel (above). One terminal state, so there is no double credit.
- **P1-2 (apply).**
  - **One allocation:** the deposit's payment keeps its single allocation (`booking_deposit_id`). Apply sets `applied_line_id` and moves the deposit's recognised VAT onto the line (bounded by the line's own VAT and room).
  - **Re-created:**
    - `kasir_line_paid` adds the applied deposits on the line;
    - `payment_line_room` and `payment_allocations_guard` subtract them;
    - `invoice_void_request` and `invoice_void_sync` treat a line with an applied deposit as paid (`invoice_paid`).
  - **Events:** `BookingDepositApplied`, with no second `PaymentAllocated`.
- **P1-3 (receipt and payment).** `booking_deposits` names its receipt, not its payment (as `balance_top_ups` does). The insert order is receipt → deposit → payment → allocation. The re-created `payments_guard` accepts a payment with no invoice when exactly one of `balance_top_ups` or `booking_deposits` matches it.
- **P1-4 (held appointments).** `deposit_receive` expires holds, then accepts `held`, `booked` and `confirmed` appointments. A held one is confirmed to booked (capability `new_booking`); an expired hold is refused (`hold_expired`).
- **P1-5:** `receipt_get` is re-created to mask a deposit receipt's patient for the auditor.
- **P2-1.**
  - **Roles:** deposits are received by owner, manager, FO/cashier and finance; decided by the appointment writers' roles, including admin pusat and central CS.
  - **VAT gate:** the `vat_on_invoices` gate lock and check at a PKP taxable receipt.
  - **Grants and gates:** column grants without reasons; scope map `tenant-shared`, support `personal`; the guard and no-truncate trigger enabled always.
- **P2-2 (cutoff).**
  - **Snapshot:** `cutoff_hours` is taken from SET-10 at receipt.
  - **Rule:** with `v_at` the action instant, `v_at ≤ starts_at − cutoff` → balance; later → forfeited.
- **P2-3: tests added.**
  - 6a by reschedule and by cancel;
  - the exact boundary and one second after;
  - held → booked, and an expired hold;
  - the PKP 6e (VAT 22,000 transferred, then 143,000 at payment = 165,000 once);
  - the gate closed;
  - a void refused after apply;
  - a cancel racing a check-in, and two receipts;
  - central CS and admin pusat effects;
  - a merge;
  - auditor masking.
- **Scope expansions declared:**
  - `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`;
  - the drift test `tests/db/deposits-drift.test.ts` with its `.expected.json`;
