- `not_eligible` (service eligibility: the performer's role/staff and mode; `supervised` needs a supervising doctor present — open point 4);
- `contraindication_unresolved` (open point 5: a verified intake fact of kind allergy/condition/pregnancy flagged by the doctor against the item, or a catalog contraindication the doctor has not cleared on the examination);
- `consent_missing` / `consent_mismatch` (PR-11, CLIN-022: an item in the plan not in the active consent, qty or dose above the consented, a different catalog version);
- the examination must be signed (T-053) — open point 6.
The gate is a reader used by the treatment-start writer (later, M3 T-0xx); here it is exposed as a function and a TS `assertCanStart` plus a `treatment_start(visit, item)` stub? — open point 7.

**UI** `/persetujuan-tindakan` (doctor list of plans needing consent; detail: generated text, items and doses, signer, pad 320×160 with Ulangi, "Tanda Tangani" (patient signature, not the doctor's PIN); history with superseded versions; blocked state with the start-gate blockers).

### Adopted design (supersedes "Design (proposed)" where they differ)

**Tables (migration 0073; 05 §3.1 tenant keys; clinical class; operator policies; no truncate; staff_access lock first in every guard, ENABLE ALWAYS):**
- `procedure_consents`: id, tenant, branch (the visit's), patient (canonical), visit, examination, plan; `version` ≥ 1 unique per (tenant, plan); state `active` | `superseded` | `withdrawn` (partial unique index: one active per plan); signer `patient` | `guardian` with `guardian_name` (2–120) and `guardian_relation` (`ayah`, `ibu`, `wali`, `lainnya`) set iff guardian; `captured_by`, `captured_as` (`doctor` | `nurse`), `captured_at`; `consent_text` (SQL-generated snapshot) and `content_hash` (sha256 hex of the text); `signature_png` bytea with `signature_sha256` and `signature_size` (68–204800; PNG signature and IHDR checked in SQL; size = octet_length; sha256 = digest); supersession (`superseded_at`, `superseded_by_id`) and withdrawal (`withdrawn_at/by/as`, `withdraw_reason` 3–500) each all-or-none and tied to the state. FKs: visit and examination by (tenant, branch, id); plan by (tenant, id) (a plan may come from another branch's examination).
- `procedure_consent_items`: id, tenant, branch, consent, plan_item, stage, catalog_version, qty, dose_micro, dose_unit — the active plan items at capture, insert-only.
- `contraindication_reviews`: id, tenant, branch, examination, plan_item, decision `cleared` | `blocked`, reason (3–500 when blocked), version, reviewed_by/at; unique per (examination, plan_item); changes only on an open visit (a re-decision raises the version).
- Guards: nothing deleted; consent content, identity and signature frozen; a consent changes once, `active → superseded` or `active → withdrawn`; items insert-only.
- Grants: `signature_png` is excluded from the column grants of `clinic_app` and `clinic_operator`; it is served only by an audited clinical-reader definer. No raw signature bytes in `runCommand` bodies or results (the body carries its sha256).

**Readers (P1-5):** invoker wall-clock `consent_readable(tenant, branch)` — doctor, nurse, therapist, facility head, records officer with the acting role held at the branch now; never BC, FO/cashier, pharmacist or non-clinical roles (they see only the visit's `consent` step).

**Writers (P2-1: `tenant_actor_lock` first, then role, then capability `existing_appointment` except withdrawal, then lookups; audit `sign` / `settings_change`; reason codes only, reason NULL):**
- `procedure_consent_text(visit, plan)` → `{text, content_hash, items}`: deterministic text from the plan's active items (name, stage, qty, dose and unit), each version's `consent_template` (a fixed Indonesian fallback clause when null), its `contraindications` summary and a fixed risk section. Doctor or nurse of the visit's branch; audited clinical read.
- `procedure_consent_capture(visit, plan, signer, guardian, expected_hash, signature)`: doctor or nurse at the visit's branch, open visit, the plan active and of the visit's patient cluster with ≥ 1 active item (`empty_plan`), the examination draft or signed; regenerates the text and refuses when the hash differs (`ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: plan_changed`); guardian required when the patient is under 18 on the branch-local date or registered `newborn_via_guardian` (`guardian_required`; DEC-050); supersedes the active consent; snapshots the active items; visits.consent → done only while open; event `ConsentRecorded` (ids and codes).
- `procedure_consent_withdraw(consent, reason)`: doctor or nurse of the branch; never blocked by the tenant lifecycle (PRIV-1); visits.consent → not_started while open.
- `contraindication_review(examination, plan_item, decision, reason)`: the visit's examining doctor only; open visit; draft or signed examination; event `ContraindicationReviewed`.

**Start gate (P1-1, P1-3):** `treatment_start_check(visit, items uuid[], performer, supervisor default null)` → `{ok, blockers[]}` (every blocker reported, ordered; no writes; audited clinical read). Reader roles: the clinical readers above.
- `examination_unsigned`: no examination for the visit or not signed (open point 6).
- `credential_missing` / `credential_expired` (CLIN-005): `credential_valid(performer, branch, now)` false → expired when a verified STR and branch permit pair of the profession exists and one is past its date, else missing.
- `not_eligible`: the performer holds an active clinical role at the branch now; services resolve the mode from `service_staff_eligibility` (a named staff row over role rows), capped by `delegation_floor.max_mode` for the version's category and the performer's profession; non-service items follow the DEC-065 rule (doctor performs, nurse supervised, therapist none); `assists` or no row → not eligible; `supervised` needs a supervisor ≠ performer who is a doctor at the branch now with a valid credential.
- `contraindication_unresolved`: a requested item with a `blocked` review, or whose version has contraindications and no `cleared` review on the visit's examination.
- `consent_missing` / `consent_mismatch` (PR-11, CLIN-022): no active consent for the plan → missing. Otherwise compare the plan's **active** items with the consent items grouped by (stage, catalog version, dose unit): a requested item removed; an active group absent from the consent (added item or other version); a group's total qty or total dose above the consented; a dose where none was consented; a different unit → mismatch. Equal or lower amounts stay valid.
- TS `checkTreatmentStart` returns the result; `assertCanStart` throws `ERR_CREDENTIAL_INVALID` for credential blockers, `ERR_CONSENT_MISSING` for consent blockers (18:213–214), otherwise `ERR_FORBIDDEN` with the first blocker as reason.
