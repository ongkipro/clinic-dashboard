Alternative: leave the registry href broken until a nav task (worse).
2. **Letter UI place.** 19:92 puts "Surat" under Klinis, reached from UX-8 (Lembar Pemeriksaan toolbar "Buat Surat") and UX-29 (Profil Pasien). Both pages are outside the Allowed Paths. Proposal: the letter pages live at `/templat/surat/baru?visit=` and `/templat/surat/[id]` (inside the Allowed Paths), and the "Buat Surat" entry buttons on UX-8 / UX-29 are a later UI task (recorded). Alternative: a scope expansion into `/pemeriksaan/[visitId]` for one link.
3. **Signing step-up.** Same as T-053: `signLetter(db, key, letterId, StepUp)`; the database checks `two_factor_enabled`; the TOTP producer stays deferred (one producer for exam and letter signing, with the UI task that wires "Tanda Tangani"). The browser check therefore cannot sign through the UI; the PR-74 scenario is proven in the database/module test; the UI shows "Tanda Tangani" disabled with the reason (BlockedState) — or the reviewer asks for the producer here (it would touch `src/auth/server.ts`, outside the Allowed Paths).
4. **Which template types are in scope.** PR-74 names receipts, procedure consent and letters; UX-54 adds legal site pages. Receipts are rendered by the cashier (T-072), consent text today is generated by `consent_text_of` (T-054, from `catalog_versions.consent_template`), legal pages by the public site task. Proposal: the template store holds all four kinds (versioned, immutable once used), but only **letter** templates are consumed in T-058; receipt / consent / legal versions can be saved and previewed, and their consumers switch to the store in their own tasks (recorded, not wired here — changing `consent_text_of` would reopen T-054's hash contract).
5. **Required fields.** SET-32 "required legal and clinical fields cannot be removed". Proposal: each kind has a fixed list of required placeholders enforced by the writer (letter: `{{pasien.nama}}`, `{{pasien.no_rm}}`, `{{tanggal}}`, `{{dokter.nama}}`, `{{dokter.sip}}`, `{{klinik.nama}}`; receipt: clinic name and address, NPWP, number, date, lines, total; consent: patient, procedure, risks, signature; legal: none). Placeholders are a closed set; unknown ones are refused.
6. **Who.** Templates: owner and admin pusat (all kinds), facility head (clinical kinds: consent, letter); others read nothing (FO "read" in 07:65 is for reminder rules; the receipt preview can come with T-072). Letters: create and sign by a doctor of the visit's branch (author = signer, as exam signing); read by doctor, facility head, records officer of the branch (the medical-record readers of 0070); the patient copy (PR-49) includes signed letters — out of scope here unless trivial (record_copy_data is T-053's; recorded).
7. **Correction.** "only corrected by a new version": a signed letter cannot change; `letter_correct(letter, body, reason)` writes version n+1 with the reason, the original kept (same 48 h rule as T-053? Proposal: yes, reuse the rule: direct within 48 h of signing by the author, later through the `record_late_correction` approval — or simpler: letters correct by the author at any time with a reason, as a new signed version). Reviewer to pick; default proposal: **reuse T-053's rule** (consistency of the medical record).

## Design (proposed)

**Data** (migration 0075, `src/modules/documents/schema.ts`):
- `document_templates`: tenant, kind `receipt` | `consent` | `letter` | `legal`, letter_type (`surat_keterangan` | `rujukan` | `resume`, only for letters), name, created_by/as. One row per template.
- `document_template_versions`: template, version (1..n), body (text with `{{…}}` placeholders, ≤ 20 000), paper (`a4` | `80mm` | `58mm`; receipt only may be 58/80), state `active` | `retired` (one active per template), used_at (set on first use → immutable), created_by/as/at. Insert-only except the used_at stamp and retirement on a newer version; "Simpan Versi" on a used version creates a new version (copy "Versi sudah dipakai — buat versi baru").
- `medical_letters`: tenant, branch, visit, patient (canonical), examination? (nullable; a letter can follow a visit without an exam? — proposal: require the visit's examination to exist and be the author's, as record_sign), template_version, letter_type, state `draft` | `signed`, author, signed_at/by/as, content_hash; current body in `medical_letter_versions`.
- `medical_letter_versions`: letter, version, kind `original` | `correction`, body (rendered text: placeholders resolved at sign), content_hash, reason (correction), created_by/as/at. Insert-only.
- Support classes (support_object_kinds, 0045 check: config | personal | clinical | money_total | never): templates and template versions `config` (tenant configuration, no patient data); letters and letter versions `clinical`. Operator policies for each.

**Writers** (actor lock first; capability; audit and ids-only events; through `runCommand`):
- `template_save(template?, kind, letter_type?, name, body, paper)`: new template or new version; required placeholders present, no unknown ones; a used active version is never changed (new version instead).
- `template_retire(template)`: no new letters from it (kept for history).
- `letter_create(visit, template_version)` (doctor of the visit's branch, the visit's examination author or any doctor? proposal: any doctor of the branch with an open or today's visit): draft with the rendered body (placeholders resolved now; editable while draft).
- `letter_save(letter, body)` (draft only, author).
- `letter_sign(letter)` (author, `two_factor_enabled`, active branch): body frozen, hash, version 1, template version marked used, event `LetterSigned`, audit `sign`.
- `letter_correct(letter, body, reason)`: per open point 7.
**Readers:** `template_list(kind?)`, `template_get(template)` (versions, preview data), `letter_list(visit | patient)`, `letter_get(letter)` (body, versions; clinical_read audit). PDF of a signed letter via `records/pdf.ts`'s writer (reused, not copied: import).

**UI** (`src/app/(app)/templat/**`):
- `/templat/dokumen`: list by kind (segments Struk · Persetujuan · Surat · Halaman Legal; facility head sees Persetujuan · Surat), versions, "Buat Templat".
- `/templat/dokumen/[id]`: versions, editor (textarea + placeholder list), Pratinjau (sample data), "Simpan Versi" (the one prominent action), retire.
- `/templat/surat/baru?visit=`: pick template, edit draft; `/templat/surat/[id]`: draft edit, "Tanda Tangani" (per open point 3), signed view with versions and correction form, PDF download.

**Tests** (`tests/documents/`): PR-74 scenario; template versioning and immutability once used; required/unknown placeholders; roles; letter draft → sign → frozen; correction as a new version; guards; events/audit content-free; cross-tenant; isolation cases; stale context; rls allow-list; authority clock (scope expansions as in T-057).

**Scope expansions (to declare):** `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`, `tests/context/stale-context.test.ts`, `tests/isolation/cases/templat.cases.ts`, `tests/ui/templat.spec.ts`, `src/modules/settings/registry.ts` + `src/components/shell/nav.ts` (open point 1), `DECISIONS.md` (DEC-067 for the defaults).
