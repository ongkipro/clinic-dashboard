## Why
`tests/cashier/deposits.test.ts` failed test-full twice on the same case, line and error: T-094 close run 2 (ended 23:37:42 WIB) and T-099
close run 1 (23:29–23:38 WIB) — "PR-22: a DP of Rp200,000; the patient reschedules 10 h before → forfeited and recorded",
`ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT` from `reschedule` (appointments.ts:69 via deposits.test.ts:249).
- The fixture branch is open and the doctor rostered 00:00–23:45 every day (:92-102); the service lasts 30 min (:85-87). `branch_is_open`
  (0058) needs the interval on one local date with its last instant before closing, so the latest start is 23:15.
- `quarter(ms) = ceil((now + ms) / 15 min) × 15 min` (:126). PR-22 reschedules to `quarter(72 h)` (:249): for now in 23:15:01–23:45:00 WIB
  the target is 23:30 or 23:45 and ends after 23:45. 6a reschedules to `quarter(80 h)` (:263): the same for 15:15:01–15:45:00 WIB.
  Arithmetic over every second of a day: /data/tmp/clinic-dashboard/dep-flake/arith.mjs (1800 s each, exactly those windows).
- Hypothesis to confirm on a database before any fix: the writer refuses such a target with `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: slot_taken`
  (booking_pick returns no row when branch_is_open is false; 0093_deposits.sql:747-749).

## Change (tests only; no product code, no branch hours widened)
1. Reproduce first: a deposits.test.ts case reschedules to a fixed target, local 23:30 three days out, and asserts the refusal reason;
   then the corrected target (23:15) is accepted. Clock-independent (a fixed target; the database clock is not faked).
2. `tests/support/dates.ts`: `withinHours(t)` — the target moved back by whole quarter hours so a 30-minute slot ends by 23:45 on the same
   local date; unchanged otherwise. Native date arithmetic; no scheduling abstraction.
3. `deposits.test.ts`: the two reschedule targets only (:249 `quarter(72 * H)`, :263 `quarter(80 * H)`) wrapped in `withinHours`; the
   import. Unchanged: every original appointment start, every money, cutoff, decision and event assertion (the DP decision depends on the
   original start, not the target), the branch hours and rosters, every other call.
4. `tests/support/dates.test.ts`: `withinHours` at the window times (explicit `now`, no global fake clock) and its invariants (same local
   day, quarter-aligned, never later than the input, ends by 23:45).
