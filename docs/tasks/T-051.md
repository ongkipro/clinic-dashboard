## Design (proposed)

**Scope split.** Of UX-7's four steps, T-051 builds **step 1 (Intake & Foto) and "Kirim ke Dokter"**. Steps 2–4 belong to later tasks: Dokter (T-052 Lembar Pemeriksaan), Diskusi Pilihan and Konfirmasi (the plan items, prices, balance and purchase agreement: M3/M4 tasks). The page shows those steps in the header as not yet available ("Menunggu dokter"), not as dead buttons.

**Data** (migration 0069; module `src/modules/clinical`, schema in `src/modules/clinical/schema.ts`):
- `intakes`: tenant, branch, visit (unique per visit; FK), patient, `state` (`draft`, `sent`), `complaint` (text 3–1000), `history` (jsonb of answered facts), `author_id` (the BC), `author_role`, `created_at`, `sent_at`, `sent_by`. No diagnosis column, no score column.
- `intake_facts`: one row per answered fact (`kind`: `allergy`, `medication`, `pregnancy`, `condition`, `skincare`, `prior_treatment`, `note`), `value` text (≤ 500), `provenance` (`patient_reported`, `bc_captured_unverified`), `changed` (true when new or different from the latest prior fact of the same kind — CLIN-001), `prior_fact_id` (the history row it repeats, if unchanged), visit, patient, author. Facts are never edited after `sent`; corrections before sending replace the draft fact.
- `skin_observations`: per intake, `chips` (text[] from a fixed vocabulary: `kering`, `berminyak`, `kombinasi`, `kemerahan`, `jerawat_aktif`, `bekas_jerawat`, `flek`, `pori_besar`, `garis_halus`, `sensitif`, `iritasi`, `lainnya`), `note` text (≤ 1000). **No grade, score or type column**; a check rejects any chip outside the vocabulary.
- Unverified allergy caution (CLIN-002): an `allergy` fact with `bc_captured_unverified` provenance on a sent intake surfaces as a caution through a definer reader `patient_cautions(patient)` for doctor, nurse, pharmacist (and facility head), returning kind, the short value and "belum diverifikasi" provenance — nothing else of the intake. T-052 (doctor verification) moves it to verified.
- RLS: intakes, facts and observations are **clinical**: read by `bc` (own branch, the visit's branch), `doctor`, `facility_head`, `records_officer`, `nurse`, `therapist` for allowed branches; never finance, marketing, FO, CS, purchasing, auditor. Support class `clinical` if it exists in `support_object_kinds`, else `never` (vendor-authority 2.8: the operator never reads clinical content without its gate). Scope map rows.

**Writers** (definer, `tenant_actor_lock`, capability `existing_appointment`, ids-only events, content-free audit):
- `intake_open(visit)`: BC (assigned to the visit's branch); creates the draft intake (idempotent per visit), sets `visits.intake = in_progress`; pre-fills the latest prior facts as history (CLIN-001) — returned for display, not copied as today's facts.
- `intake_save(intake, payload)`: BC author only, draft only. Payload: `complaint`, `facts[]` ({kind, value} — unchanged facts are sent as `{kind, same_as: prior_fact_id}`), `observation` ({chips[], note}). **Rejects any key outside the schema** with `ERR_INVALID_INPUT: score_field` when the key looks like a score, grade or Fitzpatrick (`score`, `grade`, `fitzpatrick`, `skin_type`, `tipe_kulit`, `nilai`, any numeric value in observation) — scenario PR-9 — and audits the refused attempt (content-free).
- `intake_send(intake)`: "Kirim ke Dokter": BC author; requires a complaint; state `sent`, `visits.intake = done`, event `IntakeSent` (ids only), notification to the doctor role at the branch (`intake_ready`, system-only).
- **CLIN-003:** the BC has no writer for diagnosis or signing; any diagnosis/sign function (T-052/T-053) refuses `bc`. In T-051: a test that the BC role cannot call any clinical-record writer that exists, cannot write `intakes` directly (no grants), and that `intake_save` refuses diagnosis-like keys (`diagnosis`, `diagnosa`, `icd10`) with an audited refusal.

**Photos.** T-055 owns private media (object storage, short tokens, compare). T-051 does **not** store image bytes. Proposal: the intake step shows "Foto" as a step that will be enabled with T-055 (blocked state with the reason), and the intake records `photo_status` (`pending_media`) — or T-051 is allowed a minimal reference table `intake_photos` (object key only) without upload. Open point 1.

**UI** (`/pendampingan`, `/pendampingan/[visitId]`): the BC's list of today's visits at their branch (from `visits_today`, intake state); the flow page with the step header, intake form (complaint, facts with "Sama seperti kunjungan lalu" / "Berubah" per prior fact, allergy flagged), Observasi Kulit chips (44 px+, 52 px rows on iPad widths) and note, "Kirim ke Dokter" (one prominent action); history column at ≥ 1180, "Riwayat Singkat" sheet at compact.

- **P1-3 RLS:** the three tables are readable by `bc, doctor, facility_head, records_officer` only, through an invoker `intake_readable(tenant, branch)` that also checks, on the wall clock, the caller's active membership and a `staff_branches` row for the acting role at the branch (never null); nurse, therapist, pharmacist get the caution reader only.
