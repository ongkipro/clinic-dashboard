## Gap (verified on disk at `1e54042a`)
VAT on an invoice line was recognised only by money: `kasir_record_payment` (portion = round(vat × paid ÷ total) − recognised),
`receivable_convert` (S2), `deposit_apply_do` (S6). No delivery writer set `invoice_lines.vat_recognised_*`, so a taxable line delivered
before payment had its VAT dated at payment or conversion — late (T-104 DEC-115 (8), known limit). Where delivery precedes payment:
1. a released addition (`addition_release`, Lepas Sebelum Bayar) performed while its line is unpaid, or before its line exists;
2. a consultation fee: the service is performed before the fee is charged (11 :119);
3. a visit product handed before any invoice (`dispense_hand_over`: the visit step blocks only `in_progress` / `blocked`).
An ordinary single treatment cannot start while the initial bill owes (`treatment_payment_unpaid`): no gap.

## Decisions (Panel 2, 2026-11-18)
- **Q1** delivered share: target = round half up (vat_amount × delivered ÷ line quantity) — the payment writer's rounding — and d =
  greatest(0, target − vat_recognised_amount) (monotone).
- **Q2** consultation: the charge instant (`consultation_invoice_issue` `p_at`), share 1.
- **Q3** addition performed before billing: recognised at the line's issue (`invoice_issue`, addition path) with `vat_recognised_at` =
  the execution's `finished_at` (the true tax point) and the `vat_events` row effective at `greatest(finished_at, cutover_at)` so the T-104
  reader (events from the cutover on) never drops it.
- **Q4** visit product handed before any invoice: **out of scope (stated limit)**. A visit dispensing stores `selection_item_id` /
  `prescription_item_id` and never an `invoice_line_id` (0087 :104–105: exactly one of the three; `invoice_line_id IS NULL` for visit rows),
  and `invoice_lines` carries no selection or prescription key, so the delivered line is not identified by a stored key. Counter sales
  already require full payment before hand-over (`dispensing_blocker`). Such a visit product's VAT stays recognised at payment (tested).
  `dispense_hand_over` is not changed.
- **Q5** bookkeeping: this file, a `TASKS.md` hygiene row, DEC-116; scope expansions recorded before editing.
- **S7** (request 2, option a): an approved void reverses the voided lines' recognised, not-yet-reversed VAT in `invoice_void_sync`:
  `vat_reversed_amount = vat_recognised_amount`, `vat_reversed_at = coalesce(…, voided_at)` (`invoice_lines_t076_check`), and a
  `vat_events` row (`line`, −that amount, cause `line_void`, effective `greatest(voided_at, cutover)`, source = the approval). Without it
  the supported void-then-rebill path (`tests/cashier/additions.test.ts:296`) counted the VAT twice and stranded −VAT in
  paid-not-delivered.
- **S8** (request 2, option a): `vat_line_deliver` skips a line while its manual discount is pending; `discount_sync`, once the decision
  settles (approved, rejected, cancelled), lets an already-performed addition line recognise its final VAT at once (tax point the
  execution's `finished_at`, event clamped to the cutover as Q3).

## What is built (migration `0122_vat_delivery`; DEC-116)
- `vat_line_deliver(tenant, line, delivered, of, at, source)` (not granted; not a definer): a line of an issued PKP-at-issue invoice with
  VAT (`vat_amount > 0` implies the `vat_on_invoices` gate was open at issue and the item taxable), never while its manual discount waits
  for a decision (`discount_pending`: the decision rewrites `vat_amount` and refuses a line with recognised VAT), locks the line, raises
  `vat_recognised_amount` by d, keeps the earlier tax point (`vat_recognised_at = coalesce(…, at)`) and writes one `vat_events` row
  (object `line`, +d, cause `line_delivery`, effective at `greatest(at, cutover)`). Returns d.
- Call sites, each ONE marked block (`-- HYG-023 A-12 >> … <<`) added to the latest body, nothing else changed (drift-pinned):
  `treatment_finish` (an addition counted, not on a package session, whose line is issued: share = plan-item qty ÷ line quantity, at
  `finished_at`); `invoice_issue` (addition path: an addition already performed — its first counted execution — at its `finished_at`);
  `consultation_invoice_issue` (the fee line, share 1, at `p_at`; also the re-charge line); `discount_sync` (a decided discount on a
  performed addition line, at `finished_at` — S8).
- `invoice_void_sync` (S7): one marked block reverses the voided lines' recognised VAT with a `line_void` event; the re-billed line then
  recognises it again (Q3), so the VAT counts once.
- `vat_events_check` cause list + `line_delivery`, `line_void` (`VAT_EVENT_CAUSES`); the Kewajiban page labels the terms "PPN saat
  layanan diberikan (sebelum bayar)" and "Pembalikan PPN invoice dibatalkan". The T-104 reader needs no change: the line VAT object already feeds the VAT ledger and paid-not-delivered's VAT
  component.
- Effect: a later payment recognises only the remainder (`kasir_record_payment` portion 0 on a delivered line); a receivable conversion
  records 0 (S2 unchanged); a refund or interruption reverses from the recognised amount as before.

- Locks: `treatment_finish` and the addition path of `invoice_issue` hold the visit lock first (existing order visit → invoice → line);
  the helper locks the line last (`FOR UPDATE OF x`). The payment writer takes the same visit → invoice → line order.
- Idempotency: one execution per plan item; a retried finish is refused (`finished`); a second issue of the same addition is refused
  (`addition_already_invoiced`); the helper is monotone (d ≤ 0 → no write).
- Guards: `invoice_lines_guard` allows a monotone rise of `vat_recognised_amount` and a first `vat_recognised_at`.
- RLS: no new table; `vat_events` keeps its T-104 policies; the helper is not granted (tested: permission denied); cross-tenant read of
  `vat_events` by another tenant's cashier returns nothing (tested).
- Interacting writers (found in the self-audit, decided by Panel 2 S7/S8): a void after delivery recognition (reversed, `line_void`); a
  pending manual discount (deferred, recognised at the decision). The void block locks the voided invoice's lines in id order under the
  invoice lock the trigger already holds.
