## Design (proposed)

**Data** (migration 0061):
- `appointments`: tenant, branch, patient (composite FKs), `catalog_version_id` (the service version used; composite FK; marked through `catalog_version_reference`), `starts_at`, `ends_at` (tstzrange source: the slot = prep + treatment + buffer from the version), `performer_staff_id` (the doctor / therapist / nurse who performs), `state` (`held`, `booked`; later states belong to T-045), `hold_until` (for `held`), `channel` (`walk_in`, `phone`, `whatsapp`, `staff`, `online`), `source` (short code, e.g. `ig-ad`; `[a-z0-9_-]{1,40}`, optional), `campaign` (same format, optional), `note` (≤ 500), `created_by`, `created_as`, `created_at`.
- `resource_reservations`: tenant, branch, appointment, `kind` (`staff`, `room`, `device`), `resource_id` (staff, room or device id of that branch), `during` tstzrange, `active` boolean (false once the appointment is cancelled / released: T-045). Exclusion constraint `EXCLUDE USING gist (tenant_id WITH =, kind WITH =, resource_id WITH =, during WITH &&) WHERE (active)` (btree_gist). Staff reservations include every branch (a person is in one place); room and device ids are branch-unique anyway.
- No patient double booking rule (a patient may book two services back to back); a same-patient overlap is refused (`patient_overlap`).
- RLS: appointments and reservations readable by the 07 booking roles at allowed branches (Central CS tenant-wide); `scope_map` branch-scoped; support classes `personal` (appointments name patients) and `config`? → `personal` for reservations too (they reference appointments). Operator policies (TEN-19).

**Slot finding** (`booking_slots(branch, version, date, staff?)`, definer, booking roles at an allowed branch):
- The service must be offered at the branch (`branch_services`) and active; the duration is prep + treatment + buffer.
- Candidate starts every 15 minutes inside the branch's open intervals of that date (`branch_is_open(branch, start, end)` for the whole duration).
- Staff: eligible by the version's eligibility (role or named staff, mode `performs` or `supervised`), on the branch roster, and `staff_available(staff, branch, start, end)`; not reserved in that range (any branch). A named staff restricts to that person.
- Room: one active room at the branch of each required room kind, not reserved. Device: one active device of each required device type, not reserved. A required resource missing at the branch → no slot (scenario PR-88).
- Returns up to N slots with the staff, room and device that would be taken (deterministic: earliest, then least-loaded staff by id order).

**Booking** (`booking_create(branch, patient, version, start, staff?, channel, source, campaign, note, hold)`, definer, under `tenant_actor_lock`):
- Re-runs the slot rule for that exact start under the lock and picks resources; inserts the appointment and one reservation per resource taken in one transaction; the exclusion constraint is the final guard (a concurrent writer gets `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: slot_taken`).
- `hold = true` creates `state = held` with `hold_until = now + SET-17` (the DP flow, M4); `booking_confirm(appointment)` turns a live hold into `booked`; an expired hold no longer blocks (reservations of an expired hold are treated as inactive by the slot reader and released by `booking_release_expired()` / at the next write).
- Calls `catalog_version_reference(version)`; audit `appointment_booked`; event `AppointmentBooked` (ids and codes only).
- Through `runCommand` with the caller's idempotency key (TS wrapper).
- Roles: FO/cashier, Central CS, Manager, BC (07 ◐: own patients?) → proposed FO, CS, Manager, Owner, Admin Pusat; BC and clinicians read only.
- Channel `online` is only for the public flow (later task) and requires `online_bookable`, SET-16 horizon and cutoff; staff channels ignore SET-16.

## Open points for the plan review

1. **Expired holds:** treat as inactive at read time (a `hold_until > now()` predicate cannot live in an exclusion constraint's WHERE) — so the constraint covers only `booked` reservations plus live holds marked active, and a release step deactivates expired holds before each write under the tenant lock. Alternative: reservations of holds expire by a job only.
2. **Writer roles:** FO/cashier, Central CS, Manager, Owner, Admin Pusat; not BC/doctor/nurse/therapist (07 ◐ "own"?).
3. **Slot granularity:** 15 minutes fixed (no setting exists; PR-38 forbids adding one without a decision).
4. **Staff reservation across branches:** reserve the person globally (one place at a time) — the constraint keys on (tenant, kind, resource_id) without branch.
5. **Supervised/assists eligibility:** only `performs` and `supervised` staff can be the performer; an `assists` resource is not reserved in T-044 (no requirement names a second person).
6. **Online channel:** the public flow is a later task; T-044 stores `channel` and validates SET-16 only when `channel = online`.

## Plan review round 1 (Argon; GPT-6.1 Sol 429): REVISE PLAN → adopted

Open points:
1. **Expired holds:** the exclusion constraint keeps `WHERE (active)` (a `clock_timestamp()` predicate cannot be in an index). Readers treat a reservation as blocking when it is active and its appointment is booked or a live hold. An internal `booking_expire_holds(tenant)` deactivates expired holds' reservations at the start of every booking and schedule writer, after `tenant_actor_lock`; no job.
2. **Roles:** writers Owner, Admin Pusat, Manager, FO/cashier, Central CS (19:63); the slot reader also for BC, doctor, nurse, therapist.
3. **15-minute grid,** fixed; the reader returns every valid start of the day (≤ 96).
4. **Staff reserved across branches:** the constraint keys on (tenant, kind, resource) without branch; staff checks look at every branch, rooms and devices at the branch.
5. **Performers:** `performs` and `supervised` only; no `assists` reservation (DEC-047 (3)).
6. **Online channel:** `online_bookable` and SET-16 (branch) only when `channel = online`; staff channels need a start not in the past and within 366 days.

Findings adopted:
- **P1-1:** the UX-33 appointment-conflict check (DEC-053 left it here): `roster_set_week` and `leave_add` are re-created (drift-tracked) to release expired holds first and refuse with `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT: appointment_conflict` when a live appointment of a listed staff member would no longer be served (after the shift change) or falls in the new leave.
- **P1-2:** event payloads carry ids and codes only (`appointment_id`, `branch_id`, `patient_id`, `catalog_version_id`, `performer_staff_id`, `channel`); source and campaign stay on the row. `AppointmentBooked` when an appointment becomes booked (create without hold, or confirm). Audit `settings_change` / `appointment` with `appointment_held`, `appointment_booked`, `appointment_confirmed`.
- **P1-3:** `booking_confirm` (role first; release expired holds; lock the row; branch scope; an expired hold → `hold_expired`, already booked → `already_booked`). Guards, `ENABLE ALWAYS` with truncate guards: `appointments_guard` (never deleted; inserted held or booked with `hold_until` set exactly for held; only `state` held → booked with `hold_until` cleared changes in T-044) and `resource_reservations_guard` (never deleted; inserted active with the appointment's range and a resource of that branch, or the performer; only `active` true → false changes).
- **P1-4:** `tenant_capability('new_booking')` for create and confirm; `assertProductionGoLive` in the TS wrappers.
- **P1-5:** eligibility at the target branch: a role row needs a `staff_branches` row with that role at the branch (and no named row demoting the person to `assists`); a named row needs a clinical role at the branch and the `delegation_floor` for the category; the version active and a service.
- **P2-1:** scope expansions: `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`, `tests/db/booking-drift.test.ts`; the drift capture excludes extension-owned functions (`btree_gist`).
- **P2-2:** database errors are mapped outside `runCommand` (the T-038 lesson); conflicts are retryable and leave no record.
- **P2-3:** `appointments` has `UNIQUE (tenant_id, branch_id, id)` and reservations reference `(tenant_id, branch_id, appointment_id)`; `booking_create` checks role, lifecycle, branch scope, the patient and the version in the caller's tenant before any availability check.
- **P3-1:** all valid starts of the day. **P3-2:** staff ordered by that day's live reservation count, then id; rooms and devices by id; the same order in the reader and the writer.

## Plan review round 2 (Argon): APPROVE PLAN

Implementation notes adopted:
- (a) Appointments that count for the UX-33 conflict: live (booked, or held with `hold_until` in the future), at the branch, of an affected staff member, whose local date is in the edited window (`greatest(week, today) .. week + 6`; `from .. to` for leave), not yet ended. `roster_set_week` releases expired holds, replaces the shifts, then requires each such appointment inside one work shift of its performer on that date and outside every break (shifts, not `staff_available`, so later hour changes on untouched days do not raise false conflicts); else `appointment_conflict` rolls the save back. The drift test expects exactly `roster_set_week` and `leave_add` re-created and ignores extension-owned functions.
- (b) Both guards take the tenant's `staff_access` lock first, as `staff_shifts_guard`.
- (c) `resource_reservations_guard` checks the branch and the active room or device only on insert; an update may only set `active` true → false (so an expired hold is always releasable).
- `appointments` keeps `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)` beside `UNIQUE (tenant_id, branch_id, id)`; reservations have `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)`.
