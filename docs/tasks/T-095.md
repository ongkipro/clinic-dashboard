T-093 pattern). `reminder_scan(p_now)` (unbound; refuses a bound connection): for appointments booked / confirmed, starting within the next 2
days, in the branch timezone:
- h1: due at (day − 1) + h1; enqueued only while the branch-local date is day − 1;
- same_day: due at starts_at − sebelum h; if that falls before 07:00 or on the previous day → 07:00 that day;

silently and counted in the scan result), command key `reminder:<appointment>:<kind>`, plus an appointment_reminders row. A cancelled /
rescheduled / no-show / arrived appointment gets none. Budget, consent and suspension come from T-094's claim unchanged.
