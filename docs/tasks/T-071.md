## Design (proposed)

Migration `0086_tax_invoice`. Module `src/modules/tax/` (tax profile, policy, monitor). Module `src/modules/cashier/invoice/` (the invoice header and lines with their snapshots; the cashier writers come in T-072).

### 1. Tax profile and history (A-07, SET-39)

- `tenants` keeps the current values: `npwp`, `is_pkp`, `pkp_effective_from`.
- A new insert-only table `tax_profile_changes` records every change: `tenant_id`, `npwp`, `is_pkp`, `pkp_effective_from`, `changed_by`, `changed_as`, `changed_at`, `reason`, `command_key`.
- Writer `tax_profile_set(p jsonb)`, owner only (SET-39):
  - It sets the NPWP (15 or 16 digits) and the PKP flag with its effective date.
  - **Becoming PKP** requires the gate `vat_on_invoices` to be open, meaning the tax advice is approved. It runs `feature_gate_lock` and refuses otherwise with `ERR_GATE_CLOSED`. This is scenario "PR-24 VAT gated by tax advice". The PRD says "finance enables VAT", while SET-39 makes the owner the changer. **Open point 1.**
  - **The effective date** may not be earlier than the tenant's latest issued invoice date. That keeps "invoices before the date are unchanged" true: a PKP date can never reach back over issued invoices. It may be up to 366 days ahead.
  - **Ending PKP** (`is_pkp` false) needs an end date and the same lock. **Open point 2:** whether ending PKP is in scope at all. The owner's fact may be wrong; the correction keeps history.
  - It emits `TaxProfileChanged {tenant_id, change_id}` (ids only) and an audit entry with reason `tax_profile_changed`.
- The PKP state at an instant is read through `tax_pkp_at(tenant, at timestamptz) → boolean`. It is taken from the change history, so history decides, not today's flag. The tenant's timezone decides which local date the effective date covers.

### 2. VAT policy (pure functions; unit-checked)

`src/modules/tax/policy.ts`, mirrored in SQL as `tax_vat_split(price bigint, category text, pkp boolean) → (base, vat)` so that the database snapshots exactly what the module computes.

- **Taxable** means `taxable_service` or `taxable_goods`. `exempt_medical` and `non_object` never carry VAT.
- **For a PKP, taxable line** with a VAT-inclusive price P: base = round(P × 100 / 111), and VAT = P − base. That equals 12 % × (11/12 × base). Rounding is half-up to the rupiah (SET-39), so base + VAT = P always.
  - The fixture's multiples of 111 give exact results.
  - **PENDING (Q-05):** the adviser's rule for the other prices, recorded as the "rounding" PENDING item in T-070.
  - If the adviser rules otherwise, this is the one function that changes, behind the same snapshot columns.
- **Tax point (A-12).** `tax_point(paid_at, delivered_at)` is the earlier of the two, and VAT is due only if the tenant is PKP at that instant. The policy keeps the not-yet-recognised part per line (`vat_recognised_amount`).
- **PKP boundary.** A line issued before the PKP date carries no VAT and keeps that snapshot. A line issued on or after the date carries VAT.
  - Fixture scenario 16 (package paid before the PKP date, redeemed after) is the packages task's. Its reading is **PENDING (Q-05)**.
  - This task provides the primitive `tax_pkp_at` and the unit checks for both readings, so the package task can follow the adviser.
- **Gate.** VAT is computed only while the tenant is PKP at the line's time **and** the gate `vat_on_invoices` is open. If evidence is revoked later, new lines carry no VAT. **Open point 3:** the alternative is to refuse issuing the line instead. Proposed: refuse, because issuing a PKP taxable line without VAT would produce a wrong faktur.

### 3. Invoice foundation (DATA-27)

The tables come in this task; the cashier writers (issue, pay, void) are T-072.

- `invoices`:
  - `tenant_id`, `branch_id`, `sold_branch_id`, `patient_id` (null for a counter sale; T-072);
  - `visit_id` (nullable), `number` (per branch per month, T-072 sets it), `state` (draft → issued → void);
  - `issued_at`, `issued_by`, `is_pkp_at_issue`, `command_key`.
- `invoice_lines`:
  - `invoice_id`, `item_id`, `catalog_version_id`, `quantity` (integer), `unit_price` (frozen, VAT-inclusive, integer rupiah);
  - `discount` (rupiah, T-080), `line_total` = unit_price × quantity − discount;
  - `tax_category`, `tax_basis` (snapshot), `vat_base`, `vat_amount` (computed at issue);
  - `vat_recognised_amount` (0..vat_amount), `vat_recognised_at`, `tax_document_ref`.
- **Freezing.** One writer, `invoice_line_snapshot` (internal, used by T-072), reads:
  - `catalog_branch_price(item, branch, issued_at)`;
  - the active version's category and basis;
  - `tax_pkp_at(issued_at)` and the gate;

  and writes them once. Guards make the frozen columns immutable after issue (ENABLE ALWAYS). Lines are never deleted, and a void is a state change (T-072). This satisfies PR-42's "keeps it after later price changes".
- **To prove PR-24 before T-072 exists,** this task adds a minimal internal writer pair `invoice_open` / `invoice_issue`, with no payment, allocation, receipt or UI. T-072 extends them with the full Kasir rules.
  - **Open point 4:** whether these minimal writers belong in T-071 at all (the contract names `src/modules/cashier/invoice/**`), or whether PR-24 should be proven only through `invoice_line_snapshot` called by a test harness. Proposed: the minimal writers, with grants to `clinic_app` withheld until T-072. That way PR-24 runs through a real path, and no unfinished cashier surface is exposed.

### 4. PKP turnover monitor (A-07, PR-24)

- `tax_turnover_12m(tenant) → bigint` sums `line_total` of issued, not-void invoice lines over the trailing 12 months, by issue date in the tenant's timezone.
- **Open point 5:** whether turnover means gross VAT-inclusive invoice value, or performance (revenue, M8). Proposed: issued invoice value for now. It is the PR-24 warning only, not a tax filing, and it gets the basis from the tax adviser later.
- Reader `tax_profile_get()` returns the profile, the PKP history, the gate state, the 12-month turnover, and a `warning` level:
  - `none` below Rp4.0 billion;
  - `near` from Rp4.0 billion;
  - `over` at Rp4.8 billion or above.

  The 4.0 threshold is a fixed display threshold, not a setting (no new setting, AGENTS.md). **Open point 6:** whether the warning instead fires only at Rp4.8 billion.
- A daily job is not needed for a warning that can be read when the Pajak page or Ringkasan loads.

- Readers of the tax profile: owner, finance, admin pusat, auditor. Writer: owner (SET-39).
- Invoice tables are read by the cashier roles in T-072. In this task they are readable by owner, finance and auditor only.
- Cross-tenant negative tests come for the new definers; route isolation cases come with T-072's pages.
- Mutation tests cover the policy, the gate, the PKP boundary, the frozen-column guards and the turnover window.

## Plan review r1 (Argon `8142b2c4`, REVISE): every finding checked against the code and adopted

Report: `/data/tmp/clinic-dashboard/t071/plan-argon-8142b2c4.md`.

The facts below were checked in the code: there is no `tenants.timezone` (only `branches.timezone`); `invoices` is a placeholder name in `rls.test.ts:282`, `migrations.test.ts:160` and `support.test.ts:619`; `tenant_capability('cashier')` exists (0038); `patient_is_merged` (0067) and `catalog_version_reference` (0054) exist.

These decisions supersede the design above where the two differ.

- **P1-1, timezone.** The local date always comes from a branch, per DATA-7. A new helper `tax_local_date(tenant, at, branch)` uses the branch's timezone. Without a branch it uses the tenant's earliest branch, and `Asia/Jakarta` when there is none. Wherever an invoice is involved, the invoice's own branch decides: in `tax_pkp_at(tenant, at, branch)`, in the latest-issued-date rule and in the turnover window.
- **P1-2, PKP changes cannot reach issued invoices.**
  - Changing only the NPWP is always allowed.
  - Any change to `is_pkp` or `pkp_effective_from` needs the new effective date to be strictly after the latest issued or void invoice's local date. It is refused with `ERR_VERSION_CONFLICT` once any invoice has been issued in the current PKP era (on or after the current effective date). That includes ending PKP.
  - With no issued invoice, any date up to today + 366 days is allowed.
  - `invoice_issue` always stamps `clock_timestamp()`. There is no backdating parameter in a money writer, and the repository has no test clock override. Scenario PR-24 is proven on the real path:
    1. Invoice 1 is issued while the tenant is not PKP.
    2. The owner sets PKP from today's local date. The "strictly after the latest issued date" rule would refuse that, because invoice 1's date is also today. So the test first moves invoice 1's `issued_at` back one day on the owner connection, a documented stand-in for "issued yesterday": the guard trigger is disabled, the row is updated, and the trigger is enabled again, the pattern the T-063 costing tests use. Then the PKP switch from today is accepted.
    3. Invoice 2 is issued today and carries VAT.
    4. Invoice 1 is unchanged.
    
    The boundary itself is also unit-checked through `tax_pkp_at` at instants on either side of the effective date in two branch timezones.
- **P1-3, vendor support and placeholders.** `support_object_kinds` gets `tax_profile_changes` → config, `invoices` and `invoice_lines` → personal, with `clinic_operator` read policies and grants (TEN-14, TEN-19). The placeholder `invoices` in the three tests is renamed to `fixture_invoices`. Scope expansions recorded:
  - `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`
  - `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`
  - `tests/db/migrations.test.ts`
  - `tests/vendor/support.test.ts`
  - `tests/db/tax-drift.test.ts`
  - `tests/isolation/cases/*` (only if the isolation gate requires a case for a granted definer without a route)
- **P1-4, grants.** `invoice_open` and `invoice_issue` are granted to `clinic_app` now (no route or action exposes them before T-072). They are added to `ALLOWED_DEFINER_FUNCTIONS` and to the authority-clock cases. `invoice_line_snapshot`, `tax_pkp_at`, `tax_turnover_12m`, `tax_vat_split` and `tax_local_date` stay internal: revoked from PUBLIC and not granted.
- **P2-5, lock order.** Every app-callable definer begins with `tenant_actor_lock()`, then checks the role and the capability, then resolves the branch, and only then reads anything.
  - `tax_profile_set`: owner only. It locks `tenants FOR UPDATE`, then runs `feature_gate_lock('vat_on_invoices')` / `feature_gate_open` inside SQL. The TypeScript side never calls `assertFeatureGate` first, so the evidence lock is never taken before `staff_access`.
  - `tax_profile_get`: owner, finance, admin pusat, auditor.
  - `invoice_open` / `invoice_issue`: owner, manager, fo_cashier, finance, with `tenant_capability('cashier')`. A Level 3 role uses its active branch; a Level 2 role names a branch of the tenant.
  - No app-callable definer takes a tenant id as a parameter.
- **P2-6, data model.**
  - VAT is computed in integers: base = (price × 100 + 55) / 111 with integer division, so it rounds half up and base + VAT = price.
  - `invoice_lines` carries `tenant_id` and `branch_id`, with composite foreign keys and these checks:
    - quantity between 1 and 10,000;
    - unit price between 0 and 1e12;
    - discount between 0 and unit price × quantity;
    - line total = unit price × quantity − discount;
    - VAT base ≥ 0, VAT ≥ 0, VAT base + VAT = line total;
    - exempt or non-object lines have VAT 0;
    - recognised VAT is between 0 and the VAT, and is set together with its timestamp.
  - VAT is computed on the line total after discount (BILL-28). The snapshot calls `catalog_version_reference` (CLIN-011).
  - `invoices` checks:
    - `sold_branch_id = branch_id` (here);
    - a visit implies a patient, and the visit belongs to that patient;
    - the patient is not merged (DEC-061);
    - a draft has no issue fields;
    - issuing needs at least one line.
  - `tax_profile_changes` checks:
    - `is_pkp` exactly when an effective date is set;
    - PKP needs an NPWP;
    - an NPWP is 15 or 16 digits;
    - `changed_as` is owner;
    - a reason and a key are given;
    - insert-only.
  - A guard on `tenants` requires the tax fields to change only together with a new change row.
- **P2-7, monitor.** It warns only non-PKP tenants: `none` below Rp4.0 billion, `near` from Rp4.0 billion, `over` at Rp4.8 billion or above. These are fixed display thresholds, not settings. The window is exactly the 12 months ending on the as-of date (`date > as_of − 12 months AND date ≤ as_of`), by the invoice branch's local date.
- **P3-8.** While PKP, a taxable line with the gate closed is refused with `ERR_GATE_CLOSED`. Lines are never issued without VAT. The contradictory sentence in §2 is withdrawn.
- **Open points, resolved:**
  1. The owner switches PKP on (SET-39). Finance owns the tax-advice evidence.
  2. Ending PKP is allowed only as a correction before any invoice is issued in the PKP era.
  3. Refuse.
  4. Minimal writers, granted now.
  5. The basis is the line total of issued invoices.
  6. Fixed display thresholds; non-PKP only.
