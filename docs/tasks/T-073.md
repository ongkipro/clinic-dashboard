## Sources

  - Release 1 ships a mock adapter that simulates paid, expired, failed, duplicate and late webhooks. Its receipts are marked SIMULASI and it never counts as cash.
  - A real provider is one adapter per tenant (Q-14). Cash, EDC and manual transfer need no gateway.
- **Scenarios (02:680–688).**
  - Duplicate webhook: one simulated payment and one simulated allocation, the receipt marked SIMULASI, and the line still unpaid for treatment start and visit close.
  - Mock hidden in production: a production tenant whose provider is still mock is not offered QRIS, VA or link.
- **TD-40.** The clinic is merchant of record on its own gateway account. Keys live in the tenant vault. `payment_routes(merchant_ref / order prefix → tenant_id)` routes webhooks. Item descriptions are generic ("Layanan klinik", "Produk"). A webhook with an unknown prefix is dropped and audited.
- **TD-41.** States are `created → pending → paid | expired | failed`. A payment is recorded only on a verified paid webhook or a verified status query. Duplicate and late webhooks are idempotent. A paid notification after expiry is credited to the patient's balance and flagged for review.
- **TD-43, the contract suite (every future real adapter must pass it):**
  - duplicate webhook → one payment;
  - paid after expiry → recorded and flagged;
  - amount mismatch → rejected and audited;
  - bad signature → 401 and audited;
  - lost webhook → a status query settles it once;
  - out-of-order events (paid after failed or cancelled) → flagged for review;
  - refund idempotent, including partial refunds;
  - webhook outside the timestamp window → rejected;
  - `payment_routes` tenant mismatch → dropped and audited;
  - gateway paid after the line was settled in cash → the value goes to the patient balance and is flagged.

  The mock sends HMAC-signed webhooks to `/api/v1/webhooks/payment/mock`, and every artefact is flagged simulated (BILL-29).
- **BILL-29.** The mock runs only in non-production environments and demo tenants. A production tenant whose provider is still `mock` sees only cash, EDC, transfer and balance. A simulated payment or allocation never satisfies BILL-1, BILL-8, visit close or receivable checks, and never enters K1, sub-ledgers or commission.
- **DATA-51.** `payment_port_configs`, `payment_charges` and `payment_webhook_events`, chained charge → webhook event → payment → allocation with foreign keys.
  - External IDs are unique per (tenant, provider, provider_account, environment), and event IDs are unique per provider.
  - Charges and events are written by the port service; payments and allocations by the cashier.
- **DATA-28.** Payments and allocations carry copies of method, provider, provider_account, environment and `is_simulated` (T-072 already added those columns).
- **SEC-8.** Signature verification, a timestamp window, and idempotency on the provider event ID. **SEC-15.** Generic item descriptions only.
- **Money scenario 18.** A QRIS charge of Rp1,500,000 for a treatment line. The app's response is lost, and the cashier's retry checks the charge status instead of creating a new charge. - **Existing code.**

## Design (proposed)

### 1. Tables (0088; module `src/modules/payments/`)

- **`payment_port_configs`** (tenant-shared, config): tenant, provider (`mock` only now), provider_account (e.g. `mock-<tenant short>`), environment (`production` | `staging` | `demo` | `local`), channels (`qris`, `va`, `payment_link`), the webhook secret wrapped with the tenant DEK (envelope, `src/lib/keys`) — never a plain column, never in a log — and `order_prefix` (unique across all tenants, e.g. `CD` + 8 base32). One active config per tenant. The row is created on first use by the port service, when the deployment is non-production or the tenant is a demo tenant (see §4).
- **`payment_routes`** (platform): `order_prefix → tenant_id, provider, provider_account, environment`, unique per prefix. Readable only by the definers (TD-40).
- **`payment_charges`** (tenant-ledger, personal): tenant, branch, invoice, the target lines with their amounts (a jsonb array, ≤ 100, or a child table `payment_charge_lines` — proposed: the child table, for FKs), channel, amount (1..1e12), `order_ref` (`<prefix>-<uuid short>`, unique), external id (unique per tenant, provider, provider_account and environment), state (`created | pending | paid | expired | failed | cancelled`), `expires_at` (from SET-14 link hours), `is_simulated`, `review_flag` (null or `paid_after_expiry` | `paid_after_failed` | `paid_after_cash` | `amount_mismatch`), `command_key`, created_by/as/at, settled_at, the resulting receipt id.
- **`payment_webhook_events`** (tenant-ledger; platform-written): provider, provider_account, environment, provider event id (unique per provider), order_ref, received_at, signature_ok, timestamp_ok, outcome (`applied | duplicate | rejected_signature | rejected_window | rejected_amount | dropped_unknown_route | dropped_tenant_mismatch | flagged`), charge id (nullable), the raw body hash (not the body: no PII stored; the mock body carries ids and amounts only).
- **`payment_refunds`** (tenant-ledger): charge, amount, provider refund id, state, command key, unique on (charge, command key); Σ refunded ≤ paid. (TD-43 "refund idempotent incl. partial"; the business refund flow is T-078 — here only the port operation and its idempotency.)

### 2. Port interface (TypeScript) and the mock adapter

`src/modules/payments/port.ts`: `PaymentPort { createCharge, status, cancel, refund, channels, verifyWebhook(rawBody, headers) → {eventId, orderRef, state, amount, at} | refusal }`. `src/modules/payments/mock.ts` implements it in-process: `createCharge` returns an external id and a QR/VA/link placeholder (text "SIMULASI"); the simulator (UX-56) changes the mock charge's state and emits a signed webhook (HMAC-SHA256 over `timestamp.body` with the tenant's secret; header `x-mock-signature: t=<unix>,v1=<hex>`), delivered by an in-process call to the same webhook handler the route uses (no network; nothing leaves the process). `status` reads the mock state. Nothing calls an external provider (AGENTS: no live calls).

### 3. Database writers (SECURITY DEFINER; actor lock first; ids-only events)

- `payment_charge_create(key, invoice, {channel, lines?})` (Kasir roles at the branch): the invoice issued, the port config usable (§4), the amount = Σ chosen lines' outstanding (default: the whole outstanding) allocated with `kasir_allocate`; generic description; state `pending`; `expires_at`; `PaymentChargeCreated {charge_id}`.
- `payment_charge_status_settle(charge, observed {state, amount, at, provider_event_id?})` (internal): the single settle path used by both the webhook and the status query (lost-webhook case). Idempotent on the charge: a charge already `paid` returns its receipt. `paid` on a `pending` charge within expiry with the exact amount → one receipt (marked simulated for the mock) + one payment (method = channel, provider, provider_account, environment, `is_simulated`) + allocations to its lines (the T-072 writer path refactored into an internal `kasir_record_payment(...)` so cash and port share it). Edge cases per TD-41/TD-43: paid after `expired` → recorded and `review_flag = paid_after_expiry` (the "credit to balance" half waits for T-075's balance ledger: recorded on the charge as `balance_pending`, open point 2); paid after `failed`/`cancelled` → recorded and flagged `paid_after_failed`; amount ≠ charge amount → not recorded, flagged, audited; a target line already fully paid (cash first) → recorded as excess, `review_flag = paid_after_cash`, balance credit pending T-075 (open point 2).
- `payment_webhook_ingest(provider, account, environment, event jsonb, signature_ok, timestamp_ok)` (worker connection, unbound, like `logbook_due_scan`): records the event row first (unique provider event id → duplicate outcome, no second effect); routes by `order_prefix` through `payment_routes` → tenant; an unknown prefix or a route whose provider/account/environment differ → dropped and audited (platform audit); a bad signature or a stale timestamp → event row with the refusal outcome, audited, nothing applied; else binds the tenant and calls `payment_charge_status_settle`.
- `payment_charge_poll(charge)` (Kasir roles): the "Cek Status" retry — asks the adapter's status (TypeScript) and settles through the same path; never creates a new charge (scenario 18).
- `payment_charge_cancel(charge)`, `payment_refund(key, charge, amount)` (owner/manager; the port operation only, idempotent by key; Σ ≤ paid; T-078 owns the patient-facing refund).
- `simulasi_*` (UX-56: FO/cashier and manager of a demo/non-production tenant only): mark paid / expired / failed, resend the last webhook (duplicate), send a late webhook, send with a wrong signature (test runner only? — open point 3).

### 4. Where the mock may run (BILL-29, PR-68 "hidden in production")

`payment_port_usable(tenant)`: the config's provider is `mock` ⇒ usable only when the deployment environment (`DEPLOY_ENV`, passed in by the TypeScript layer as the server's known value, never from the request) is not `production`, or the tenant is a demo tenant. There is no demo-tenant flag yet: **open point 1** — proposed: treat staging/demo/local deployments as mock-allowed and production as mock-forbidden for every tenant until a demo-tenant flag exists (no new setting; a demo tenant on the production cluster is not planned in Release 1). The Kasir tender options (T-072 `kasir_invoice_get`) become: QRIS/VA/link available when the port is usable, else blocked `payment_port` (production with mock: not offered — scenario PR-68 hidden). This re-creates `kasir_invoice_get` and `payment_receive` (scope inside `drizzle/**`; the T-072 functions are in this task's migration area).

### 5. BILL-29 consequences already enforced, and the ones added

- T-072: `kasir_line_paid` ignores simulated allocations → the line stays unpaid; the visit payment step stays `in_progress` → treatment start and visit close stay blocked (scenario PR-68 "still counts as unpaid"). Tested here end to end.
- K1 / sub-ledgers / commission do not exist yet (M5/M8); the simulated flag on every payment and allocation is what they will filter on (asserted by a schema test).
- Receipts of a simulated payment: `receipts.is_simulated` (T-072 column) and the body begins with "SIMULASI".

### 6. Webhook route

`src/app/api/v1/webhooks/payment/mock/route.ts` — **scope expansion** (outside `src/modules/payments/**` and `src/app/(app)/simulasi/**`; required by TD-43 and served on the booking host by `hosts.ts`): POST only, raw body ≤ 16 KB, verifies the signature against the tenant secret looked up through the route (constant-time compare), the timestamp window ±5 minutes, then `payment_webhook_ingest` on a worker pool; answers 401 on a bad signature, 200 otherwise (including duplicates and drops, so a provider stops retrying), never echoing data. In production with no mock config it answers 404.

### 7. UI

- `/simulasi` (UX-56): list of the tenant's simulated charges (pending first) with SIMULASI banner; each row: Lunas · Kedaluwarsa · Gagal · Kirim Ulang Webhook (secondary: Kirim Terlambat). Hidden (404 + nav absent) when the port is not the mock or the deployment is production.
- Kasir (scope expansion `src/app/(app)/kasir/**`): the tender sheet's QRIS/Link chips become available when the port is usable; choosing one creates a charge ("Buat Tagihan QRIS Rp…"), shows the SIMULASI QR/link placeholder, "Cek Status" (poll) and "Batalkan"; the receipt of a simulated payment says SIMULASI. VA as a third chip only if SET-14 lists it (it does not: open point 4 — SET-14's enum has qris but no va/link; proposed: the port's channels decide availability; SET-14 `qris` gates QRIS; link and VA follow the port config until a decision adds them to SET-14 — no new setting).

## Plan review r1 (Argon `eefd48be`, REVISE): every finding checked against the code and adopted

Open points 1–6 were judged acceptable without an owner decision. Changes to the design follow.

- **P1-1. Unbound ingest: audit, keys, attribution.**
  - **Webhook secret.** No secret is stored. The mock's webhook secret is derived from the server's KEK ring: HKDF-SHA256 over the current KEK, with info `payment-mock|<tenant>|<provider_account>|v<kek version>`. The config stores only the KEK version. This removes the DEK lookup on an unbound connection, the `tenant_data_key_ensure` role limit, and any wrapped secret column.
  - **Without `KEK_FILE`.** The port is unusable and the webhook route answers 404. Tests use `setKekRingForTests`.
  - **Real adapters (Q-14).** These bring a vault reference with their own task.
  - **Audit on a service context.** An internal helper `payment_audit_service(tenant, action, object_kind, object, outcome, reason)` writes an `audit_events` row under the `audit:<tenant>` lock with `service_identity = 'payment_webhook'` (the actor check accepts a service), using the same chain as `audit_append`.
  - **Platform drops** (unknown route) go to `vendor_log(NULL, NULL, NULL, 'payment_webhook_drop', NULL, NULL, 'denied', <reason>, NULL, NULL, 'payment_webhook')`.
  - **Interactive paths** (poll, cancel, refund, the simulator) use `audit_append`.
  - **Attribution.** A receipt or payment settled by webhook is attributed to the charge's creator (`created_by`/`created_as`, DATA-60 origin attribution). A poll is attributed to the actor who polled. The receipt's command key is `charge:<charge id>`, deterministic, so the settle path is idempotent across webhook and poll.
- **P1-2. Event storage and ingest order.**
  - **Event rows.** `payment_webhook_events` has `tenant_id NOT NULL` and `UNIQUE(tenant_id, id)`. An unknown prefix writes no event row: only `vendor_log`.
  - **Ingest order:**
    1. Route by `order_prefix`.
    2. If the provider, account or environment don't match the route: `dropped_tenant_mismatch`, recorded under the routed tenant and audited.
    3. Check the signature and the timestamp window, using the routed tenant's secret (derived in TypeScript before the call). A bad signature is `rejected_signature`; a stale timestamp is `rejected_window`.
    4. Only then apply the event.
  - **Idempotency index.** The unique index on `(provider, provider_account, environment, provider_event_id)` is partial (`WHERE outcome IN ('applied','duplicate','flagged','rejected_amount')`), so a forged or unverified request can never claim a real event ID.
- **P1-3. The simulator.**
  - **Separate mock state.** The mock gateway's state lives in its own table `payment_mock_states`: charge, mock state, amount, time, last event ID. It is written only by the simulator and the mock's `createCharge`, and read by the mock's `status()`.
  - **Single settle path.** `payment_charges.state` changes only through `payment_charge_status_settle`.
  - **Simulator flow.** A `simulasi_*` action commits its `withTenantContext` transaction and returns the signed webhook `{rawBody, headers}`. The TypeScript caller then calls the webhook handler on the worker pool, outside any tenant context. There is no nesting and no self-deadlock.
- **P1-4. The shared recording path and its edge cases.**
  - **Shared helper.** `kasir_record_payment` is internal and shared by cash and the port.
  - **VAT.** It updates the line's VAT recognition only when `NOT is_simulated`, so BILL-29 stays out of the tax sub-ledger.
  - **`balance_pending`.** `payment_charges` gets a `balance_pending bigint NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` column.
  - **Paid after expiry.** A receipt and payment, no line allocations, `review_flag = paid_after_expiry`, `balance_pending` set to the amount.
  - **Excess over what is left to allocate.** Paid after cash, after failed, or after a cancelled charge: allocate up to the lines' remaining room and put the excess in `balance_pending`. The room counts real outstanding for a real charge, and `line_total` minus simulated allocations for a simulated charge. The excess is flagged.
  - **Void.** A void cancels pending charges. A webhook paid on a void invoice records the receipt and payment against the charge with no allocations, puts everything in `balance_pending`, and is flagged `paid_after_failed`. `payments_guard` is re-created to allow a port payment on a void invoice only through its charge.
  - **One pending charge per invoice.** A partial unique index; a retry returns the active pending charge. `invoice_void_request` cancels pending charges through the void sync.
- **P1-5. The DATA-51 chain, lock order and gates.**
  - **Payment links.** `payments.charge_id` and `payments.webhook_event_id` are nullable composite foreign keys. `payments_guard` (re-created) requires a port method if and only if a charge is present.
  - **Ingest lock order.** Ingest takes the `staff_access:<tenant>` lock as soon as the tenant is resolved, before visit, invoice, lines, charge.
  - **Gates for every new table:** tenant ID and the unique key, composite foreign keys, RLS, scope map, support class, an operator policy, and the no-truncate trigger.
  - **Drift test.** It is `tests/payments/payments-drift.test.ts`, inside the allowed paths. It declares every re-created function: `payment_receive`, `kasir_invoice_get`, `kasir_receipt_body`, `receipt_get`, `payments_guard`, `invoice_void_sync`, `kasir_actor`, each with the same signature.
- **P2-6. BILL-29, hidden in production, and the T-072 tests.**
  - **No signature changes.** `kasir_invoice_get(uuid)` keeps its signature. The deployment environment reaches SQL through a transaction-local `app.deploy_env` that the TypeScript layer sets from `deployEnv()`. When it is missing, SQL treats the deployment as production (fail closed).
  - **When the port is usable.** `payment_port_usable(tenant)` needs both an active port config and `(provider <> 'mock' OR deploy_env <> 'production')`. An unprovisioned tenant keeps `blocked: payment_port`, so the T-072 tests stay as they are. Simulasi provisions the mock config explicitly with "Aktifkan Port Simulasi" (owner/manager, non-production only).
  - **In production with mock:** QRIS, VA and link are omitted from `tenders` entirely (not shown as disabled chips).
  - **VA.** VA is added to the tender methods and labels.
- **P2-7. The webhook route and scope.**
  - **Status codes.** 401 for a bad or short signature (the hex length is checked before `timingSafeEqual`) and for a timestamp outside ±5 min; 413 above 16 KB of raw bytes; 400 for bad JSON; 404 in production or when the port is unusable; 200 otherwise, with an empty body. Every response sends `Cache-Control: no-store`.
  - **Scope expansions:**
    - `src/modules/cashier/kasir/kasir.ts` and `src/modules/cashier/schema.ts` (the payment foreign keys, VA, readers);
    - `src/app/(app)/kasir/**` (QRIS, VA and link in the tender sheet, Cek Status, the SIMULASI receipt, the link to /simulasi);
    - `src/app/api/v1/webhooks/payment/mock/route.ts`;
    - `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`, `tests/context/stale-context.test.ts`;
    - `tests/isolation/cases/payments.cases.ts`: `/simulasi`, `/api/v1/webhooks/payment/mock#route`, the simulasi and new Kasir actions;
    - `tests/ui/simulasi.spec.ts`.

    `nav.ts` is not touched: /simulasi is reached from the Kasir.

## Plan review r2 (Argon `a7967759`, APPROVE): the four build notes, adopted

- **F-1.** The partial unique index excludes `duplicate`: `WHERE outcome IN ('applied','flagged','rejected_amount')`. Duplicate deliveries are recorded as `duplicate` rows.
- **F-2.** The room of a simulated charge is the line total less every allocation, real and simulated, so paid-after-cash on the mock goes to `balance_pending` and is flagged. A real charge's room is the line total less its real allocations (`kasir_line_paid`).
- **F-3.** `kasir_record_payment` and every port writer name `tenant_id`, `branch_id`, `staff_id` and `acting_role` explicitly when inserting into `domain_events`. The service audit helper writes the reason code into `reason_code` and leaves `reason` null.
- **F-4.** `kasir_invoice_get` lists VA only for a provisioned port whose channels include it, so an unprovisioned tenant keeps the six T-072 tenders. The secret is derived with the KEK of the config's recorded version (`ring.keys.get(kek_version)`), and provisioning records `ring.current`.
