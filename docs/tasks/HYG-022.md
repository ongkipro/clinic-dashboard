## Why
T-100's gated close 2 (2026-11-17 03:15 WIB) could not complete test-full: `tests/platform/hosts.spec.ts` runs `next build`, and Next's
build-time advisory gate (`experimental.agentUpgrade: 'security'`, the default, node_modules/next/dist/server/config-shared.js:192) stopped
it with `SecurityFatalError` (evidence: /data/tmp/clinic-dashboard/t100/failure-close2/). The advisory: GHSA-mfr8-8qww-v76h (moderate),
"Information disclosure across root params when a 'use cache' function is called through unstable_cache()", vulnerable next >=16.3.0
<=16.4.4, first patched 16.4.5, published 2026-11-16T18:51:22Z (Panel 2 verified on the GitHub advisory API; `pnpm audit` agrees). The
app uses neither `unstable_cache` nor `'use cache'` (grep of src), but every build — local, CI, every worktree — is blocked until patched.
