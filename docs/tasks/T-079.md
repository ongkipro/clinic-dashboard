### 4.1 Vocabulary (one spelling per concept)
- **Consultation visit**: a visit with an `examinations` row (the doctor step was opened, `0070:390-411`). A visit without an examination (counter-like visit, package session with no doctor) never owes a fee.
- **Fee amount**: SET-6 read with `setting_value_at('SET-6', NULL, at)`; `null` ("Belum diisi") means the fee rule is not active for the tenant yet (§7 point 2).
- **Consultation item**: an active catalog **service** whose version `category = 'consultation'` (`catalog/schema.ts:25`); it gives the line its item, version and tax snapshot (A-06). The line price is the fee amount, not the branch price (§7 point 1).
- **Consultation invoice**: an invoice with the new flag `invoices.consultation = true`, exactly one line (the fee line). It is never "ordinary" (`invoice_is_ordinary` re-created), so it never blocks or receives the visit's purchase invoice, DP or treatment gating.
- **Qualifying purchase** (BILL-4) of visit V: a line with `line_total > 0` of an issued invoice that is either (a) a non-consultation invoice with `visit_id = V` and no substitution line (ordinary and addition invoices), or (b) a package invoice of V's canonical patient issued at V's branch while V was open (`issued_at` between `checked_in_at` and the close) — the package invoice carries no visit (`0093_deposits.sql:845`) (§7 point 3). Counter (Jual Cepat) invoices never qualify (no visit).
- **Fully refunded** line: an ordinary line whose paid refunds (`refunds.state = 'paid'`) sum to `line_total − cancelled_amount`; a package line whose package was closed `cancelled` with no session redeemed (its paid value went back to the balance, DEC-090 (1)). Interrupted-treatment moves (`from_interrupted_treatment`) and expiry are not refunds (§7 point 4).

### 4.2 Tables (DATA-34; tenant-shared; insert-only or a guarded state machine; never deleted; `*_no_truncate` ENABLE ALWAYS; composite `(tenant_id, …)` FKs; guards take the `staff_access` lock first)
- **`invoices.consultation boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`** — set only by the consultation definers (insert); frozen by `invoices_guard` (re-created). CHECK `not consultation or (visit_id is not null and not counter)`.
- **`consultation_fees`** — one per consultation visit, written when the visit closes (or earlier by an explicit exemption): `id, tenant_id, branch_id` (the visit's), `visit_id` (unique), `patient_id` (canonical at decision), `state` (`charged | waived | recharged`, DATA-34), `waiver` (`purchase | package_session | control | complaint`, null when charged), `fee_amount` (SET-6 at the decision for `purchase`; the line total for `charged`; null for the other waivers), `fee_line_id` (charged: the consultation line; recharged: the re-charge line), `control_id`, `complaint_task_id`, `recharge_refund_id`, `recharge_package_id`, `deducted` (rupiah taken from the refund), `decided_by/as/at`, `recharged_at`, `command_key`. - **`consultation_fee_purchases`** — the qualifying lines recorded at the waiver (`fee_id, invoice_line_id` unique per fee, `id, tenant_id, branch_id`). Insert-only. The re-charge test reads exactly these rows, so a purchase made after the close never changes the decision (no retroactive waiver, 4b).
- **`consultation_controls`** (BILL-5; DATA-34 "control: source purchase, due date, valid_until, used"): `id, tenant_id, branch_id` (scheduling branch), `patient_id` (canonical), `invoice_line_id` (the source purchase), `seq` (1..catalog `control_count` of the line's version; unique `(tenant_id, invoice_line_id, seq)`), `control_date` (branch-local date), `valid_until` (= `control_date + SET-7.hari`, snapshotted), `state` (`scheduled | used | cancelled`), `used_visit_id`, `used_at`, `cancel_code` (`source_refunded`), `scheduled_by/as/at`, `command_key`. Guard: insert `scheduled`; `scheduled → used` once (with `used_visit_id`) or `→ cancelled` once.

- **Refund hook** (in `refund_settle`, re-created, between the `from_refund` credit and the `cash_refund` debit): when the refund's invoice has a visit with a `waived/purchase` fee whose recorded purchases are all fully refunded **counting this refund** (`r` is not yet `paid` inside `refund_settle`) → `D := consultation_recharge(f, 'refund', r.id, r.amount, …)`; a cash refund then debits `cash_refund −(r.amount − D)`. Balance destination: `+amount`, `−D` → scenario 4c "balance ends at 183,000"; cash: the patient gets `amount − D` in cash ("deducted from the refund", PR-12). - **Package hook** (in `package_close_do`, re-created, `p_state = 'cancelled'`): after the paid-unused value moves to the balance (`from_cancel`), when the package's invoice line is a recorded purchase of a `waived/purchase` fee and every recorded purchase is now fully refunded → `consultation_recharge(f, 'package', pk.id, <moved amount>, …)`.

- **Ordering constraints found in the code.** (a) On the balance path `refund_request` calls `refund_settle` *before* it inserts the `refunds` row (`0094:514-519`), so `consultation_fees.recharge_refund_id → refunds` is a composite FK declared `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED` (checked at commit). (b) Inside `refund_settle` the current refund is not yet `paid`, so "every recorded purchase fully refunded" counts paid refunds **plus `r.amount` on `r.invoice_line_id`**. (c) The deduction must precede the `cash_refund` debit: a cash refund debits the whole amount (`0094:390-392`), which would leave nothing in the balance to fund the fee. (d) Lock order stays staff_access → visit → invoice → lines → balance: `refund_request` and `refund_pay` already hold the visit lock (`0094:469-472`, `:556`); `package_close_do` has no visit (a package invoice has none), so the hook takes the fee's visit lock `FOR UPDATE` before any new invoice row (§7 point 3 notes the deadlock review).

## 7. Open points (each with a recommendation; **[OWNER]** marks a genuine product decision)

1. **Which item and price carry the fee line.** SET-6 holds the amount (tenant scope, `0028:18`); the line needs an item for its tax snapshot (A-06) and version. *Recommendation:* the line uses the tenant's active service with `category = 'consultation'`; with several, the caller names one (`item_id`), with none → `ERR_NOT_AVAILABLE: consultation_item`. The **price is SET-6**, not the branch price, because SET-6 is the one fee setting (PR-38) and `19:340` makes "consultation fee > 0" a fixed rule; a branch price override on that item is ignored for the fee. Record in DEC-092 (1). Not [OWNER]: SET-6 already decides the amount.
2. **SET-6 unset.** *Recommendation:* the rule is inactive (no charge, no close refusal) while SET-6 is `null`; once set, it applies to visits closing from then (read at the close/charge instant, `setting_value_at`). This keeps every existing test and tenant unchanged (16 test files reference `closeVisit`/`visit_close`).
3. **Package bought on the visit.** A package invoice has no visit (`invoice_issue` refuses one, `0093:845`), yet a package bought during the consultation is a qualifying purchase (fixture `:41` "visits with a qualifying purchase (fee waived) or package"). *Recommendation:* count a package invoice of the canonical patient issued at the visit's branch between check-in and close; record it in `consultation_fee_purchases`. The package re-charge path (cancel with nothing redeemed) must keep the Kasir lock order staff_access → visit → invoice → lines: `package_cancel` (`0091_packages.sql:1688`) locks the package first (`:1698`) and has no visit. *Recommendation:* re-create `package_cancel` to look up the fee visit (a `consultation_fee_purchases` row for `packages.invoice_line_id`, a plain read) and lock that visit `FOR UPDATE` **before** the package; `package_close_do`'s hook then only re-reads it. Package expiry (the scheduler path) never re-charges, so it needs no visit lock.
4. **What counts as "fully refunded".** BILL-4/21 say refund. *Recommendation:* paid `refunds` rows (balance or cash) and a package **cancelled** with no session redeemed. Interrupted treatment (clinic or medical stop moves value to balance, `from_interrupted_treatment`), package expiry and a void are not refunds: they never re-charge. A void of the purchase invoice cannot happen after payment (void only while unpaid), and an unpaid purchase still counts as a purchase at close (§4.1). Record in DEC-092 (2).
5. **Refund smaller than the fee, and cash refunds.** "deducted from the refund" (PR-12) presumes the refund ≥ the fee. *Recommendation:* deduct `D = least(fee, refund amount)` from the refund that completes the full refund; the rest of the fee stays owed on the re-charge invoice (Tagihan Terbuka; collectable or convertible to a receivable). A cash refund pays out `amount − D`; a cash refund that the fee would absorb completely is refused with "refund ke saldo" (`refund_absorbed_by_fee`) because a zero cash payout is not a cash refund. Not [OWNER]: it follows from "deducted from the refund" and B-04.
6. **Who schedules a control and when.** BILL-5 "per item count and validity (default 1, until control date + 7 days)"; research `BEAUTY-CLINIC-SYSTEM-BLUEPRINT.md:57` "doctor-scheduled control linked to the purchase" (evidence only). *Recommendation:* `control_schedule` by doctor, FO/cashier, manager or owner, per paid-or-issued line whose version has `control_count > 0`; the control date must fall within `control_validity_days` of the purchase; `valid_until = control_date + SET-7.hari`. **SET-7 `jumlah` vs catalog `control_count`:** the catalog field is the per-item count (DATA-15, `19:295` "per item"); SET-7 `jumlah` is the default for new catalog versions (Master Layanan pre-fills it). T-079 uses the catalog count and SET-7 `hari` only; pre-filling the Master Layanan form is outside paths. Record in DEC-092 (3).
7. **No Kasir UI in this task.** With SET-6 set and no UI, staff cannot charge or exempt from the app, and `visit_close` would refuse consultation-only visits. *Recommendation:* ship the close refusal (it is the PR-12/`17:341` rule) and add a STATUS.md follow-up "Kasir: Biaya Konsultasi row, exemption sheet, Hari Ini blocker copy" before any tenant sets SET-6; demo/staging seeds keep SET-6 null until then. If the supervisor prefers, gate the close refusal behind the follow-up instead (charge/exempt still available through the module).
8. **"New complaint" on a control or package-session visit** (BILL-4 "not charged for package-session-only or eligible control visits **without new complaint**"). There is no "new complaint" field; an examination exists on most visits. *Recommendation:* the default at close is the waiver (package session or valid control); the cashier charges explicitly (`consultation_fee_charge`) when the doctor reports a new complaint, which is allowed on such visits because no fee row exists until close. Treatment-caused complaints are free through the `kontrol_gratis` complaint outcome (PR-43). A structured "keluhan baru" flag on the examination is a clinical-module change outside paths — not added.
