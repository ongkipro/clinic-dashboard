## Design (proposed)

**Data** (migration 0074, `src/modules/clinical/schema.ts`):
- `complaint_tasks`: tenant, branch, patient (canonical), visit (nullable), kind `klinis` | `layanan`, source `visit_note` | `staff_flag` | `pharmacist` (| `message` reserved for the inbox task), triage `merah` | `kuning` | `hijau` (klinis only, set by the flagger, changeable by a clinician), summary (short text; klinis content), category for layanan (`waktu_tunggu`, `tagihan`, `sikap_staf`, `lainnya`), state `open` | `in_progress` | `resolved` | `closed`, due_at (computed at creation from SET-31 / SET-31.layanan and branch hours), assigned_to (nullable: the clinician who takes it), flagged_by/as, resolution note and outcome, resolved_by/at, created_at; escalated_at (set when overdue is first seen).
- `complaint_events` (history): task, kind (flagged, taken, triaged, note, resolved, reopened, escalated), by/as, at, note (klinis content only on klinis tasks).

**Writers** (actor lock first; capability `existing_appointment`; audit and ids-only events):
- `complaint_flag(patient, visit?, kind, triage?, category?, summary)`: who may flag (07:48): FO, central CS, BC, doctor, nurse, pharmacist, facility head, manager (manager layanan only?) — open point 3. Computes due_at.
- `complaint_take`, `complaint_triage`, `complaint_note`, `complaint_resolve`, `complaint_reopen`: klinis by doctor, nurse, pharmacist, facility head of the branch; layanan by manager (and facility head?) — open point 4.
- Overdue: due_at < now and not resolved → shown as overdue; escalation notification to the facility head (klinis) / owner (layanan) — open point 5 (job vs. read-time).

**Readers:** `complaint_list(branch, kind, filter)` with role projection: FO/CS/BC see their flags' status only (no summary of a klinis task, no notes); manager sees layanan only; clinicians see klinis (and layanan?). `complaint_get(task)` with history.

**UI:** `/keluhan` segments Klinis · Layanan (Insiden later), filters (status, overdue), rows with triage chip and due time; `/keluhan/[id]` detail with Tangani (take), notes, resolve; a flag form reachable from the visit (Pendampingan / Pemeriksaan pages are outside the Allowed Paths — open point 2).

### Adopted design (supersedes "Design (proposed)" where they differ)
- **Readers and RLS (P1-1):** invoker wall-clock `complaint_readable(tenant, branch, kind)`: klinis → doctor, nurse, pharmacist, facility head; layanan → manager, facility head (acting role held at the branch now; never null). Direct table reads only through it; FO, central CS, BC and therapist read nothing directly. Definer `complaint_list` / `complaint_get`: clinicians klinis with content; manager layanan only; facility head both; FO, central CS, BC only the tasks they flagged, projected to status (no summary, resolution or event notes); therapist refused everywhere.
- **due_at (P1-2):** `complaint_due_at(tenant, branch, kind, triage, from)`: merah and kuning add wall-clock minutes from SET-31; hijau and layanan add working hours across `branch_hours` minus `branch_closures` in the branch timezone (`23:59:59.999999` as midnight), bounded to 60 local days, wall-clock fallback when the branch has no hours or the window runs out. Re-triage recomputes from `created_at`.
- **Model and states (P1-3):** states `open` | `in_progress` | `resolved` (no `closed`); the reviewed CHECKs (kind ⇔ triage / category; sources incl. reserved `message`; visit_note needs a visit; pharmacist source only by a pharmacist on klinis; flagger roles with manager layanan only; open ⇔ unassigned; resolved ⇔ the five resolution columns; outcome codes incl. `kontrol_gratis`). Resolving an open task assigns the resolver; reopen moves resolved → in_progress and clears the resolution (history kept). `complaint_flag(patient, visit?, kind, triage?, category?, summary, branch?)`: the visit's branch (checked against the patient cluster and the actor's branch) or, without a visit, the actor's or named branch (central CS).
- **Notifications and escalation (P1-4):** notification types `complaint_klinis` (doctor, nurse, pharmacist, facility head), `complaint_layanan` (manager, facility head), `complaint_overdue` (facility head, owner); flagging emits `ComplaintFlagged` and notifies the kind's roles at the branch. Readers and writers take `tenant_actor_lock` first (DEC-043 order), check `clinical_read`, and when `existing_appointment` holds mark overdue tasks once (`escalated_at`, an `escalated` event, `ComplaintEscalated`, a `complaint_overdue` notification to the facility head for klinis or the owner for layanan); `overdue` is also computed live.
- **Guards, events, audit (P2-5):** scope map and support class clinical with operator policies; guards (staff_access first, ENABLE ALWAYS, no truncate): events insert-only with the reviewed CHECKs; tasks never deleted, open on insert, identity and summary frozen, escalation set once, a resolved task changes only by reopen. Events `ComplaintFlagged`, `ComplaintTaken`, `ComplaintTriaged`, `ComplaintResolved`, `ComplaintReopened`, `ComplaintEscalated` (ids and codes). Audit `settings_change` per writer; `clinical_read` for a clinician's klinis detail; reason always NULL. TS writers under `assertProductionGoLive` and `runCommand` (no free text in results).
- **UI (P3-7):** `/keluhan` with role segments (facility head Klinis · Layanan · Insiden; clinicians Klinis · Insiden; manager Layanan; FO/CS/BC "Laporan saya" with status), Insiden as a BlockedState pointing to T-068; `/keluhan/baru` (the flag form, `?visit=` prefill, today's visits at the branch); `/keluhan/[id]` detail with Tangani, triage, notes, resolve, reopen. No action inside a row link; rendered context and rotating keys on every form; forms stay mounted after success. Therapist gets the permission state (nav.ts untouched).
