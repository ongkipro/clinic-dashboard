## Design (proposed; needs the plan review)

- **Where the data lives:** on the same immutable `catalog_versions` row (one version = one complete snapshot, so bookings that store the version keep its durations and resources; DATA-62 "add columns"): `category`, `prep_min`, `treatment_min`, `buffer_min`, `online_bookable`, `aftercare_text`; child rows per version `service_resources` (kind room|device, value: room kind or device type code, required boolean) and `service_staff_eligibility` (role or staff_id, delegation mode `performs`|`assists`|`supervised`), written in the same `catalog_publish` call and immutable with the version. Services only (kind = service); other kinds keep these empty.
- **Which changes need which review (PR-88):**
  - clinical review (facility head, T-033 gate): the four T-033 clinical fields **plus** staff eligibility/delegation and aftercare text (both clinical content: who may perform a procedure; post-treatment instructions).
  - materials review (pharmacist): the BHP recipe (`material_formula`, now with units) — a second gate (`catalog_materials_review`, approver pharmacist, owner fallback when no pharmacist other than the requester).
  - durations, resources, category, online-bookable: operational, active at once (like price).
  - One pending version per item (T-033 lock): a version whose change touches both clinical and materials needs **both** approvals; it becomes active when the last one is approved, and a rejection of either rejects it.
- **SET-30 "only narrower than the legal table":** the legal floor by profession is a fixed table in code/migration (class F): e.g. filler/toxin/threads/ablative laser → doctor only; therapist never invasive (CD-16). A version's eligibility may only narrow it; a widening is refused at publish.
- **UI:** `/layanan` (Master Layanan list per category) and `/layanan/[id]` with the 19 tabs that exist now (Umum, Durasi & Sumber Daya, Staf & Delegasi, Pajak, Resep BHP, Persetujuan Tindakan, Aftercare, Booking Online); Harga Cabang (T-035) and Situs (public-site task) show "belum tersedia" honestly. ## Research and recommendation (owner 2026-11-08: "lakukan riset … jika memang dibutuhkan kamu boleh mensetujuinya … review dulu dan audit")

Evidence (Indonesian law and regulators; clinical validation still pending as Q-07, a G2 gate):
- **Aesthetic medical acts are for medical personnel.** UU 17/2023 Pasal 137(1): aesthetic surgery only by Tenaga Medis with expertise and authority. Kemenkes (Dec 2024, after the Ria Beauty case; restated 2026): medical acts in beauty clinics, injections included, only by authorised medical personnel (doctor / Sp.DVE).
- **Nurses perform medical acts only by written delegation.** UU 38/2014 Pasal 32 (written delegation, delegatif or mandat, from medical personnel); UU 17/2023 Pasal 285(1) (practice within competence-based authority) and Pasal 290 (pelimpahan kewenangan by mandat or delegatif, from Tenaga Medis to Tenaga Kesehatan); Permenkes 26/2019. In a clinic the nurse's authority is detailed by credentials and the clinic leadership's clinical assignment letter.
- **Beauticians / therapists are non-medical**: skin analysis, facials, non-medical devices; injections and medical acts excluded.
- **The clinic's technical lead is a medical professional.** Permenkes 9/2014 Pasal 9: the penanggung jawab teknis of a clinic is a tenaga medis with SIP there (the system's facility head).
- **Pharmacy.** Permenkes 34/2021: a clinic running pharmacy services has an Instalasi Farmasi under an apoteker with SIPA; mandatory for inpatient, optional for outpatient. So an outpatient beauty clinic may have no pharmacist.
- Spec: 07 row "Catalog & branch prices" (Facility head ◐ clinical review; Pharmacist ◐ materials); SET-30 (delegation only narrower than the fixed legal table; facility head + owner, two different people; edited in Master Layanan; DEC-031 D2); CD-16 default; DEC-046.

Recommendation (to be audited before it is recorded as DEC-047):
1. **Staff eligibility / delegation** (SET-30): a version that changes it needs the facility head's approval **and** must be proposed by the Owner — exactly "facility head + owner, two different people". No owner fallback for delegation: without an active facility head (the clinic's medical technical lead) a delegation change is blocked ("Tetapkan Penanggung Jawab Klinik dulu"). **Aftercare text** is clinical content: the T-033 clinical review (facility head; owner fallback per DEC-046).
2. **BHP recipe**: a materials review by the pharmacist (new approval type `catalog_materials_review`; any active pharmacist tenant-wide; the owner decides only while no pharmacist other than the requester could, since outpatient clinics may have none). The pharmacist opens Persetujuan for this type only.
3. **Legal floor (class F, fixed in the migration, mirrored in code, pending Q-07)**: categories `filler, toxin, threads, laser_ablative, injection, infusion, laser_non_ablative, peeling_chemical, microneedling, facial, body_treatment, consultation`; modes ranked `performs > supervised > assists > none`; floor: doctor `performs` everywhere; nurse `assists` for filler/toxin/threads/laser_ablative/consultation, `supervised` (written delegation) for injection/infusion/laser_non_ablative/peeling_chemical/microneedling, `performs` for facial/body_treatment; therapist `none` for every medical or invasive category and consultation, `performs` for facial/body_treatment. An eligibility row naming a staff member is checked against that person's active clinical roles. Device codes: `laser, laser_ablative, laser_non_ablative, ipl, rf, hifu, microneedling_device, centrifuge, facial_machine`; room kinds per DATA-46 (`consultation, treatment, pharmacy`).
4. **Data per role (data flow needed by the role)**: category, durations, online-bookable, resources and the eligible roles/staff (no clinical text) are readable by booking roles (FO, central CS) through a projection, because T-044 finds slots and performers with them; aftercare text, recipe and the clinical fields stay with clinical roles and editors (DEC-046). Vendor support class: `service_resources` config; `service_staff_eligibility` personal (it names staff).
5. **Scope expansions** (ledger): `src/modules/catalog/catalog.ts`, `tests/catalog/catalog.test.ts` (publish gains the service fields and the materials gate), `src/modules/approvals/inbox.ts` + test, `src/app/(app)/persetujuan/**`, `src/components/shell/nav.ts` (pharmacist → Persetujuan), definer/authority-clock lists, isolation cases, browser spec, drift test.

## Design (final, per DEC-047; plan review rounds 2-3)

- **Columns on `catalog_versions`** (services; null/false/empty for other kinds): `category` (enum, required when eligibility rows exist), `prep_min` 0–120, `treatment_min` 5–480, `buffer_min` 0–120 (defaults 10/30/10 for a new service), `online_bookable`, `aftercare_text`; a second review ref `materials_approval_id` beside the T-033 `approval_id` (clinical). Guard and CHECK: `pending_review` (and its outcomes) needs at least one of the two refs.
- **Child tables** (immutable, written only by `catalog_publish` in the version's own transaction; tenant-keyed composite FKs; RLS; scope_map; support class): `service_resources` (version, kind room|device, code from the room-kind or device enum, required) and `service_staff_eligibility` (version, either role doctor|nurse|therapist or a staff id, mode performs|supervised|assists).
- **Floor**: platform table `delegation_floor` (category, profession, max mode) seeded by the migration (scope_map platform, RLS, no grants), mirrored in `src/modules/catalog/service/floor.ts` and checked equal by a test. `catalog_publish` refuses a row whose mode exceeds the floor for the version's category: by role, or for a named staff member the highest floor across that person's active doctor/nurse/therapist roles (none → refused).
- **Publish** (`catalog_publish`, extended, item lock and pending-lock as T-033): omitted service fields and child rows carry forward from the active version, else the latest (as the material formula in T-033); gates compare with the **active** version only: clinical = the four fields + aftercare + eligibility; materials = the recipe; operational (category, durations, online-bookable, resources) active at once when alone. A version needing both gets both approvals.
- **Sync** (the T-033 trigger, extended to both types): a version becomes active when every attached approval is approved; when one is rejected or cancelled the version takes that state and the other still-pending approval is cancelled in the same transaction (audited). Withdraw from the item page cancels the open approval(s).
- **Approvals**: `catalog_materials_review` (requesters owner/admin_pusat/manager; approvers pharmacist, facility_head, owner); `approval_decide` refuses facility head/owner on it while a pharmacist other than the requester is available (`approval_ph_available`), as the D2 rule; `approvals_read` and the inbox admit a pharmacist for this type tenant-wide; nav: pharmacist gets Persetujuan.
- **Readers**: `catalog_versions` policy also admits a pharmacist on unpublished versions that carry `materials_approval_id`, and `catalog_review_target` returns the recipe (proposed vs active) for a materials review without clinical text to a pharmacist; `catalog_commercial()` gains category, durations, online-bookable; `catalog_service_requirements(version)` (definer) returns resources and eligibility of an active version for the 07 booking and catalog roles.
- **UI**: `/layanan` list by category; `/layanan/[id]` tabs Umum, Durasi & Sumber Daya, Staf & Delegasi, Resep BHP, Persetujuan Tindakan, Aftercare, Booking Online, Pajak (Harga Cabang → T-035, Situs → public-site task, shown as not yet available); edit form with Simpan / Minta Review naming which review(s) apply.

## Plan review round 2 (Argon): REVISE PLAN → adopted

1. **Pharmacist read scope:** RLS is row-level, so admitting a pharmacist to unpublished rows would expose unapproved clinical text on a dual-review version. The `catalog_versions` and `service_staff_eligibility` policies keep unpublished rows to owner, admin_pusat, manager, facility_head; the pharmacist sees an unpublished recipe **only** through the definer `catalog_review_target` (recipe proposed vs active, item identity, no clinical text). `/katalog/[id]` and `/layanan/[id]` never render pending clinical fields to a pharmacist (they read nothing unpublished for that role).
2. **Sync order:** in `catalog_review_sync` the version is updated (state, decided_at) and audited **before** the still-pending sibling approval is cancelled, so the recursive trigger sees a decided version and exits. The sibling is cancelled with decided_by/decided_as/decided_at = clock_timestamp(), audited `approval_cancelled`, and an `ApprovalDecided` event. Withdraw passes **one** pending approval of the version (`approval_id` if pending, else `materials_approval_id`) to `approval_cancel`; the trigger cancels the other. A materials-only change starts `pending_review`, audits `catalog_review_requested`, and emits `CatalogVersionPublished` only on activation.
3. **Routing:** `approvals_read`, the tenant-clinical helper and `approval_decide`'s not-found guard admit pharmacist and facility_head tenant-wide on `catalog_materials_review` regardless of the request's branch. `approval_decide` and the inbox gate **both** facility_head and owner on that type by `approval_ph_available` (clock_timestamp, after `tenant_actor_lock`). `INBOX_READERS` gains pharmacist; Persetujuan loads the review target for both catalog types.
4. **Contract:** child tables get `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)`, tenant composite FKs ON DELETE RESTRICT, immutability (ALWAYS) and truncate triggers, scope_map, support class and one operator policy each; `delegation_floor` is platform (scope_map platform, RLS, no grants, not in support_object_kinds). New definers (`catalog_service_requirements`, `approval_ph_available`) join the allow-list where granted to the app. `catalog_commercial()` and `catalog_review_target(uuid)` change their result shape: DROP + CREATE + REVOKE PUBLIC + GRANT clinic_app, declared in the drift test.
