## Design (proposed)

**Data** (migration 0068):
- `staff_credentials`: tenant, staff (composite FK), `kind` (`str`, `sip`, `sipp`, `sipa`), `number` (3–60), `issuer` (3–120), `profession` (code list: `dokter`, `dokter_gigi`, `perawat`, `bidan`, `apoteker`, `tenaga_kesehatan_lain`), `scope` (optional, ≤ 200), `branch_id` (null for STR, required for SIP/SIPP/SIPA: a practice permission is for one facility), `valid_from` (date), `expires_on` (date; null only for STR = lifetime; required for SIP/SIPP/SIPA), `evidence_note` (≤ 300: where the document is kept; uploads arrive with the document store), `state` (`pending`, `verified`, `rejected`, `suspended`, `revoked`), `recorded_by/at`, `decided_by/at`, `decision_reason` (3–500 for reject/suspend/revoke), `replaces` (a renewal is a new row; the old row stays). Never deleted; fields never edited after recording (a correction is a new row that replaces the old one).
- `credential_alerts`: tenant, credential, `threshold` (60, 30, 7), `alerted_on` (date), unique (credential, threshold). Ids and dates only; readable by no app role (support class `never`, like `patient_search_log`).
- RLS on `staff_credentials`: Owner, Admin Pusat (tenant-wide), Manager and Facility head (credentials of staff assigned to a branch in their set; SIP rows of their branches), the holder (own rows). Support class `personal`, operator policy (TEN-19).

**Writers** (definer, `tenant_actor_lock`, audited, ids-only events):
- `credential_record(staff, fields)`: Owner, Admin Pusat. The holder must have an active membership and a clinical role (doctor, nurse, therapist, pharmacist, facility head); a SIP branch must be one where the holder has that role. Starts `pending`. Audit `staff_credential` / `credential_recorded`; event `CredentialChanged` (credential_id, staff_id, change).
- `credential_decide(id, verified | rejected, reason)`: the Facility head, for a SIP of one of their branches, or for an STR of a holder assigned to one of their branches. **Not the recorder, not the inviter of the holder (`staff_invitations.invited_by` of the accepted invitation), not the holder** (IAM-4). Refuses verifying an expired credential.
- `credential_suspend(id, reason)` / `credential_revoke(id, reason)`: Facility head (same scope) or Owner; takes effect immediately.
- Guard (`ENABLE ALWAYS`, `BEFORE UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE`): no delete; recorded fields immutable; state transitions only pending→verified|rejected, verified→suspended|revoked, suspended→verified|revoked.

**Readers:**
- `credential_valid(staff, branch, at timestamptz)` (definer, STABLE; false, never null): a `verified` STR not expired at `at`, and a `verified` SIP/SIPP/SIPA for that branch not expired at `at` (dates in the branch timezone). Used by T-054 (start gates) and T-102 (SATUSEHAT). Expired, pending, rejected, suspended, revoked → false: "expired or invalid credentials remain blocked".
- TS `listCredentials(staffId)`, `credentialStatus(staff[])` for the list segment (worst state per staff: none, pending, expiring ≤ 60 days, expired, verified).

**Expiry alerts (the daily job):**
- `credential_alert_scan(p_today date default null)` (definer; runs on an unbound worker connection, like `events_claim`, refused on a tenant- or staff-bound connection): for every `verified` (and `pending`?) credential with `expires_on`, days left in the credential's branch timezone (STR with an expiry: the holder's first branch? see open point 3); for the highest threshold in {60, 30, 7} with days_left ≤ threshold and no `credential_alerts` row, insert the alert row and two notifications: one to the holder (`recipient_staff_id`) and one to the role `facility_head` at the credential's branch. A missed day catches up with only the most urgent threshold not sent (no burst of three). Lifetime STR (no expiry) never alerts. A new notification type `credential_expiry` (system-only, recipients holder + facility_head, subject `staff_credential`).
- TS `runCredentialAlerts(pool)`. The scheduler that calls it daily is deferred to the operations job runner (like T-046's lateness release); the test calls it with a fixed `p_today`.

**UI** (`/staf`, `/staf/[id]`):
- Kredensial segment: real status per clinical staff (Belum ada, Menunggu verifikasi, Terverifikasi, Habis dalam N hari, Kedaluwarsa, Ditangguhkan), filter chips, filtered-empty; manager sees own branches.
- Staff detail: a "Kredensial" section listing STR and each SIP (branch, number, issuer, validity, state); Owner/Admin Pusat: Tambah Kredensial (sheet); Facility head: Verifikasi / Tolak (reason), Tangguhkan / Cabut (reason, confirm); blocked copy "Kredensial belum diverifikasi" on clinical staff without a valid set.

## Plan review round 1 (Argon `0207b418`): REVISE PLAN → adopted

Open points:
1. **Accepted:** PR-73 is met at the notification data layer (system-only `credential_expiry` rows for the holder and the `facility_head` role, read through `notification_visible` / `notifications.list`); the expiry badges render on `/staf?segmen=kredensial` and `/staf/[id]`; Ringkasan Klinis › Perlu Ditangani (UX-60) and `/saya/notifikasi` are the open half for their tasks.
2. **Accepted:** `credential_alert_scan(p_today date default null)` in SQL and `runCredentialAlerts(pool, today?)` in TS, tested with fixed dates and with null; the daily loop goes to the operations job runner (STATUS note, as T-046).
3. **Accepted:** an STR's timezone and its facility-head notification branch come from the holder's clinical branches (each distinct branch where the holder holds a clinical role gets a facility-head row; never a null-branch role row); fallback to the tenant's earliest branch for the timezone only. `credential_valid` evaluates both STR and SIP dates in the target branch's timezone.
4. **Accepted:** `pending` and `verified` credentials with an expiry alert, while the holder's membership is active, the tenant is onboarding / live / suspended, the credential is not expired and not superseded by a non-rejected replacement.
5. **Accepted:** Owner and Admin Pusat record, including their own credentials (an Owner who practises); the verifier is never the holder, the recorder or the holder's inviter.
6. **Accepted:** fixed profession list with `(kind, profession)` consistency (`sipp` → `perawat`; `sipa` → `apoteker`; `sip` → `dokter`, `dokter_gigi`, `bidan`, `tenaga_kesehatan_lain`; `str` any) and a clinical role held by the holder (at the branch for a practice permit).

Findings adopted:
- **P1-1 scan:** `days_left = expires_on − today` (branch time), only 0–60; the threshold is the smallest reached (7, else 30, else 60); it fires only when no alert exists for that threshold or a smaller one; `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING` decides whether notifications are inserted (concurrent runs alert once). A late first run sends only the most urgent alert.
- **P1-2 separation of duties:** the facility head of the credential's branch decides (STR: a branch where the holder holds a clinical role); the Owner decides only when no active facility head there is distinct from the holder, the recorder and the inviter (the sole-PJ case, the DEC-031 D2 / DEC-046 pattern). Every decider is checked against holder, recorder and inviter (`ERR_APPROVER_IS_REQUESTER`), plus a table check `decided_by <> staff_id and decided_by <> recorded_by`. Writers take `tenant_actor_lock()` first and check the live `staff_branches` scope; out of scope = not found. Recording and verifying need the holder's active membership and clinical role on the wall clock; an expired credential cannot be verified (`ERR_CREDENTIAL_INVALID`).
- **P1-3 contract and lifecycle:** both tables follow 05 §3.1 (`id` v7, `tenant_id`, `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)`, composite RESTRICT FKs). `replaces` must name a row of the same staff, kind and branch, never itself; one non-rejected replacement per row (partial unique index); a second un-replaced pending/verified credential for the same staff, kind and branch is refused. Readers and the scan ignore a credential superseded by a verified replacement. Guards `ENABLE ALWAYS` with `BEFORE TRUNCATE` on both tables: recorded fields immutable; transitions pending→verified|rejected (decide), verified→suspended|revoked, suspended→revoked; **no `suspended → verified`** (reinstatement is a new recorded credential that goes through verification). `credential_alerts` append-only.
- **P1-4 `credential_valid`:** false (never null) without a bound tenant, for nulls or a branch of another tenant; dates in the branch timezone; needs the holder's active membership, a clinical role at the branch, a verified non-superseded STR and a verified non-superseded practice permit for the branch, both within `valid_from`…`expires_on`, with the same profession. TS `assertCredentialValid(db, staffId, branchId, at?)` throws `ERR_CREDENTIAL_INVALID` (18:214) for T-054 and T-102.
- **P2-1 RLS:** managers and facility heads read a permit only for an allowed branch, and an STR only when the holder holds a clinical role at an allowed branch; `/staf` Kredensial and `/staf/[id]` list only staff with a role at the viewer's branches for branch-scoped viewers.
- **P2-2 notifications:** the type `credential_expiry` is seeded by the migration (system-only); the scan inserts rows directly (no bound tenant, as 0066); facility-head rows carry the permit's branch, or each clinical branch of an STR holder; the holder row is addressed to the staff.
- **P2-3 scope expansions** (recorded before building): `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`, `tests/isolation/cases/staff.cases.ts`, `tests/context/stale-context.test.ts`, `src/modules/org/staff/page-data.ts` (if the staff loaders change), plus `tests/vendor/support.test.ts` / operator files if the TEN-19 gate requires them.
- **P2-4:** capabilities: record and decide need `configuration` (onboarding and live); suspend and revoke need `configuration` or `existing_appointment` (also while suspended). TS writers run under `assertProductionGoLive` and `runCommand` (idempotency keys). Audit `settings_change` (recorded, suspended, revoked) and `approval` (verified, rejected), reason null (the reason stays on the row). Event `CredentialChanged` with `jsonb_strip_nulls` of ids and lower-case codes.
- **P3-1:** status filter chips with filtered-empty and reset; the blocked notice "Kredensial belum diverifikasi"; a facility head who cannot decide sees the reason in words instead of the buttons.
- **P3-2:** no separate drift file (no pre-0068 function is re-created); the function-ACL and trigger checks live in `tests/org/credentials.test.ts`.

Noted for T-054 (not decided here): which clinical roles need a valid credential to start a treatment (PR-11). A beauty therapist may hold no STR/SIP; `credential_valid` stays generic and T-054 decides the role mapping.

## Plan review round 2 (Argon `75480ea9`): APPROVE PLAN

Build notes adopted:
- (a) `decided_by/at` record only the verification decision (pending → verified | rejected) and never change afterwards; suspension and revocation are recorded in their own columns `ended_by`, `ended_at`, `end_reason` (3–500), so the table check on `decided_by` never blocks an Owner suspending a credential they recorded.
- (b) `replaces` may name a prior credential of the same staff, kind and branch in any state; it is required only when an un-replaced pending or verified row exists for that staff, kind and branch (a reinstatement after suspension or revocation may link the old row or start fresh).
