## What is built (migration `0126_dashboards`; DEC-121)

### Read models (on read; no table, no aggregate, no refresh job)
- `dashboard_actor(roles, branch)` (internal): actor lock, the KPI's role list, a Level 3 role bound to its active branch (another branch is
  not found), a tenant-wide role may name any branch of its tenant or none (consolidated).
- `dashboard_kpi(kpi, from, to, branch, detail)` (granted): K1–K18 as fact rows — branch, local day, part, amount, source kind and id — and
  their sums: `total` (the money parts; K17 = owed), `by_dim`, `by_branch`, `by_grp1` / `by_grp2`, `extra`, and the drill-down `rows`.
  Roles per KPI follow `07` Dashboards (owner, Admin Pusat, manager for its branch, finance, auditor; central CS and marketing for K13 and
  K14; purchasing and pharmacist for K18; K5 keeps the sub-ledger readers: owner, finance, auditor).
- K19 is the existing `target_mine` / `target_list` (0103).
- `dashboard_home(branch)` (granted): the UX-60 role homes from existing facts — Klinis (open clinical complaints, pending record
  corrections, STR/SIP expiring within 60 days, SATUSEHAT rejected, open incidents), Marketing (leads per stage, promo uses and discount this
  month, dormant patients by SET-22, surveys), Apotek (to prepare / hand over, near expiry by SET-27, opened vials lapsed / due in 24 h,
  receipts today, quarantined / recalled batches), Pembelian (below minimum per branch, open POs, supplier invoices due within 7 days), Audit
  (manual discounts, voids, cash-shift variance, stock-count variance, write-offs; no score, no table — Panel 2).

### The five open KPI definitions (Panel 2, PANEL2-DECISIONS-SLICE4.md)
| KPI | Decision | Reader |
|---|---|---|
| K4 Piutang | (b) an unpaid line of a closed visit ages from `invoices.issued_at`; a receivable from `created_at`; r3: every subtraction is a dated fact up to the period end, an undated cancellation makes K4 incomplete | open receivables net of collections and write-offs at the period end + unpaid closed-visit lines (excluding package lines), buckets 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / > 90 |
| K12 Tidak datang / batal < 24 jam | (b) the denominator excludes cancels ≥ 24 h before the start and reschedules | no-show + late cancel ÷ the appointments that stayed on the book; fixed 24 h window (not SET-10) |
| K14 Respons lead | (a) opening-hours leads (`branch_is_open`) for both the median and the share | median minutes (display, one decimal), within 15 min (Appendix A literal) and within SET-20 in effect at each lead's entry (tenant SLA; `sla_mix` per threshold), compared losslessly (r4: microseconds internally; public amounts in seconds) |
| K15 Utilisasi performer | (b) interval union per performer-day | booked (booked / confirmed / arrived) ∪ counted executions, clipped to the local day ÷ rostered work − breaks of the branch's performers — `booking_eligible` on a service version in effect on the shift date of an item the branch offers (idle included; r3/r4), leave days out; detail rows carry the performer and non-additive source refs |
| K17 Komisi terutang | (a) accrued + adjustments − paid net | r3: dated movements — entitlements (`accrued_at`), adjustments (`adjusted_at`), the net actually paid (`payout_batch_lines.net_payable`, negative, `paid_at`) = owed at the period end (the commission_payable roll-forward); the batches' current workflow state apart in `extra.current_workflow` (gross rupiah as of now, informational); earned in the period in `extra` |

### UX-60 pages (`/ringkasan/peran`)

`dashboard_home` (`role-home-view.tsx`; links only to pages on disk); nurse and therapist get Ruang Saya (today's room visits grouped by room
from `treatment_room_list`, the next booked/confirmed patient from `appointments_window`, own commission this month from `target_mine`);

## r3 (independent review `3ba1bf52`; Panel 2 `PANEL2-R2-REVIEW-DECISION.md`; Sol decisions 2026-11-20)
Each finding was confirmed against the freeze-r2 source and reproduced by a new test that fails on r2 (`exec/r3-on-r2-*.log` in the lane
notes: 7 failures), then fixed:
- **F1 Klinis credentials:** a branch permit (SIP/SIPP/SIPA) only of this branch; a branch-independent STR only when its holder holds a
  clinical role at this branch (0068 `credential_readable`'s rule). Test: a two-branch doctor, a B-only nurse, and a nurse clinical at B but a
  cashier at A — each branch's home lists exactly its own (positive control at B).
- **F2 K4 historical invariance:** allocations (`created_at`), applied deposits (`decided_at`), conversions (the receivable's `created_at`)
  and cancellations only when dated up to the period end. `cancelled_amount` has two writers (0121): `interrupted_to_balance` (addition
  branch) — dated by its `balance_entries` `from_interrupted_treatment` row, source = the execution of the line's plan item on the visit —
  and `package_close_do` (package or substitution difference lines, never a closed-visit line in K4). A cancellation no dated fact explains
  is never valued: K4 returns no total, no aging and no rows, with `extra = {undated_cancellations: n, complete: false}`. Tests: a payment, a
  deposit, a conversion and a cancellation dated after the period leave it unchanged and count in the next period; the real writer on a
  half-paid addition dates its cancellation (complete, the line owes 0); a synthetic undated cancellation → incomplete.
- **F3 K17:** owed from dated movements (above). `by_dim` = `{accrued, adjusted, paid_net}`, summing to `total`; paid_net added once and
  never as gross. Tests: a negative adjustment carried forward (September batch nets 0, carries 50,000) and an October batch paid after the
  period → October owed 150,000, November owed 0 (no double subtraction); the paid_net row dated `paid_at` with the batch as source.
- **F4 / F6 K15:** roster = the branch's eligible performers (a role named in a service's eligibility, performs / supervised, or a named
  clinician — `booking_eligible`'s rule), idle ones included; a cashier's shift excluded. Detail rows add `grp1` (performer) and `refs`
  (appointments, executions, shifts) as non-additive metadata; minutes stay once per performer-day.
- **F7 K14:** elapsed time in exact seconds; on time = `<= 900` s and `<= SET-20 × 60` s. Test: 15:00 / 15:01 and 30:00 / 30:01.
- **F5 Marketing home:** `window` (month to date), `k13` (stages and by source) and `k14` (response extra) from `dashboard_kpi` itself; the
  page renders "Sumber lead (K13)" and "Respons lead (K14)" with the window and written empty states. Browser flow 16 asserts both.
