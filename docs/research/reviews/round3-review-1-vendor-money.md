# R3-A review: Clinic Dashboard kit v0.4 (business model, vendor platform and money)

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES, with 3 P1s that must close before PRD approval.** Nothing is wrong in structure. The P1s are gaps and contradictions that need wording fixes. Vendor money and clinic money are kept apart properly. The AutoLaris adapter mostly follows the skill rules. Leftover subscription and "Midtrans first" wording is gone from the requirements. What's weak:
- the server-fee lifecycle after go-live (suspension, exit, waiver, credit, VAT, an expired AutoLaris payment) is only partly written;
- in a live tenant a mock payment can satisfy the payment gates;
- discount limits are not tied into the settings list, the approver rules or promos.

Line numbers come from my reads during this session. `05-DATA-MODEL.md` returned different line numbers in two greps (106–112, then 154–160), so the file may have been edited during the review. Rely on the IDs.

## Checked against older decisions

- **H-09** (clinic is merchant of record) is held consistently in BILL-17, TD-40, SEC-15 and ARCH-13.
- **B-01..B-21:** the only conflicts are B-09/B-16, the bounded settings list (R3A-8), and BILL-1 vs mock allocations (R3A-1).
- **A-***: A-06/A-12 VAT vs discounts needs the R3A-9 wording. A-02 accountant sign-off is needed for the R3A-12 rows.

## Already good

- Two separate money domains: ARCH-13 says AutoLaris is vendor-only and never takes patient payments, and CTX-6 agrees. TEN-16 puts the vendor tables in the `platform` policy kind, and the AutoLaris key is kept in the vendor secret store, never in a tenant vault (TD-42, 15 l.50).
- TD-42 follows most of the skill rules: server-side key, `list_payment` at runtime, `reff_id` saved before the create call, Advice by `transaction_id`, `rc === "00"`, no shipping endpoints, `create_payment` until the `courir_id: 1` profile is approved (Q-13), and "pending" never counts as paid.
- Fee changes need two people (vendor finance proposes, platform owner approves; PR-57, IAM-10, UX-43, scenario PR-57). Fee versions are tracked by effective month, and issued invoices never change.
- Billing starts at go-live, after the checklist gate (PR-59 and its scenario: no invoice while an item blocks). Suspension is manual only, never automatic, and the clinic keeps reading and exporting records (PR-56).
- The payment port is provider-neutral with normalized states (TD-41). Webhooks are signed, idempotent and resolved to a tenant through `payment_routes`. The mock flags everything as simulated and shows the SIMULASI banner (UI-18). Mock money stays out of K1, sub-ledgers and commission (BILL-29), and every future adapter must pass one shared contract suite.
- Q-09 is closed correctly. BR-4 sends WhatsApp message costs to Meta and the clinic directly. Tender value is kept as a reference only.
- Discounts reduce the commission basis and K3 the same way as BILL-2 allocation. Every manual discount appears in the suspicious-activity report (PR-72, SEC-19, UX-53).
- PLAN puts vendor billing in M7, after the vendor console core in M0, and the Numa Klinik onboarding check in M9. The order makes sense.
