**P1-1 `target_closing_value(p_tenant, p_staff, p_from, p_to, p_branch DEFAULT NULL) RETURNS bigint`** — over lines of invoices with
`state = 'issued' AND NOT counter AND NOT consultation`, `(p_branch IS NULL OR sold_branch_id = p_branch)`, `'closing_bc' =
ANY(commission_item_scopes(...))`, an **active** attribution naming the BC as closing, no write-off on the line; **period inflows** (net of VAT):
allocations (`created_at` in the window, not simulated), applied deposits (`decided_at` in the window), the receivable share collected in the
window (the `kasir_allocate` share of collections up to `p_to` minus up to `p_from`, scaled by `vat_base/line_total`); **period reductions**
(net of VAT): paid refunds (`paid_at` in the window, `amount − vat_reversed`), interrupted-treatment moves (the `from_interrupted_treatment`
balance entry in the window for an execution of the line), package cheaper substitutions (`decided_at` in the window, the base drop) and package
closes (`closed_at` in the window, the net basis that left: paid − redeemed − VAT reversed); clamp `greatest(0, …)::bigint`.

**P1-2 `target_plans_selected(p_tenant, p_staff, p_from, p_to) RETURNS integer`** — care plans `active|completed` with ≥ 1 non-removed plan item,
every non-removed item covered by a `selection_items` row with `quantity >= qty`; the completing selection `s_last` = the latest `(confirmed_at,
id)` among those items' selections, `s_last.confirmed_at` in the window; credited to `coalesce(s_last.closing_bc_staff_id, the visit's active
non-consultation sale attribution's closing BC, s_last.guiding_bc_staff_id, the plan's guiding assignment at s_last.confirmed_at) = p_staff`.

**P2-1 scorecard.** `target_mine` and `target_list` rows carry a scorecard over the window: `completed_services` (finished, counted executions
by the performer), `commission_gross` / `commission_adjustments` / `commission_net` (entitlements `accrued_at`, adjustments `adjusted_at`),
`rostered_minutes` (staff_shifts of kind work in the window, less dates under a non-cancelled staff_leave), `executed_minutes` (finished
executions' duration) and `utilization_bps` (K15; NULL when nothing rostered).

**P2-2 table.** `staff_targets(id, tenant_id, staff_id, period_kind bulanan|triwulan, period_key, closing_value_target, plans_target NULL,
state active|superseded, superseded_at, set_by, set_as='owner', set_at, command_key)`: UNIQUE (tenant_id, id) and (tenant_id, command_key);
partial unique (tenant_id, staff_id, period_key) WHERE active; composite FKs ON DELETE RESTRICT; checks (key pattern, 0 ≤ target ≤ 10^12,
plans 0–100000, set_as owner, state/superseded_at, period_kind ↔ period_key pattern). Guard (staff_access first; no delete; insert active;
update only active→superseded with superseded_at), no-truncate, RLS `staff_targets_readable` (owner; manager for a staff assigned to an allowed
branch; own row in an own-target role; never Admin Pusat), operator read, column grants, scope_map (tenant-ledger, commission), support class
`personal`.

**P2-3 scope expansions:** `tests/rls/rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/authority-clock.test.ts`, `tests/db/targets-drift.test.ts` (+ expected),
`tests/isolation/cases/targets.cases.ts`, `docs/tasks/T-088.md`, DEC-100, STATUS, BUILD-LOG, ARCHITECTURE.
